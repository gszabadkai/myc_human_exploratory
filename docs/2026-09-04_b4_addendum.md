## 1. TASK 1 - the CCLE configuration check

### 1.1 The question B4 never asked

B4 tested V1's **dependency** prediction. It **assumed**, and never checked,
that the tumour transcript configuration holds in the cells where the
dependency was measured. Synthesis 3.1: `BCL2L1` **+0.388** cross-cohort mean
with OXPHOS, `MCL1` **-0.266**. V1 reads a dependency off that configuration;
B4 measured the dependency; nobody measured the configuration in CCLE.

Measured now, in `scripts/E18_ccle_configuration.R`:

- **71 breast lines with expression** - the cohort B4 *scored*. B4 *fitted* on
  the 51 of them that also carry CRISPR; this check needs no CRISPR.
- **1,140 pan-cancer lines** - B4's pan-cancer scoring cohort, re-scored,
  because GSVA and every z-score are cohort-relative.
- **Partial Spearman on the CCLE `PROLIF` score**, Fisher-z intervals,
  Bonett-Wright variance - `E16`'s `.cor_block` verbatim, so the CCLE row and
  the tumour row are the same quantity computed the same way.
- **All 12 BCL2-family genes** `E16` carries, not two. Taking `BCL2L1` and
  `MCL1` alone after seeing them is the grid-of-cells trap.
- Endpoints are `log2(TPM+1)` **as supplied** - already log of linear.

**The reproduction control.** Before anything is read, the script proves these
rulers are *B4's* rulers and not a same-recipe rebuild: the CCLE coverage table
must come back identical to `x$coverage` (40 rows, both scopes), and B4's saved
`MCL1` / `ox_gsva` `OX` coefficient must be reproduced to `1e-10`. Both hold
(`-0.0481653` against the saved `-0.0482`). No coefficient from that control is
reported as a result anywhere.

### 1.5 The rule, and which reading it gives

Fixed in `E18` section 0b **before any number was computed**, and printed by
the script:

> On one ruler, the configuration reproduces iff `rho(BCL2L1) > 0` **and**
> `rho(MCL1) < 0` **and** `gap > 0`. Cohort verdict: 4/4 or 3/4 = REPRODUCES,
> 2/4 = SPLIT (not resolved by a tie-break invented afterwards), <= 1/4 = DOES
> NOT REPRODUCE.

**Breast: 0 of 4. Pan-cancer: 0 of 4.** Every ruler fails on the gap; three of
four also fail on `BCL2L1` itself. There is no split to adjudicate.

> **READING B - the configuration does not reproduce, so B4 never tested V1.**
> The dependency was measured against an axis that orders these genes
> differently in culture than in tumours. B4's reversal therefore says nothing
> about the tumour model.

**This is not a rescue and must not be written as one.** What it removes is
B4's standing as a test of V1 - not V1's exposure to one. V1 is untested, not
supported. The prediction still has no functional evidence for it in any
system, and the arrow it sits on (synthesis 5.2 arrow (f)) is back to having
none rather than to having favourable evidence.

**And the cheapest explanation for the non-reproduction is already eliminated.**
Cell lines have no stroma, no infiltrate and no adipose, so the natural first
thought is that the tumour `BCL2L1`-OXPHOS association is a compartment
artefact. `E16` check 4 already tested that on the 1,007 TCGA samples carrying
purity and leukocyte fraction: `BCL2L1` on `ox_gsva` moves **+0.419 -> +0.406**,
`MCL1` **-0.235 -> -0.204**, `BBC3` **+0.363 -> +0.348**. **The tumour
configuration survives adjustment.** So the two systems genuinely disagree, and
section 7 item 2 is where the remaining candidate explanations are listed.

**Reading A did not occur and is recorded here only so the branch is visible.**
Had the configuration reproduced, transcript and dependency would have been
running in opposite directions in the same cells - script 14's two-sided
problem as a finding rather than a caveat, coherent, and inverting the
therapeutic reading toward MCL1 dependence. That is not what the numbers give.

### 1.6 A sensitivity, so the cohort definition cannot be blamed

B4's pan-cancer *fit* dropped lines in lineages of fewer than 10, leaving 1,127
of the 1,140 scored. Re-running the configuration on exactly those 1,127 - the
same z-scored vectors, no re-scoring - moves nothing: **maximum absolute change
0.0101 across all 48 gene-by-ruler cells.**

## 3. TASK 3 - B4-b was scored against the wrong comparator

**The B4-b verdict is NOT reversed.** It passed on the rule as written and that
stands, and `docs/2026-09-04_b4_declaration.md` is not amended. What follows is
a **limitation recorded**, not a new score.

B4-b's rule was that floor estimates be small *relative to the primary genes'*.
`vs_primary_max` implemented that by comparing each floor gene to the **largest**
primary estimate - which is `BCL2L1` at `+0.196`. So every ratio came out
comfortably below 1 (0.06 to 0.46) and the rule passed. Against `MCL1` it is a
different picture.

`[-0.045, -0.004]` and `ox_rel` `[-0.046, -0.006]` pan-cancer only. `RPL3`
excludes zero nowhere, in either scope, on any ruler.)

**What this establishes.**

1. **The `MCL1` leg sits at the floor.** `POLR2B` carries the **same sign as
   `MCL1` on all four rulers in both scopes**, exceeds it on two of four in
   breast, and runs at 55-69% of it pan-cancer. A pan-essential moving with the
   exposure the same way and about as far as the endpoint is what "at the
   floor" means. `MCL1`'s `OX` coefficient cannot be read as a dependency
   result, in either scope.
2. **The `BCL2L1` leg is not affected the same way, because the floor moves
   the OTHER way.** `POLR2B` is opposite in sign to `BCL2L1` on all four
   rulers in both scopes. A floor effect of the opposite sign cannot manufacture
   the primary's sign; where the ratio approaches 1 (`ox_ppd` 1.07, `ox_lvl`
   1.06 in breast) that says `BCL2L1`'s estimate is *small on those two rulers*,
   not that it is floor-driven. `BCL2L1` is clearly above the floor on
   `ox_gsva` and `ox_rel` and marginal on `ox_ppd` and `ox_lvl`.
3. So **`BCL2L1` is the one readable coefficient in B4** - reversed from
   prediction, above the floor on two rulers and marginal on the other two, and
   null at both n = 51 and n = 1,127 (task 2).

**Why the comparator was coarse rather than wrong.** `vs_primary_max` answers
"could the floor account for the largest thing we found?" That is a reasonable
question and it was answered honestly. It simply cannot see a case where one
primary is four times the other, which is what happened.

## 4. What B4 now establishes, and what it does not

**Establishes.** B4 fitted the `OX` main effect that Block G computed everywhere
and reported nowhere, in 51 breast and 1,127 pan-cancer lines, on four rulers,
with a declared direction and a pre-registered-in-spirit floor. The estimate
runs **opposite to V1's prediction on 8 of 8 breast cells and 8 of 8 pan-cancer
cells**, and that reversal is a real feature of the data. It also establishes,
through this addendum, three things about its own scope: the `MCL1` leg is at
the pan-essential floor; the `BCL2L1` leg is the only readable one and is null
at both sample sizes; and the transcript configuration the whole prediction
rests on **does not exist in these cells**.

**Does not establish.** It does not test V1, because of section 1. It does not
touch **M2**, which is a claim about transcripts in tumours and is measured in
tumours. It does not touch **N2**, which is pre-registered and outranks
everything here. And it does not license any statement that OXPHOS-high tumours
are more or less MCL1- or BCL-XL-dependent - the direction it found is a
cell-line dependency gradient with no transcript gradient behind it.

**Two limits that belong in this paragraph and are not to be argued past.**

- **B4-c already met the third declared falsifier, and that is independent of
  everything above.** `OXPHOS subunits` ranks **11th or 12th of 18** arms in the
  declared direction in **seven of the eight** gene-by-ruler cells, and 17th in
  the eighth (`MCL1` on `ox_ppd`). The three arms
  whose CIs exclude zero for `BCL2L1` are **`TCA cycle` +0.330
  `[+0.062, +0.598]`**, **`CII subunits` +0.288 `[+0.011, +0.565]`** and
  **`Fatty acid oxidation` +0.278 `[+0.018, +0.538]`**; the `OXPHOS umbrella` is
  +0.142 and **`mtDNA-encoded OXPHOS` is -0.040, opposite in sign to the nuclear
  set**. **Whatever this gradient is, it is not respiratory.** With no matched
  null in CCLE and n = 51, the TCA / anaplerotic cluster is an **observation,
  not a lead**, and it is recorded as one.
- **Single-agent CRISPR knockout of `BCL2L1` is a weak test of whether BCL-XL
  restrains a loaded trigger,** because `MCL1` routinely compensates for its
  loss - the standard finding behind combination BH3-mimetic dosing. **This is a
  limitation of the readout, not a defence of the prediction.** It says the
  experiment is less able to detect the predicted dependency than V1 assumed; it
  does not convert a reverse-signed estimate into support.

## 7. What would change this answer

1. **`E18` sourced, and any number moving.** The dry run and the author's run
   should agree object for object, as `E16` and `E17` did. If they do not, this
   note is wrong before it is anything else.
2. **A stated reason why CCLE `BCL2L1` should differ.** Cell lines have no
   stroma, no infiltrate and no adipose, and CLAUDE.md trap 2 says breast is the
   worst TCGA tissue for exactly that. So the obvious escape is that the tumour
   `BCL2L1`-OXPHOS association is carried by a non-tumour compartment, in which
   case CCLE is not failing to reproduce it - it is the purity-adjusted answer.

   **That escape is already closed, and it was closed before this addendum was
   written.** `E16` check 4 (`docs/2026-09-04_e16_respiratory_rulers.md`) refits
   all twelve genes on the 1,007 TCGA samples with purity and leukocyte
   fraction on top of `PROLIF_DISJOINT`, on all four rulers. `BCL2L1` on
   `ox_gsva` moves **+0.419 -> +0.406**, a shift of **-0.013**; the largest
   shift on any of the three genes the model names is `MCL1` on `ox_gsva` at
   **+0.031** (-0.235 -> -0.204), and `BBC3` moves by at most -0.014.

   **So the tumour association is not an infiltrate artefact, and the CCLE
   discrepancy is not explained by purity.** That makes the non-reproduction
   more interesting, not less: two systems disagree about `BCL2L1` and the
   cheapest explanation has been eliminated. What would still change the answer
   is a *different* compartment argument with its own measurement - not this
   one.
3. **A wider pan-essential floor panel** (handoff item 0). If several common
   essentials carry the same same-signed gradient as `POLR2B`, the `MCL1` leg is
   a general fitness effect and section 3's conclusion hardens. If `POLR2B` is
   alone, it softens.
4. **A combination readout.** The limitation in section 4 is testable: a
   `BCL2L1` dependency conditioned on `MCL1` status, or a co-dependency, is the
   readout the single-agent screen cannot give.
