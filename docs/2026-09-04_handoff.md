## 6. THINGS THAT WILL BITE

1. **No ortholog function anywhere, in either direction.** The reconciliation is
   a comparison of **conclusions**, not of gene lists. Tripwire:
   `grep -rnE "(mouse_to_human|human_to_mouse|ortholog[s]?)[[:space:]]*\(" scripts/`
   must stay empty.
2. **Never score SCAN-B without `scanb_pheno.rds$symbol_map`.** If a mouse model
   is projected onto SCAN-B by symbol, this breaks first and silently - coverage
   drops from 0.989 to 0.775 and Complex V loses its F1 head and c-ring.
3. **Never pool GSVA or mitoPPS values across cohorts - and a species is a
   cohort.** Compare correlations, patterns and rankings, never values.
4. **N2 outranks anything exploratory.** The sibling study is pre-registered,
   found the functional `MYC x OXPHOS` interaction on apoptotic priming null,
   and is frozen at `d3ac60e`. Nothing here overturns it and nothing may be
   written as if it did.
5. **Priming is not measurable in transcript abundance.** Write "carries a
   higher `BAD`/`MCL1` transcript ratio", never "is more primed". N3.
6. **A ratio result needs its `gain`.** In either species. 3.3, 3.3a, 3.3b.
6b. **A ruler result needs its estimator named.** In either species. Synthesis
   8.4 trap 9: `ox_rel` is the least MYC-entangled ruler on the MSigDB signature
   and the *most* on the CollecTRI regulon, in both cohorts. "The separable
   ruler" is not a property a ruler has on its own here.
6c. **`data/raw/depmap` is a SYMLINK into the closed repo, and this repo cannot
   regenerate what it points at.** `data/raw/depmap_README.md` says so and
   carries the checksums anyway. If `myc_human_validation`'s `data/raw/` is ever
   cleared, B4's input is gone from here too and the three omics files need a
   BROWSER to re-fetch - the portal is behind Cloudflare and figshare stops at
   24Q4. `E17` stops rather than skipping if the link breaks, because a broken
   symlink reads as an absent directory and script 14's idiom is to skip.
6d. **An endpoint that fails an n floor is not an endpoint that was null.**
   `E17` records every skipped fit in `$skipped` with its n and reason. The live
   case: navitoclax and venetoclax sit in a different PRISM screen from the MCL1
   compounds and cover 21-22 of the 28 lines against a floor of 25, so the only
   interpretable BCL-XL read was never fitted. Read `$skipped` before believing
   an endpoint is absent because it was null.

6e. **A FUNCTIONAL RESULT IN A SYSTEM THAT DOES NOT CARRY THE CONFIGURATION IS
   NOT A TEST OF IT.** Synthesis 8.4 trap 10, added 2026-09-04 from `E18`. B4
   measured guardian dependency against OXPHOS in DepMap, got the clean reverse
   of the prediction, and only afterwards was CCLE asked whether it carries the
   3.1 configuration at all. It does not - `BCL2L1` runs `+0.35` to `+0.43` with
   OXPHOS in both tumour cohorts and `-0.11` to `+0.01` in cell lines, and the
   `BCL2L1 - BBC3` gap flips sign in 8 of 8 cells. **Before any mouse functional
   readout is read as agreeing or disagreeing, measure the mouse's own
   3.1-equivalent.** This is NOT the same as 6-8 / trap 8 ("different
   measurement types are not a disagreement"); they stack.

6f. **A floor comparison must be per endpoint, not against the largest one.**
   B4-b passed because `vs_primary_max` compared each floor gene to `BCL2L1` at
   `+0.196`. Against `MCL1` at `-0.048`, `POLR2B` is 1.69x. The verdict stands
   as declared; the reading does not. Addendum section 3.
