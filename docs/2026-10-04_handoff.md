## 2. FOR THE FIGURE PANELS - what a panel may and may not assert

Collected here because it is currently spread across three result notes, and a
figure is where a qualification gets dropped.

### 2.1 The one quantity that may carry a panel

**The pooled pCR `m1` coefficient**: -0.1616 [-0.281, -0.042], sign negative in
all three cohorts, I2 = 0.0%. It is the only quantity in the declared line that
meets section 9's reading rule.

**A panel showing it must also show `m0`**, which fails the rule in both
directions (signs `+ - +`, pooled CI including zero, I2 48.8). The licensed
clause's second qualification is that the unadjusted coefficient does NOT
support it and is reported beside it - so a forest plot of `m1` alone, without
`m0`, breaks the licence.

**Axis and units.** Coefficients are per **within-cohort SD**, so a panel may
place three cohorts on one axis in those units but **may not** invite
comparison with any TCGA or SCAN-B value (4.1, 4.2): GSE25066 covers 57 of 89
OXPHOS subunits on GPL96 and METABRIC 69 of 89.

### 2.2 What may NOT appear in any panel

- **No distant-outcome panel asserting an association.** Nothing meets the rule
  in any pre-specified set, in either cohort family.
- **The `s508` DRFS cells** (HR 0.776 / 0.774 / 0.802). **That set has no
  subtype term.** A panel containing them implies a result the analysis does
  not have.
- **METABRIC's ER-positive `m1`** as a result (HR 0.8962, excluding zero by
  3.7e-04, sensitivity p 0.186, m2 and m3 including zero).
- **Any panel implying METABRIC replicates the pCR finding.** Different
  estimand, different spine, so the two `m0` are not the same model (10.5,
  10.1a).
- **Anything from `X01`**: no sentence, figure, legend or supplementary table,
  without its own declaration first.
- **Any subtype as a finding.** `E42` 13.2, and `X01` does not relax it.
- **Any two-instrument claim.** mitoPPS exists in none of the four outcome
  cohorts; a panel must not place a GSVA-only result where the arm's
  two-instrument convention would be read into it.

### 2.3 Two qualifications a figure legend will want to drop, and must not

1. **The subtype scope is checked but INCOMPLETE.** `E42` found no pair of
   subtype summaries with opposite signs or non-overlapping CIs, so the clause
   stands unqualified - **but `HRneg_HER2pos` was never fitted** (minority class
   33 against 7 parameters). **The clause's generality over HER2-positive
   disease rests on nothing measured**, and a panel spanning subtypes must show
   that stratum as absent rather than omit it silently.
2. **TNBC is heterogeneous, not null.** Its pooled interval includes zero at
   I2 62.1% with signs disagreeing across three cohorts. A panel that renders
   it as a null point estimate asserts something the data do not say; section 8's
   "uninformative, not absence" wording applies.

### 2.4 One number that is a result and is easy to omit

**The forkscale statement.** `rho(OX, MB1_forkscale) = 0.416` in METABRIC
against F3-pre's 0.529 (GSVA) and 0.418 (mitoPPS) - three readings, two
instruments, two cohorts, all inside the INTERMEDIATE band, and this one in the
cohort the forks were derived from. **INTERMEDIATE is a property, not an
instrument artefact.** `F3` was specified in `myc_human_validation`; **these
rungs are NOT F3** and any legend mentioning them must say so.

## 6. What this session added to the repo's stock of traps

All three are in `CLAUDE.md` and will bite someone who does not know them.

1. **An alias is not automatically safe, and the guard belongs on the ROUTE,
   not the symbol.** `COX7A2L -> SCAF1` and `POLR1B -> RPA2` each point at a
   different gene's approved symbol (58506, 6118); both rejected, which is why
   OXPHOS subunits recover 69 of 89. **But a blanket ban is also wrong**:
   `RPA2` is a legitimate direct member of `PROLIF_DISJOINT`. The `E41` script
   found this by stopping on it.
2. **`BBC3` is absent from METABRIC**, so any future METABRIC analysis touching
   the 6-gene signed configuration is blocked on it; a five-gene stand-in is
   forbidden.
3. **A numbered script saves its analysis frame** - R rule 4 and
   `docs/R_CODING_INSTRUCTIONS.md` section 3. `E41` saved 26 tables and no
   frame, so `X01` could not stratify at all until `51465b6`, and separately
   could not weigh confounding by indication because the indication covariates
   were never carried across. **Carry the covariates a downstream question
   might plausibly need, not only the ones the declaring script modelled.**
