## The standing result, unchanged

**MYC-high human breast tumours order the mitochondrial compartment the way the
MYC-driven mouse gland does, MYC-low tumours the way the older wild-type gland
does, and the developmental axis that motivated the comparison is the one axis
human tumours do not match.**

`E26` showed that is not composition: the positive control kept 96% of its MYC
difference while the adjusters explained 8.8% of its variance, and the
copy-number split widened under adjustment.

**`E27`, `E28` and `E29` were three attempts to extend or sharpen this. None
succeeded, and none damaged it, because neither `E25` nor `E26` is built on any
of them.**

## What the three follow-ons actually established

**`E27` - the level-1 partition.** The internal null holds **on the rule as
written** and not on a stricter statistic: central dogma's interval lies inside
the equivalence bound, but the paired difference of magnitudes covers zero. The
reallocation is not clean - three of six non-OXPHOS categories fall alongside
OXPHOS. **Its durable finding is that assembly factors barely move anywhere**
(mean absolute deviation 0.0095 human, 0.0208 mouse, against subunits at 0.0503
and 0.0657), so the respiratory chain and the machinery that builds it are
prioritised differently.

**`E28` - the gap readout. IT FAILED and must not be used.** Its own protection
claim failed, the mouse contrast is not resolvable at n = 6, and **the sign of
the human gap difference depends on which MYC estimator defines the groups, with
the copy-number split disagreeing with the primary.** Section 8 of its note
lists the six things it cannot carry. The first is that **no human gap number
from that script belongs in a manuscript.**

**`E30` - a second comparator for the gap. IT FAILED THE SAME WAY, and that
equality is what it contributes.** Two comparators with the same numerator give
2 of 4 estimators each and the copy-number instrument disagrees with both, which
turns `E28`'s inference into a demonstration: **the failure is a property of the
rank-gap statistic, not of the comparator.** Its one gain is on the mouse side,
where both developmental windows clear zero at -38.0 where `E28`'s covered it.
Its PART B NADH panel is description only and names nothing.

**`E29` - the map. Explicitly not a readout.** Position matches between groups
are coincidental: **not one of 28 pairs exceeds its rho-matched null.** The
off-diagonal pathways are scattered, 81 distinct names across 12 pairs. No
opposing programme is established, and the coherence claim its own rule would
have allowed was declined.

## Four facts worth carrying that are not in the conclusion

1. **mitoPPS is NOT a closed composition.** The per-sample sum ranges 135.8 to
   184.6 in TCGA, and pairwise rho between the 142 scores sits on zero. **The
   standard compositional objection does not apply**, and no earlier note says
   so. `CLAUDE.md` records the fact in its phase summary; the trap list is
   deliberately untouched because R5's condition was not met.
2. **The percentile transform IS constrained.** Percentiles sum to exactly 7100
   per group, so any change vector sums to zero and the neutral point is
   `-|delta|/141`. Negligible at P = 142 and **-2.7 on a 20-pathway panel**, so
   it matters if the panel ever shrinks.
3. **The OXPHOS branch splits on gene sharing with the reference.** Every
   complex-subunit set is 100% contained in `OXPHOS subunits`, so its
   co-movement is arithmetic. The seven gene-disjoint sets are the informative
   ones, and four oppose the reference in both species.
4. **Sign agreement across species is carried by pathways that move.** 84 of 101
   with substantial means agree; 19 of 40 near-zero ones agree, which is chance.
