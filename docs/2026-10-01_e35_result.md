`Q2 - Q1` shows no such trend (`Aneuploidy.Score`: +1.073, +0.651, +1.144).

**Every CI overlaps its neighbours, so this changes nothing about the reading**,
and it is in the direction *opposite* to GRADED — more burden, if anything,
slightly less coupling where MYC is high. It is recorded because reporting only
the monotone-increase direction the rule was looking for would be the failure
mode this design exists to prevent. **It is not promoted, and it is not a
finding.**

## 7. What this licenses, and what it does not

**Burden is a proxy for drive and not a measure of it.** Aneuploidy, fraction
genome altered and genome doublings index chromosomal instability, which is as
much a consequence of drive as a measure of it.

**Configuration is not lethality.** The endpoint is a transcript relationship.
There is no death readout in TCGA, and the sibling study's functional BH3 assays
already returned null against a transcript-level expectation, so a transcript
coupling and a functional liability can come apart.

**The cohort is single and unreplicated.** TCGA only. SCAN-B carries no CNV, so
no replication cohort exists for this question.

**Nothing licenses a treatment-selection claim.** No cohort here carries an
OXPHOS-directed intervention or a death readout, and nothing here licenses any
statement about killing.

### Licensed — one clause

> In TCGA, the coupling between respiratory state and the apoptotic
> configuration is present at every level of genomic burden the cohort spans,
> on three burden proxies, both instruments and both MYC strata, and it is not
> explained by proliferation.

**This reinstates no stratifier.** It is consistent with every established
tumour lying above whatever condition the mouse manipulates, which is the
reconciliation the declaration set out to test. **It does not demonstrate that
condition, and it cannot**: a cohort of tumours has no sample below the
threshold to contrast against.

### Not licensed

- **Any claim about killing or lethality**, in any form.
- **Any claim that burden is irrelevant to the biology.** SATURATED means the
  coupling does not *vary* across the burden range this cohort spans. The
  cohort has no low-drive arm.
- **GRADED in either direction.** The `Q4 - Q3` decline (section 4) is not a
  finding; its CIs overlap throughout.
- **Anything from `genome_doublings` level 2**, or from `m2` as declared.
- **Any causal reading**, and anything prognostic. No outcome variable entered.

## 8. What would change this answer

1. **A cohort with a low-drive arm.** The declaration names it: the Hannon
   pre-malignant series is the graded-drive axis this question wants, and it is
   not in hand. This is the best available substitute, not the intended test.
2. **The TCGA solid-tissue-normal comparison** (~113 samples), named in the
   declaration's section 6 as the companion that gives the anchor *below* the
   tumours. It needs its own declaration and carries its own confounder, since
   normal-adjacent breast is largely stroma and adipose.
3. **A burden measure that is not a proliferation correlate.** All three here
   run +0.343 to +0.568 with `PROLIF_DISJOINT`, and all three correlate 0.50 to
   0.80 with each other, so they are **three versions of one measurement**, not
   three tests.
4. **Within-Basal estimates.** Basal has a minimum quadrant cell of 0 in both
   continuous proxies, so the within-subtype layer is effectively Luminal-only
   and the claim is a Luminal claim wherever it is subtype-controlled.
