## The argument

The human arm asks whether established breast tumours carry the configuration the mouse predicts, and whether it is separate from proliferation.

1. **Selection.** The hypothesis asks for BCL-2 family genes that track OXPHOS at fixed proliferation. Six do, and together they form the signature (Fig. 4g).
2. **Justification.** BCL-xL rises more steeply than PUMA, the reverse of the MYC-expressing gland. BAD and MCL1 are explained rather than set aside.
3. **Illustration.** In one joint model the signature follows OXPHOS, the two mouse genes fall with proliferation, and MYC activity adds to OXPHOS (Fig. 4g).
4. **Clinical link.** OXPHOS and proliferation also separate on chemotherapy response (Fig. 4h).

**The two points, and where each lands.**

- **Proliferation and OXPHOS are separate.** The selection paragraph sets it up, the joint model shows it on the mouse genes, and the clinical paragraph and closing sentences carry it to the patient.
- **High-OXPHOS tumours, and those high in both OXPHOS and MYC activity most, carry pro-apoptotic signalling restrained by BCL-xL.** The balance and MCL1 paragraphs give the configuration, and the joint model adds MYC activity.

## Results text

Ready to paste into the Doc. The Extended Data letters are placeholders, mapped under Figure references.

### Bridge: last sentence of the mouse paragraph

> Consequently, established human tumours with high OXPHOS and MYC activity were predicted to carry raised pro-apoptotic signalling restrained by BCL-xL, a configuration distinct from that accompanying proliferation.

### 1. The selection

> In tumours, however, OXPHOS rises with both MYC activity and proliferation (Extended Data Fig. Xa), so testing this prediction required OXPHOS to be separated from proliferation. We therefore searched the twelve BH3-only and anti-apoptotic genes of the BCL-2 family in TCGA-BRCA (n = 1,095) and SCAN-B (n = 3,207) for those whose expression tracked OXPHOS at fixed proliferation. Six of the eleven mitochondrial members did, and together they formed a signature (Fig. 4g). BCL2L1, encoding BCL-xL, rose with the activators BBC3 and BID and the sensitisers BAD and BIK, whilst MCL1 fell.

### 2. The balance

> Its structure fitted the predicted restraint of pro-apoptotic signalling by BCL-xL, as BCL2L1 rose more steeply with OXPHOS than BBC3, encoding PUMA, in both cohorts (Extended Data Fig. Xb). This is the reverse of the MYC-expressing gland at 6 and 12 weeks, in which PUMA rose relative to BCL-xL with OXPHOS priority (Fig. 1i; Extended Data Fig. Xk), and resembles the mouse tumours that sustained high OXPHOS only alongside Bcl-xL (Fig. 3h,i). BAD rose more steeply than BCL2L1 (Fig. 4g), but BAD neutralises BCL-xL only when unphosphorylated, and survival signals phosphorylate it and hold it away from BCL-xL\[Zha\]. Its expression therefore does not measure its activity, unlike that of PUMA, which is controlled mainly through transcription\[Nakano\].

### 3. MCL1

> MCL1 fell as BCL-xL rose, so BCL-xL was the only one of the two main guardians to rise alongside PUMA, which binds both\[Chen\]. MCL1's reported support of respiration in breast cancer stem cells depends on a mitochondrial function separable from its anti-apoptotic role\[27\]. This suggests that tumours with high OXPHOS rely on BCL-xL rather than MCL1, like persistent OXPHOS-high triple-negative breast cancer cells, which depend on BCL-xL and are insensitive to MCL1 inhibition\[26\].

### 4. The joint model

> With OXPHOS, MYC activity and proliferation modelled together and intrinsic subtype adjusted for, the signature followed OXPHOS but not proliferation, as its selection intended (Fig. 4g). So did BBC3 and BCL2L1, the two genes named by the mouse before any selection (Extended Data Fig. Xh). Both were lower in more proliferative tumours at fixed OXPHOS and MYC activity (Fig. 4g). MYC activity played no part in the selection, yet it went with the signature in the same direction as OXPHOS, so that their associations added (Fig. 4g). This held on the two MYC scores least correlated with proliferation, and was weaker on scores that track proliferation closely (Extended Data Fig. Xa,j). The effectors BAX, BAK1 and BOK, outside the signature, also rose with OXPHOS and MYC activity in both cohorts (Fig. 4g).

### 5. The clinical link

The opening sentence is new. The two sentences after it were set by the 2 November brief, and the rest of the paragraph stands as in the Doc.

> Having separated OXPHOS from proliferation on PUMA and BCL-xL, we next asked whether the two are also separable in their association with chemotherapy response. Additional adjustment for MYC activity, and then for the mean expression of BCL2L1 and MCL1, did not materially alter the OXPHOS–response association (pooled odds ratios 0.87 and 0.88). The transcriptional coupling of OXPHOS and proliferation therefore concealed a clinically relevant distinction.

### Methods: the signature

E47's definition as the addendum amended it, with four sentences re-cut to fit STYLE.md (14 to 34 words each).

> We examined twelve BCL-2-family genes that set the threshold for mitochondrial apoptosis: seven BH3-only proteins (BCL2L11/BIM, BID, BBC3/PUMA, BAD, BIK, BMF and PMAIP1/NOXA) and five anti-apoptotic proteins (BCL2, BCL2L1/BCL-xL, MCL1, BCL2L2/BCL-w and BCL2A1/BFL-1). For each gene we computed the Spearman correlation of its expression with the OXPHOS subunit score (GSVA), partial on a proliferation score, in TCGA-BRCA and SCAN-B, and averaged the two cohorts. The signature comprised every gene that is a mitochondrial protein (Human MitoCarta 3.0 lists eleven of the twelve, but not BMF) and whose mean correlation with OXPHOS was at least 0.20 in absolute value. Five rose with OXPHOS (BAD, BCL2L1, BIK, BBC3 and BID; mean rho 0.21 to 0.50) and MCL1 fell (mean rho −0.27). Selection did not use correlation with MYC activity, although BID, which qualified narrowly (0.21), is the one member more strongly correlated with MYC activity (0.30) than with OXPHOS. Each gene was assigned a signature sign, +1 if it rises with OXPHOS and −1 if it falls, recording direction, not pro- or anti-apoptotic function. A tumour's signature score was the mean of the six signed z-scores (log2-normalised expression, standardised across the cohort), rank-normalised within each cohort. Because genes and signs were chosen by their association with OXPHOS at fixed proliferation in the same cohorts, the signature's association with OXPHOS, and in part with proliferation, is expected by construction. The informative quantity is its association with MYC activity at fixed OXPHOS and proliferation. All analyses were repeated with two rule-based variants (Extended Data Fig. Xg), the first applying the same rule without the mitochondrial restriction and adding BMF with sign −1. The second kept the five genes whose correlation with OXPHOS exceeded their correlation with MYC activity by at least 0.20 in at least one cohort, omitting BID. It is therefore selected against association with MYC activity and is reported for transparency, not as an independent test. All twelve genes are also reported individually, with the effectors BAX, BAK1 and BOK, which were fitted for comparison with the groups of Fig. 1g and not added to the signature.

### Methods: notes

> Each model entered OXPHOS, MYC activity and proliferation as main effects, and their interactions were not tested. Binding of BCL-xL and MCL1 by each BH3-only protein was taken from published peptide affinities and is shown only where the source states it\[Chen,Ku,Wang\]. This analysis was post hoc, and the signature's proliferation coefficient had been seen in an earlier analysis before it was declared; no reading rule was applied, and every coefficient is reported.
