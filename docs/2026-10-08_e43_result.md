# E43 - the proliferation coefficient, retrieved

**Option A: Claude Code wrote the script, the author sourced it.** Nothing was
rescored, `METABRIC_DATA.RData` was not reloaded, and nothing was written to
`myc_human_validation` (`d3ac60e`) or `myc_mouse`; `h4_outcome_models.rds` was
read read-only.

## 1. Verification, and the gate that made the retrieval readable

Four redirected dry runs; the final version ran twice and agreed on **14 of 14**
digest-comparable elements; the digests went into `f3b4438` **before** the
author's run; the author's run matched **14 of 14**, and `$fit_digests` matched.

**The reproduction gate passed with a discrepancy of exactly 0** in all eight
cells, on the estimate *and* the SE, with n and events matching, and the
re-pooled OX fixed effect reproducing E40's stored `-0.161555`. 14.3 asked for
six decimal places and got bit-identity. **So these are the models E40 and E41
fitted, and the PROLIF coefficient below is the one they computed and
discarded** (14.1) - not a new estimate from a similar model.

`$fits` is excluded from digest comparison by design: `coxph` and `glm` objects
carry `terms`, `formula`, `model` and `family`, each holding an environment
whose address differs between sessions. Verified that coefficients and variance
matrices are identical in every fit and only those carriers differ.
**`$fit_digests` is the stable handle.**

### 3.1 14.5's expected attenuation was too pessimistic, and that is worth recording

14.5 stated before the fit that subtype is substantially a proliferation
classification, that much of proliferation's signal is absorbed before the
PROLIF term is reached, and that **"an attenuated or null PROLIF coefficient is
expected and does not refute the published pattern."**

**The coefficient is attenuated and it is not null.** It survives subtype
adjustment in both arms - substantially on pCR (OR 1.44 to 2.28 per
within-cohort SD) and modestly on survival (HR 1.13 to 1.23). So 14.5's
protective clause was not needed, and the thing it was protecting against did
not happen. **Recording that the pre-stated expectation was wrong in the
conservative direction matters as much as recording one that was right.**

## 4. 14.6's reading rule, applied - row 1, with the asymmetry attached

Row 1 requires PROLIF positive on pCR **and** above unity on survival, **both
intervals excluding the null.** On the declared primaries - the three-cohort
pooled pCR estimate and the pooled METABRIC ladder - both conditions hold. **So
the Results may state the inversion as an internal observation.**

**What that sentence may and may not lean on, because the two halves are not
equally strong:**

| | pCR half | survival half |
|---|---|---|
| **PROLIF** | pooled +0.5422 [0.307, 0.778], three cohorts, signs agree | pooled HR 1.1336 [1.009, 1.274]; ER-pos 1.2292 [1.075, 1.406] |
| **OX** | pooled -0.1616 [-0.281, -0.042], I2 0, **the one quantity meeting section 9's rule** | ER-pos m1 HR 0.8962, **excluding zero by 3.7e-04**, sensitivity p 0.186, m2 and m3 including zero |

**PROLIF's two halves are both solid. OX's are not.** E42's result note records
the METABRIC ER-positive `m1` as one boundary cell of thirty whose own declared
sensitivity does not reproduce it, and **E43 does not change that by one
decimal.** The inversion is therefore an observation about a *pair of
directions*, with the OX survival limb carrying its existing caveat unmodified.

**14.6's third row is not reached**, and the prohibition attached to it stands
anyway: both endpoints are retrieved and both are reported, so no endpoint was
selected for cooperating.

## 5. The larger gain is inferential: PROLIF is an unplanned positive control

Inside the same models, with the same spine, on the same patients:

- **PROLIF**: better pCR, worse survival. That is the canonical proliferation
  paradox, and it is exactly **row 1 of section 9.1's sign-pair table, "the
  proliferation route"**.
- **OX**: worse pCR, better survival. That is **the combination 9.1 never
  anticipated**, which 14.2 records as a gap rather than repairing
  retrospectively.

**So the models can detect a known sign pair in these data.** That matters for
how OX's result is read: OX's failure to show the canonical pattern is not a
failure of the models, the spine, the cohorts or the endpoints, because a term
sitting beside it in the same fits shows that pattern cleanly.

**This is a post-hoc characterisation of a declared term, not a pre-declared
control.** PROLIF was in the model as a covariate under D7, not as a control,
and 14.5 named its expected direction only to caution against over-reading an
attenuated one. **It functions as a control; it was not designed as one**, and
any manuscript sentence must say so.

**The mirror is directional, not symmetric in magnitude.** OX's pooled pCR
coefficient is about a third of PROLIF's in absolute terms (-0.162 against
+0.495) and its ER-positive log hazard about half (-0.110 against +0.206).

## 6. A correction to this arm's own VIF reporting

**14.4's claim about m1 is now measured.** MYC is absent from m1, so
`rho(MYC, PROLIF_DISJOINT)` of 0.78 to 0.82 does not bear on this coefficient.
Every **PROLIF** VIF is **1.298 to 2.142** and every **OX** VIF **1.206 to
1.358** - both 1-df terms, so GVIF equals VIF - and the two are separately
identified at every rung.

> **CORRECTION, found while reading this run and applied to the script
> afterwards (display only).** The script's first version flagged two values as
> "above the section 5.3 caveat threshold of 5": GSE194040's `subtype_term`
> GVIF 7.979 and `treatment` GVIF 7.269. **That comparison is wrong.** Section
> 5.3's threshold of 5 was set for OX, a **1-df** term; a **multi-df GVIF is
> not on that scale**, and Fox and Monette's prescription is to compare
> `GVIF^(1/(2*Df))` against the square root of the 1-df threshold. Adjusted,
> those two are **1.414** (3 df) and **1.086** (12 df), and **no term in any of
> the eight models exceeds sqrt(5) = 2.236** - the maximum anywhere is
> **1.463**.
>
> So **there is no collinearity caveat to carry**, and the raw figures concern
> the **spine** rather than either exposure: they are I-SPY2's arm assignment by
> receptor status, which is a property of that trial's design.
>
> `gvif_adj` is **not** added to `$vif_vectors`, so that table's digest and the
> verified run are untouched. It is recomputable from the two saved columns as
> `vif^(1/(2*vif_df))`.

**This correction exists only because 14.7 required the full VIF vector.** Under
the one-row extraction that 14.1 describes, neither the figures nor the error in
reading them would have been visible.

## 7. What this licenses, and what it does not

### 7.1 Licensed

1. **The inversion as an internal observation** (14.6 row 1), with section 4's
   asymmetry table attached: PROLIF's two halves are solid, OX's survival limb
   carries E42's boundary-cell caveat unchanged.
2. **The positive-control statement** (section 5), worded as a post-hoc
   characterisation of a declared covariate and never as a designed control.
3. **The methodological correction** in section 6, and the statement that it
   was only visible because the full vector was saved.
4. **That 14.5's pre-stated expectation was wrong in the conservative
   direction** - the coefficient survives subtype adjustment rather than
   collapsing.

### 7.2 NOT licensed

- **Nothing about m0, m2 or m3.** Only m1 was retrieved (14.4), and at
  `rho(MYC, PROLIF)` of 0.71 to 0.82 the higher rungs do not separate the two
  terms.
- **Nothing that strengthens the OX survival side.** E43 adds no information
  about it; the boundary cell is exactly as weak as E42 left it.
- **No claim that proliferation's effect is demonstrated here** beyond these
  models. 14.5's instruction to cite the published pattern for the relationship
  itself still applies: what is shown is that proliferation behaves canonically
  *within subtype* in these four cohorts.
- **No cross-cohort numeric comparison** (4.2). The pCR coefficients are log
  odds ratios and the METABRIC ones log hazard ratios; the exponentiated
  columns are labelled by family and must not be pooled or compared as one
  scale.
- **Nothing from the ER-negative strata**, in either direction.
- **No causal claim**, no interaction, no effect-modification test, no p value
  described as a near miss.
- **SINGLE-INSTRUMENT**, as the whole line is.

## 8. Limitations

1. **The pCR heterogeneity is real** - Q p 0.049, I2 66.9 - and the largest
   estimate sits in the smallest cohort. The random-effects summary is the one
   to read.
2. **A retrieval inherits every limitation of what it retrieves.** These are
   E40's and E41's models; their spines, coverage caveats and endpoint
   definitions apply unchanged.
3. **The reading is post-hoc** (14.2), and the sign-pair combination it
   describes was not in section 9.1's table. The gap is recorded, not repaired.
4. **PROLIF functions as a control but was not designed as one** (section 5).
5. **Both ER-negative strata are uninformative** on survival, so the survival
   half of the observation rests on ER-positive disease.
6. **GSE194040's spine carries the arm's largest raw GVIFs**, and although they
   adjust to modest values, that cohort's subtype and treatment are confounded
   by trial design; its PROLIF estimate is the smallest of the three.
7. **One empty-level drop in each ER-negative stratum** - `NC` under IHC, `LumB`
   and `NC` under `ER.Expr` - reproduced from E41. Dropping an empty level
   changes no estimate.
