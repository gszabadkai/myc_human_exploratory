# E39 / E40 / E41 - decomposing the respiratory axis against two endpoints

**Option A throughout: Claude Code wrote the scripts, the author sourced them.**
Nothing was written to `myc_human_validation`, frozen at `d3ac60e`, or to
`myc_mouse`. Every number below was read from the saved objects, not
transcribed from a console.

## 1. Why this exists, and what it is not

The draft contains the sentence *"respiration did not itself predict
response"*. **Its supporting quantity had never been fitted** with a covariate
set, an interval or a pooled estimate. The only trace was three numbers printed
once in an `if (FALSE)` sandbox at `scripts/13_outcome_models.R:745` and
surviving as prose in the Block F1 note:

```
GSE194040  -0.074   p 0.32
GSE164458  -0.066   p 0.54
GSE25066   -0.120   p 0.37
```

No CIs, no meta-analysis, no subtype or treatment adjustment. **Those three are
the `m2` estimand** (section 4.1), and this analysis fits the ladder they sit in.

**It is not a test of anything.** It decomposes one exposure into what
proliferation carries and what it does not, against two endpoints, in cohorts
already on disk.

## 7. What this licenses, and what it does not

### 7.1 Licensed - one clause and four statements

1. **The pCR clause.** *"Once proliferation is adjusted for, higher OXPHOS
   subunit expression is associated with lower pathological complete response
   across three neoadjuvant cohorts (pooled -0.16 log odds per within-cohort SD,
   95% CI -0.28 to -0.04, sign consistent in all three, I2 = 0)."* **Three
   qualifications travel with it and are not optional**: it is SINGLE-INSTRUMENT
   (4, and METABRIC is an array too, 10.5); the unadjusted coefficient does NOT
   support it and is reported beside it; and GSE25066 covers 57 of 89 OXPHOS
   subunits on GPL96, so **no coefficient is compared numerically with a TCGA or
   SCAN-B one** (4.1, 4.2).
2. **The methodological statement about the draft.** The sentence *"respiration
   did not itself predict response"* refers to `m0`, a quantity that **had never
   been fitted** and which, now fitted, **does not meet the declared reading
   rule in either direction** - signs `+ - +`, pooled CI including zero, I2 48.8.
   The draft sentence must be replaced or dropped, not re-sourced.
3. **The forkscale statement.** `rho(OX, MB1_forkscale) = 0.416` in METABRIC,
   against F3-pre's 0.529 (GSVA) and 0.418 (mitoPPS). **Three readings, two
   instruments, two cohorts, all inside the INTERMEDIATE band**, and this one in
   the cohort the forks were derived from. **INTERMEDIATE is a property of the
   relationship, not an artefact of one instrument.** `F3 WAS SPECIFIED IN`
   `myc_human_validation`, **FROZEN AT `d3ac60e`** - these rungs are NOT F3 and
   must be named separate exploratory analyses in every sentence.
4. **That the identifiability limit is measured, not assumed.**
   `rho(MYC, PROLIF_DISJOINT) = 0.8236` in GSE164458 is in the declaration's own
   NOT IDENTIFIABLE band, and 0.78 to 0.79 elsewhere - while every VIF for OX
   stays between 1.00 and 1.43, so the collinearity **did not reach the
   exposure**.
5. **That the 69-gene METABRIC restriction is cosmetic, measured rather than
   assumed** (10.4): Spearman 0.9960 (TCGA) and 0.9954 (SCAN-B) against the full
   89, largest movement in any manuscript reading -0.0174.

## 8. The previous leg's open integrity item, CLOSED

The E39/E40 handoff recorded that E40's run emitted **46 warnings** never
captured, and called it the only thing between the run and this note.

**They are `car::vif`'s "No intercept: vifs may not be sensible".** A Cox model
has no intercept by design and the VIF comes from the correlation matrix of the
coefficient estimates, which needs none, so the warning carries no information.
E40 made **40 fits** and called `car::vif` **twice per fit** - the double call
its own handoff flagged as a defect. E41 counts them, muffles them deliberately
and reports the count once: **30 calls, 30 warnings.**

**No number in E40 is affected.** The warning was never about them.

## 9. Limitations, measured

1. **SINGLE-INSTRUMENT throughout.** mitoPPS exists in none of the four
   cohorts: three are arrays or targeted panels and METABRIC is an array. Blocks
   C, B and G each required agreement between two instruments. **This has not
   cleared that bar and must never be written as though it had.**
2. **Exposure coverage differs by cohort and may not be pooled as a value.**
   GSE25066 57 of 89 (64.0%) on GPL96; METABRIC 69 of 89 (77.5%); TCGA and
   SCAN-B 88 of 89. Within-cohort SD standardisation makes the coefficients
   comparable in units, **not in instrument**.
3. **The METABRIC spine is not GSE25066's** (10.1a). PAM50 there, a two-level
   ER/HER2 collapse here. The two `m0` are different models and the prose must
   say so.
4. **Two gene-level exclusions, both because an alias pointed at another gene.**
   `COX7A2L` is out because its only METABRIC route is `SCAF1`, the approved
   symbol of SR-related CTD associated factor 1 (58506); `POLR1B` is out because
   its only route is `RPA2`, approved for replication protein A2 (6118).
   `ATP5F1E` is out with no route at all, and `ATP5EP2`, a pseudogene, was not
   substituted. **The guard is on the route, not the symbol**: `RPA2` is a
   legitimate direct member of `PROLIF_DISJOINT`, and a global ban on the symbol
   would have silently altered the proliferation score in every model (10.3a).
5. **`BBC3` is absent from METABRIC**, so the 6-gene signed configuration cannot
   be fitted there and a five-gene stand-in is forbidden. **Any future METABRIC
   analysis touching the configuration is blocked on this.**
6. **Global proportional hazards fails almost everywhere** - 27 of 30 METABRIC
   models, 10 of 12 GSE25066 DRFS models. Expected over this follow-up, declared
   not a reason to change the model, and reported rather than fixed.
7. **The ER sensitivity reclassifies, it does not re-read.** 54 of 465 patients
   carrying 11 events in GSE25066; 127 of 1,940 in METABRIC, with 28 Basal and
   32 Her2 moving out of ER-positive. In GSE25066 the esr1 strata are
   additionally **unadjusted for subtype** (7.1).
8. **METABRIC's provenance rests on the author's authority.** Synapse
   `syn1757063` / `syn1688369` per Menegollo's Methods; **nothing inside the
   file names Synapse**, the readr `col_spec` is consistent-with and not
   evidence, and the md5 is the only check (4.5).
9. **Two forkscale readings the declaration never anticipated.**
   `rho(PROLIF, MB1_forkscale) = 0.619` **exceeds** `rho(OX, MB1) = 0.416`, and
   `rho(MB1, MB2) = 0.865`. The first means the published fork axis tracks
   proliferation more closely than respiration in this cohort; the second means
   the two fork rungs are near-duplicates, and their fitted coefficients differ
   by 0.007 on the log scale. **Neither is a finding and neither has a falsifier
   list** - see 10.4.

   alternatives, scored the same way - moves the pooled `m1` CI across zero.
   The clause asserts a proliferation-adjusted association, not an association
   adjusted for one particular 318-gene list.
3. **The OXPHOS exposure scored on an instrument other than GSVA** in these same

4. **Restricting to the genes measured in all three cohorts** moves the pooled

### 10.2 For the METABRIC ER-positive `m1` cell, which is NOT currently a finding

It would become one **only if all three held**, and each is a falsifier of the
cell as it stands:

1. **Its own `ER.Expr` sensitivity reproduces it** - it does not, at p 0.186.
2. **`m2` and `m3` also exclude zero** - they do not.
3. **A second ER-positive prognostic cohort with comparable events reproduces
   it.** METABRIC is currently the only one in this arm.

**Until all three hold, the cell is reported as one boundary cell of thirty and
nothing is built on it.**

### 10.3 For the forkscale INTERMEDIATE statement

2. **The relationship proving to be an ER artefact**: within strata it is 0.433
