### 5.2 On the pooled estimate

PH fails for OX in every pooled BCSS model while holding in both strata, and
the two strata carry opposite-signed coefficients. A pooled coefficient
averaging two opposite effects is what produces a time-varying one. **Reading
the pooled m0 as a null is therefore not available on this evidence** - the
quantity is not stable over follow-up. Whether to say so, and in what words, is
an authorial decision this note does not make.

### 5.3 On the forkscale question

Three readings now exist: F3-pre at **0.529 (GSVA)** and **0.418 (mitoPPS)**, and
METABRIC at **0.416** - the last in the cohort the forks were derived from. All
three sit inside the INTERMEDIATE band. **INTERMEDIATE looks like a property of
the relationship rather than an artefact of one instrument.** That is the one
new thing E41 settles, and `F3 WAS SPECIFIED IN myc_human_validation, FROZEN AT
d3ac60e - THESE RUNGS ARE NOT F3` and must be named separate exploratory

### 6.1 What the dry runs caught, for the record

Four defects, all fixed before `750af99`:

1. **The alias guard was too broad and the script STOPPED on it.** It forbade
   `SCAF1`, `RPA2` and `ATP5EP2` from entering any resolved set - but **`RPA2`
   is a legitimate DIRECT member of `PROLIF_DISJOINT`**, because replication
   protein A2 is a proliferation gene. A global ban would have removed a real
   member of the 318 and **changed the proliferation score in every rung of
   every model**, silently, under the appearance of extra rigour. The script now
   carries per-row provenance and guards the **route**, not the symbol.
   Declaration 10.3a amended at `82659ff`.
2. **`car::vif` returned NA for every ER-negative stratum**, because PAM50
   carries levels with zero observations there and `vif` errors on aliased
   coefficients. Empty levels are now dropped **explicitly and reported** per
   fitting set - `NC` in ER-neg (IHC), `LumB` and `NC` in ER-neg (`ER.Expr`).
   The estimates are byte-identical before and after, which is what an empty
   level being empty means.
3. **Two composition assertions could never fire**: they compared a `table`
   against a named integer with `identical()`.
4. **The ER-fork fits reused the stratum label as the `FITS` key** and silently
   overwrote the m0-m3 stratum fits, dropping them from the PH table.

And after the run, one display defect: **the ER-positive m1 bound is HR 0.99963
and printed as `1.000` at 3 dp**, so a reader applying "CI excludes zero" to the
printed number would have got the opposite answer from the rule applied to the
value. Fixed at `a364e36`, display only.
