### 2.2 H1. OXPHOS and proliferation run in opposite directions across most of the family, and not by function

## Part 3. Against the mouse, by direction only

**The rules (brief 1.2 and 4.8).**

### 3.1 Fig. 1a,c: proliferation maintained whilst the apoptotic response was lost

- **Mouse.**
  - Between 6 and 12 weeks, the Ki-67 to cleaved caspase-3 ratio rose, through loss of apoptosis against constant proliferation (Fig. 1a).
  - Acute Myc at a constant dose reproduced this (Fig. 1c).
  - `Bbc3` fell most steeply over the same window (Fig. 1h,i).
- **Human.** At fixed OXPHOS and MYC activity, more proliferation goes with lower `BBC3` in all four blocks.
- **Direction: it agrees for PUMA.** In neither system does proliferation travel with the PUMA transcript.
  - The mouse separates them in time, within one genotype.
  - The human data separate them across tumours, within one model.
- **Where it does not extend.**
  - Human proliferation also goes with lower `BCL2L1`, whereas the adult mouse gland kept `Bcl2l1` stable (Fig. 1g).
  - The mouse has no proliferation contrast that could give this a direction. So it is a gap, not a disagreement.
- Brief 10.3 points referee question R9 to Fig. 1a,c in exactly this way.

### 3.2 Fig. 1f: OXPHOS against proliferation in the developing gland

- **Mouse.** In the wild-type gland, OXPHOS expression and priority fell between 6 and 12 weeks about five times more than the proliferative signature. This was tested as OXPHOS minus proliferation.
- **Human.** OXPHOS and proliferation rise together (rho 0.23 to 0.42), yet have opposite associations with `BBC3` and `BCL2L1`. This was tested as proliferation minus OXPHOS, within one model.
- **Direction: the same logical form, not the same estimand** (brief 1.2).
  - Both separate OXPHOS from proliferation with a contrast inside one system.
  - In the mouse, the two moved the same way by different amounts. In the human data they move together and associate oppositely with the readouts.
  - They may be paralleled in words, never on a shared axis.

### 3.3 Fig. 2f,g: Pgc-1alpha raised PUMA independently of Myc

- **Mouse.** In iMMECs, Pgc-1alpha raised PUMA without Myc induction. Death followed only with Myc.
- **Human.** At fixed MYC activity and proliferation, higher OXPHOS goes with higher `BBC3` in all four blocks, and C1 leaves that association unchanged.
- **Direction: it agrees.**
  - An intervention on OXPHOS raised PUMA in the mouse.
  - OXPHOS and `BBC3` are positively associated at fixed MYC activity in human tumours.
- **The human sentence says "at fixed MYC activity"**, never "independently of MYC" (brief 4.9). A score held fixed in a model is not a genotype held constant.

### 3.4 Fig. 3h,i: high OXPHOS sustained only with Bcl-xL

- **Mouse.** Orthotopic tumours retained full Pgc-1alpha and OXPHOS priority only when Bcl-xL was co-expressed (Fig. 3h,i).
- **Human.** `BCL2L1` rises with OXPHOS more steeply than `BBC3`, with MYC activity and proliferation held fixed, in all four blocks (C3).
- **Direction: consistent, with one disagreement that the text must not hide.**
  - The mouse link is a requirement for exogenous Bcl-xL.
  - **Endogenous `Bcl2l1` did not rise in the construct-free arm** (`NTPgc1a` against `NTEV`; mouse escape-series note, section 4).
  - The construct-carrying contrasts are uninterpretable at gene level.
  - **So the mouse shows no endogenous transcript counterpart to the human `BCL2L1` association.**
- **Provenance note 6.3 lists `BCL2L1` as agreeing**, on a construct-carrying contrast. That entry should not be cited as agreement.
- **The OXPHOS-side paragraph's "This resembled the mouse tumours ..."** (Part 4) sets a human transcript association beside a mouse requirement, and says no more than that.

### 3.5 The orthotopic tumours, where Bbc3 fell as OXPHOS rose

- **Mouse.**
  - Across three backgrounds, `Bbc3` fell in order of the PGC1alpha dose each arm retained, against that arm's own control. It did not move where the transgene was lost.
  - **The mouse note states this as selection:** tumours that retained more PGC1alpha show less `Bbc3`.
  - PUMA protein remained elevated in the same tumours.
- **Human.** `BBC3` rises with OXPHOS in all four blocks.
- **Direction: it DISAGREES**, as provenance note 6.3 says. Nothing in E48 removes the disagreement.
- **Three hypotheses, none of them tested:**
  1. **Selection.** The orthotopic arms carried a sustained, imposed OXPHOS push and are its survivors. No human tumour carries that intervention.
  2. **Transcript and protein diverge in the mouse.** PUMA protein is up while the `Bbc3` transcript is down. The human data are transcripts, so the comparable mouse measure is the one that disagrees.
  3. **Proliferation.** Human proliferation goes with lower `BBC3`. If the high-dose arms were also the most proliferative, the mouse fall would match the human proliferation direction rather than the OXPHOS one.
     - **This is already strained.** On the non-targeting background, `Bbc3` fell whilst the tumours grew at the control rate (mouse section 3 narrative, 2026-10-27, on Fig. 3g). Growth rate is not a proliferation index, though.
     - **Falsifier:** a proliferation score in the orthotopic arms that does not rise in order of retained PGC1alpha dose.

### 3.6 C1 against the mouse's p53 observations

- **Mouse, gland.**
  - In parallel with the `Bbc3` fall, `Foxo3`, a p53-independent activator of PUMA, diverged more between the two timelines than any other gene evaluated (Doc ED Fig. 3c).

- **Human, C1.**

### 3.7 The death step, which the human data cannot test

- **Mouse.** Death was determined by Myc status rather than by PUMA upregulation alone (Doc, Fig. 2d,h,i and ED Fig. 4d):
  - Pgc-1alpha raised PUMA but induced death only with elevated Myc;
  - Ndi1 induced death on Myc elevation without raising PUMA.
- **Human.**
  - The data are transcripts from tumours that survived.
  - Only main effects were fitted, with no product term and no measure of death.
- **E48 cannot test the death step, and no sentence may imply that it does** (brief 4.6). The Doc already says so.

### 3.8 Added: MCL1, a further disagreement

- **Mouse.** Endogenous `Mcl1` did not move in the construct-free orthotopic contrast, and it was flat across the gland timeline.
- **Human.** OXPHOS goes with lower `MCL1` in all four blocks, and proliferation's sign splits by cohort.
- **Direction: not agreement.** The mouse is null where the human OXPHOS association is negative, and it gives no direction for proliferation.
