## 3. Decisions waiting on the author (proposal section 5)

1. **4g row blocks.** The proposal is to block by function: pro-apoptotic
   against anti-apoptotic. v5's MitoCarta/cytosolic split cannot be drawn from
   these twelve genes.
2. **4g instrument.** The proposal is GSVA on the main panel and mitoPPS in ED
   Fig. 7. The reason is consistency with 4h and 4i, not the result.
3. **The axis convention for 4h and 4i.** The proposal is ratios on a log axis
   with 1 marked. The alternative is log units with 0 marked. v5's mixture is
   not an option.
4. **4i rows.** The proposal is four rows:
   - the METABRIC ER-positive IHC primary;
   - the `ER.Expr` sensitivity;
   - the pooled ladder;
   - ER-negative IHC.

   Adding GSE25066 DRFS instead needs a declaration section 16 and a refit
   before anything is drawn.
5. **The validation study's place in the paper.** CLAUDE.md says it "does not
   go in the paper". v5 uses it three times:
   - paragraph 3's co-amplification;
   - paragraph 4's "pre-registered" sentence;
   - ED Fig. 9.

   **This blocks the narrative, not 4g-4i**, but one of the two documents must
   change.

## 4. What this session found that would bite the next one

1. **11 of the 12 configuration genes are in Human MitoCarta 3.0.** Only `BMF`
   is not. Paragraph 3's "cytosolic half" is **E14's 24-gene cytosolic half of
   the 44-gene machinery** (`FAS`, `TNF`, `FADD`, `XIAP`, `APAF1`, ...). It is
   a different gene set from 4g's rows, and the two must not be conflated in a
   legend.
2. **GSE25066 DRFS is excluded from the contrast by declaration 15.4.** No
   PROLIF term for it exists. v5 specified it as 4i's third row regardless.
3. **The E34 figure joins no quadrant pairs with a line**, because no product
   term was fitted. 4g keeps that rule. The only connector proposed anywhere is
   4h's `m0` to `m1`, which joins two rungs of one term.
4. **`rho(OX, PROLIF_DISJOINT)` in METABRIC is 0.381.**
   - Computed read-only this session from `e41_metabric_ladder.rds$frame`, with
     n 1,981 and Spearman.
   - **It is in no note or table.**
   - v5 paragraph 4's "0.36 to 0.49 in all four" depends on it.
   - **Do not cite it until it is recorded.** E45 should print it.
5. **The OX ER-positive upper bound is 0.9996** and rounds to 1.000 at three
   decimals. The proposal prints no OX interval on 4i.
6. **On both forests, PROLIF sits right of 1 and OX left of 1.** Without
   direction cues under each axis, 4h and 4i look like the same pattern, when
   the narrative claims an inversion. The proposal adds the cues.
7. **The E34 figure's caption carries a verdict and interpretation**, which
   v5's descriptive-only legend rule forbids. If any part of that figure is
   reused, the caption must be rewritten.

## 5. Text edits the panels force (proposal section 4)

**Not made.** v5 is the author's document. Six sentences would be contradicted
by the panels as drawn:

1. **"the two OXPHOS-high groups carrying the configuration equally"**: SCAN-B
   `Q4 − Q2` is +0.134 [0.037, 0.231].
2. **The adjustment list names purity and leukocyte fraction for both
   cohorts**: SCAN-B has neither, and proliferation enters at `m3` only.
3. **"four to seven times less"**: no rung gives that range in both cohorts.
4. **"the same amount at either respiratory level"**: it fails in TCGA/GSVA,
   the cell 4g draws.
5. **Paragraph 3 cites 4g for ten per-gene directions**: cite ED Fig. 10 for
   those, and ED Fig. 7 for the compartment and conditioning sentences.
6. **"0.36 to 0.49 in all four"**: true, but METABRIC's value is unrecorded
   (item 4 of section 4).

Also flagged, though not contradicted by any panel:

- "held in every subtype" overstates TNBC, which is heterogeneous rather than
  held;
- if the ratio axis is adopted, the ORs should be added beside the quoted
  log-odds figures.
