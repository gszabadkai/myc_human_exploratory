   correlations against two nested composite rulers without its size-matched

## 6. Two conventions worth keeping, because they earned their place this week

**Pre-specify a battery, not a coefficient.** `E23`'s pooled `beta1` was
positive with intervals excluding zero in 5 of 6 cells on one ruler and 6 of 6
on the other. Read alone that is a confirmation. **The battery is the only
thing that stopped it being written as one**, and it was fixed in the script
header before the fits.

**Give a heuristic a positive control.** `E23`'s entanglement rule is a
three-point monotonicity check - weak on its own, 1 in 3 by chance per cohort.
It was pre-specified with `E22`'s `BBC3` as a **positive control**, so a
non-firing result could not be read as reassurance. The control fired on both
rulers, which is the only reason the rule's verdict carries any weight.
