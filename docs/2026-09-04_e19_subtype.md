# E19 - is the configuration compositional? No.

**N3 throughout.** Transcript abundances and, in section 5, mouse regression
slopes. Neither a tumour, a cell line nor an animal is described as "primed".

## 1. TASK 1 - the twelve genes inside Luminal and Basal

### 1.1 What was computed, and the one thing that would have made it wrong

12 BCL2-family genes x 4 rulers x 2 cohorts x 3 strata = **288 cells**, partial
Spearman on `PROLIF_DISJOINT`, Fisher-z intervals with the Bonett-Wright
variance, `.cor_block` verbatim from E16 and E10.

**No re-scoring within stratum.** GSVA and every z-score are cohort-relative, so
re-scoring inside Basal builds a *different axis* and answers a different
question. The pooled scores are subset, exactly as E10's `.stratum_cors` does
it. This is the single most likely silent error in the whole session, which is
why it has a positive control rather than a comment:

> **Reproduction check.** E10's `component_strata` already carries this block
> for two of the four rulers - `OXPHOS` is `ox_gsva`, `OXPHOS_mitopps` is
> `ox_ppd` - on the same genes, strata and covariate. All **144** shared cells
> come back **bit-equal** (max `|d_rho|` = 0, exactly). The two new rulers are
> therefore on the same axes as the two the repo already trusts.

Strata: `all` 1,095 / 3,207; `Luminal` (LumA + LumB) 696 / 2,436; `Basal`
171 / 317.

### 1.4 The between-subtype test, and why its flag is nearly empty

E10's `$between_test` asks whether a pooled value sits **outside** the range of
its own two strata - the D3/S1 signature, where `BCL2` against MYC was -0.369
pooled and -0.009 inside LumA. E10 ran it for the 35 ratios and never for the
12 genes. Run per gene:

`BCL2L1` is flagged on **7 of 8** cells, and is one of only three genes flagged
in both cohorts on >= 3 rulers (with `BCL2L2` and `BID`).

**And it does not matter, because the flag carries no magnitude.** Adding the
excursion - how far outside the range the pooled value actually sits:

| | largest excursion | comparator |
|---|---|---|
| the 12 genes, this session | **0.053** (`BCL2` / SCAN-B / `ox_ppd`) | |
| `BCL2L1` specifically | **0.005 to 0.043** | |
| E10's motivating D3/S1 case (`BCL2` vs MYC) | | **~0.36** |

Every excursion in the grid is under a *sixth* of the case the test was built
to catch (0.053 against 0.36). The flag is firing because the Luminal and Basal values are close
together, not because the pooled value is reading a between-subtype difference.

**This qualifies a number already in the synthesis.** 3.7 reports that 15 of 35
TCGA ratios and 19 of 35 SCAN-B ratios sit outside the range of their own
Luminal and Basal values. Computed the same way from `$between_test`, the
ratio-level excursions are **also small**: largest 0.091 (`PMAIP1/BCL2A1`, TCGA,
MYC), and the median excursion among flagged cells is **0.007 to 0.042**
depending on cohort and axis. The counts are correct; what they are counting is
mostly narrow stratum ranges. **A flag count is not a between-subtype effect
size, and 3.7 should say so.**

sensitiser catches up; the guardian barely moves. That is a different statement from "the
configuration weakens in Basal", and only the per-gene table separates them.

### 1.6 The branch

> **BRANCH 1: `BCL2L1` holds in both strata. NOT COMPOSITIONAL.**
> Tier 1 survives; the CCLE discrepancy stays unexplained and gets harder.

Stated plainly: **subtype composition does not explain why `BCL2L1` tracks
OXPHOS in tumours.** It tracks it inside Luminal and inside Basal, on every
ruler, in both cohorts, with intervals clear of zero. The pooled value is not an
artefact of mixing two subtypes with different means.

Branch 2 - the damaging one, where the mouse comparison would have belonged in a
stratum the association did not hold in - **did not occur.**

### 4.3 The answer, and it goes against the "below `M*`" argument

The synthesis argues the human cohort sits **below `M*`**, so the human marginal
should correspond to the mouse's slope at low MYC - which is `bX`. The question
was fixed in advance: if `bX(Bcl2l1)` is positive, the human +0.39 is what the
mouse predicts there; if it is zero or negative, the explanation has a problem.

**`bX_wt(Bcl2l1)` = +0.01.** The doc's own words are "a flat `Bcl2l1`".

**At low MYC the mouse says respiration does nothing to `Bcl2l1`.** So the
"human sits below `M*`" argument does not deliver the human association - it
predicts approximately **zero** there, and the human is the strongest positive
in its own configuration. This is not a sign reversal, so it does not falsify
anything; it is a regime that has no room for the number the human arm reports.

**What the two species do agree on, and it is worth keeping.** The *ordering* of
the two guardians against respiration at low MYC is the same in both:
`Bcl2l1` above `Mcl1` in the mouse (+0.01 against -1.00), `BCL2L1` above `MCL1`
in the human (+0.39 against -0.27). **But the ordering is carried by different
limbs** - in the mouse by `Mcl1` falling, in the human by `BCL2L1` rising.
Same order, different mechanism. **No magnitudes are compared across species**;
24 animals against 1,095 tumours is not a comparison of magnitudes, and a
species is a cohort.

## 5. What survives of the reversal claim after this

**The reversal itself is untouched, and so are its two disqualifications.** B4
found the `OX` main effect running opposite to V1's prediction on 8 of 8 breast
and 8 of 8 pan-cancer cells; the addendum showed the `MCL1` leg sits at the
pan-essential floor and the `BCL2L1` leg is null at both sample sizes, and that
the transcript configuration B4 assumed is absent from CCLE. E19 changes none of
that. What E19 changes is **what is left to explain it with.**

Before this session, three compositional explanations were available for why
`BCL2L1` tracks OXPHOS in tumours and not in cell lines: **purity** (closed by
`E16` check 4 - +0.419 to +0.406), **subtype** (open), and a cell-line-specific
account. **Subtype is now closed too**, and closed decisively rather than
weakly: `BCL2L1` holds inside Basal with every interval clear of zero, in the
compartment CCLE is enriched for, and the CCLE basal subset gives the same flat
answer as the CCLE pool. So the discrepancy is not that tumours and cell lines
are measuring different mixtures of the same thing. **They disagree about the
thing.**

And the reconciliation lost its other route at the same time. The "human sits
below `M*`" argument was the way the mouse's gate model and the human's +0.39
could both be true without either being wrong; it required `bX(Bcl2l1)` to be
positive at low MYC, and it is +0.01. **Neither the compositional explanation
nor the below-`M*` explanation survives**, which leaves the tumour `BCL2L1`
association as a real, within-subtype, purity-robust, four-ruler, two-cohort
observation that **no system outside human tumours currently reproduces and no
model in either arm currently predicts.** That is a sharper problem than the one
this session started with, and it is the honest state of it.

**What it is not.** Not evidence against M2, which is a statement about
transcripts in tumours and is now better supported, not worse - it survives
stratification as well as purity adjustment. Not evidence for or against V1,
which remains untested. Not an interaction claim. **N2 outranks all of it.**

## 7. What would change this answer

1. **`E19` sourced, and any number moving.** The dry run and the author's run
   should agree object for object, as `E16`, `E17` and `E18` did.
2. **A stratum finer than Basal.** `Basal` here is PAM50 Basal, which is not
   basal-like in the MMTV-Myc sense and is not homogeneous. `LumA` alone is
   already handoff item 4 and would test the other end.
3. **`Bbc3`'s `bX_wt` in the mouse.** It is one line of the gitignored
   `gene_gate` table. If the mouse arm can print it, the `T` endpoint's
   decomposition at low MYC becomes readable and section 4.3 can be stated on
   the trigger as well as the guardian.
4. **A non-CCLE cell system, or a tumour-derived organoid panel.** The
   discrepancy is now specific enough to be worth one: it is about `BCL2L1`,
   about OXPHOS, and it is not purity, not subtype, and not ruler choice.
