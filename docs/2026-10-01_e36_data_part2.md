# E36, part 2 - what the inputs look like, and the one thing that changes

**Nothing was downloaded. Nothing was written to `myc_human_validation` or
`myc_mouse`** - the frozen `STATE` constructor and E14's comparator object were
read with `readRDS`, the MitoCarta snapshot with `readxl`. **No model was
fitted, no correlation computed, no distribution compared, no control run.**

Everything below is a count, a gene list, a barcode field, or a property of an
estimator. **Every quantity that any reading in the declaration depends on is
still unopened.**

## 1. `M_b` is rebuilt, and the reason is worth more than the decision

The E37 data note deferred `M_b`, noting it is required only by Q2. It is
rebuilt. The interesting part is *why that is cheap*, and it is a fact about the
estimator that this arm has not previously written down.

**decoupleR's ULM is not cohort-relative.** It fits, per sample, a linear model
of that sample's expression across all genes on the regulon's signed membership
vector, and reports the slope's t-statistic. Nothing in that touches another
sample. Tested rather than asserted - ULM run over all 1,208 samples, then over
the 1,095 tumours alone, compared on the 1,095 they share:

| | |
|---|---|
| scores identical | **TRUE** |
| maximum absolute deviation | **0** |
| Pearson r | **1** |

**GSVA is the opposite**, by construction: its `kcdf` is an empirical
distribution taken *across* samples, so every GSVA score depends on which
samples were in the call. That is why `CLAUDE.md` forbids pooling GSVA values
across cohorts, and it is why the joint scoring was required in the first place.

### 1.1 What this buys for Q2, and what it does not

The declaration's Q2 says MYC level "has never been measured: every MYC quantity
in this arm is scored **within** a tumour cohort, and GSVA is cohort-relative by
construction, so 'MYC-low' has carried no information about absolute level."

**That is exactly right for `M_a` and `FELSHER__PROLIFSTRIP`, and it is not true
of `M_b`.** So the three estimators answer Q2 in two different ways, and the
difference is the point rather than a redundancy:

- **`M_a` and `FELSHER__PROLIFSTRIP` (GSVA)** place a normal sample *by rank
  within a pooled set that is 91% tumour*. The normal median is therefore not an
  independent reference point; it is a position in a tumour-dominated
  distribution. **This is the only way to put the two tissues on one scale, and
  it is also a limit on what the comparison means.** It must be stated wherever
  the normal median is used as a threshold.
- **`M_b` (ULM)** is a per-sample statistic and gives a reading that does not
  depend on the mixture at all.

**The caveat that remains for all three.** `M_b` is invariant to the *scoring*
sample set but not to the *normalisation*: the size factors and the VST were
computed over the joint 1,208, so `M_b` here is not comparable with `M_b` in
`data/from_validation/`. **No value from the two snapshots is ever compared**,
as E37 declared.

**All four CollecTRI variants are scored**, as `E05` requires - never one of
them under the bare name `M_b`. Sizes from `results/set_definitions.rds`:
`M_b__FULL` 886, `M_b__MITOSTRIP` 811, `M_b__PROLIFSTRIP` 804, `M_b__BOTHSTRIP`
731. **`M_b__MITOSTRIP` is the construction counterpart of `M_a`** - both are
mito-stripped, and `M_a` *is* `FELSHER__MITOSTRIP` - and it is the variant the
validation study's `M_b` was. **That is a statement about construction, not a
promotion**; all four are reported and none is designated primary.

## 2. Amendment check 1: where the 113 sit, and the 7 that do not

The amendment asks this **before the figure is designed**, and it is reported
whatever it shows. The frozen constructor was re-applied to the joint
snapshot's tumours and **its declared assertions hold again on both
instruments** (Q3 == level 2, Q4 == levels 3 + 4).

| quadrant | GSVA: of the 113 | GSVA: % of that quadrant | mitoPPS: of the 113 | mitoPPS: % of that quadrant |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Q1 (MYC-low, OXPHOS-low) | **39** | 12.0 | **36** | 11.6 |
| Q2 (MYC-low, OXPHOS-high) | **17** | 8.7 | **20** | 9.6 |
| Q3 (MYC-high, OXPHOS-low) | **18** | 9.2 | **23** | 11.0 |
| Q4 (MYC-high, OXPHOS-high) | **32** | 9.8 | **27** | 8.7 |
| **no quadrant** | **7** | 12.7 | **7** | 12.7 |

**No quadrant holds a handful**, so the amendment's degenerate case did not
occur. But the honest reading is narrower than that:

1. **The paired contrast broken down by quadrant runs n = 17 to 39.** It is
   **descriptive**, and the note and figure will say so with the counts printed
   in the panel rather than implied. The **overall paired contrast over the
   pairs is the primary** and is the adequately powered one.
2. **The across-person per-quadrant comparison is a different n.** The
   declaration's Q2 asks for "the percentage of tumours in each quadrant
   exceeding the normal median", which uses the *full* quadrant (325 / 195 / 195
   / 325 on GSVA) against the 113 normals. That one is not thin.
3. **Seven of the 113 have no quadrant**, because `BUFFER_gistic` is missing for
   them - 55 of the 1,095 tumours lack it, and 1,040 are quadranted, which is
   the same 1,040 the gate compared. **So the quadrant-conditioned paired set is
   106 pairs, not 113**, and the unconditioned one is 113. **Both n are reported
   at every step**, as the declaration's mitigation (c) requires.
4. **Normals are never assigned a quadrant, and cannot be.** `STATE` takes
   `BUFFER_gistic`, which is copy number; normals have none. The normals are one
   group compared *against* the quadrants, which is what the declaration
   describes.

The two instruments agree on 86 of the 106 quadranted pairs, and every
disagreement is within a MYC level (Q1<->Q2, Q3<->Q4) - never across one, which
is expected, since the MYC call is instrument-independent by construction.

## 3. Amendment check 2: PAM50 is clean, THE COLLECTION SITES ARE NOT

This is the finding, and it is the reason this note exists rather than the
script going straight in.

**Subtype: no skew.** The 113 against the other 982, PAM50 from
`results/frames.rds`:

| PAM50 | other 982 | paired 113 | % other | % paired | difference |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| LumA | 441 | 58 | 44.9 | 51.3 | +6.4 pp |
| LumB | 176 | 21 | 17.9 | 18.6 | +0.7 pp |
| Basal | 156 | 15 | 15.9 | 13.3 | -2.6 pp |
| HER2 | 69 | 9 | 7.0 | 8.0 | +1.0 pp |
| Normal-like | 34 | 2 | 3.5 | 1.8 | -1.7 pp |
| unclassified | 106 | 8 | 10.8 | 7.1 | -3.7 pp |

Fisher, NA dropped, simulated p = **0.76**. **The paired subset is not a subtype
subset.**

**Collection site: a severe skew.** Tissue source site, barcode positions 6-7:

| | |
|---|---|
| distinct sites in the 1,095 tumours | **40** |
| sites contributing at least one normal | **6** (`BH`, `E9`, `E2`, `A7`, `AC`, `GI`) |
| share of the 113 normals from the top 3 sites | **87.6%** |
| share of the other 982 tumours from those same 3 sites | **20.8%** |
| Fisher, simulated | **p = 1e-05** |

**Site `BH` alone contributes 73 of the 113 normals**, against 78 of the other
982 tumours - so `BH` is very nearly the paired site, and one site supplies
nearly two thirds of the normal group.

**Why this matters for exactly one of the two questions.** TCGA site is a batch
variable, and it travels with collection, fixation and handling.

- **Every within-patient contrast is immune to it by construction**, because a
  pair is one patient at one site. **That covers Q2's amended primary**, which is
  the tumour-minus-own-normal difference over the 113 pairs. **This is the second
  time the pairing has turned out to be a gift rather than a nuisance.**
- **Q1 is not immune.** It compares a correlation computed in 113 normals that
  are 88% three-site with a correlation computed in 1,095 tumours drawn from 40
  sites. A difference between those two could be site.

## 4. The consequence, declared now: three comparators, none promoted

**Forced by section 3, and fixed before any Q1 or Q2 value exists.** Wherever
E36 compares normals with tumours *across people* - Q1's two correlations, and
Q2's "percentage exceeding the normal median" - **the tumour side is computed
three ways and all three are reported**:

| comparator | n | what it controls | what it costs |
|---|---|---|---|
| **all tumours** | **1,095** | nothing; it is the basis E34 and E35 used, so it is the comparable one | confounded with site |
| **site-matched** | **394** | restricted to the 6 sites that contributed a normal, so the two groups are drawn from the same places | still not the same *proportions*; `BH` is 38% of these tumours and 65% of the normals |
| **patient-matched** | **113** | the tightest - identical patients, hence identical sites, ages and genotypes | the tumour interval becomes as wide as the normal one |

**All three are reported in the same table, always.** A reading is taken only if
it holds in all three, and **if they disagree, the disagreement is the result**
and is reported as such rather than resolved by choosing one.

**This is not a new analysis and it is not a robustness check bolted on after a
disappointment.** It is the same estimand on three nested tumour sets, declared
before any of the three has been computed, precisely so that **"the site-matched
one is the real answer" cannot become available after the fact.** The failure
mode this guards against is the one E35's brief named: promoting whichever is
cleanest afterwards.

**What it cannot fix.** Site-matching cannot remove site, because the normals
are concentrated inside the matched set too. **If all three comparators agree,
site is an unlikely explanation; if the full and the site-matched disagree, E36
cannot separate site from tissue** and must say so. That limitation is recorded
here, before the numbers, and belongs in the result whatever happens.

### 5.1 How the control is constructed, fixed now

**The control substitutes its cytosolic half for the configuration composite and
asks Q1 again, unchanged.** Same exposure, same covariates, same three
comparators, same intervals.

- **The composite is unsigned** - mean per-gene z on `log2(linear + 1)`, every
  gene entering `+1`. The configuration composite is *signed* (`-MCL1`), and the
  control has no signs to carry. **The difference is recorded because it makes
  the control slightly conservative**: an unsigned mean over a co-regulated set
  has less internal cancellation than a signed one.
- **The cytosolic half is the control.** The mitochondrial half is reported
  beside it and is **not** a control - it is mitochondrial, so coupling to OXPHOS
  is close to guaranteed, exactly as `E34`'s two OXPHOS contrasts were
  near-guaranteed by construction. It is there to show the instrument responds.
- **The stop condition is on the declared primary**, Fe-S cytosolic, as the
  first data note fixed. Mitophagy's two versions are reported alongside and
  **cannot be promoted into the primary's place.**

**The disclosure, now checked against E14's object rather than quoted from
memory.** Fe-S cytosolic half against OXPHOS, in tumours: **TCGA +0.152 raw,
+0.118 proliferation-adjusted; SCAN-B +0.214 raw, +0.159 adjusted.** So the
cytosolic half is **not** independent of OXPHOS in tumours - it is weakly
positive and it is known to be. **The control asks whether that weak coupling
*changes between normal and tumour as much as the configuration's does*, not
whether it is zero.** Writing that down now matters, because a control that is
already non-zero is easy to misread after the fact in either direction.

## 6. Two instruments, as everywhere else in this arm

**OXPHOS enters on both GSVA and mitoPPS**, as `E34` and `E35` did and as
`CLAUDE.md`'s trap 5 requires. Both are in `results/joint_tcga_scores.rds` on
their correct opposite scales. **mitoPPS values are never compared numerically
between the tissue types** - trap 6 - only the within-tissue correlations and
their contrast, which is what Q1 is made of anyway.

## 7. One thing E37 did not leave behind

**`results/e37_gate.rds` does not persist the per-sample quadrant.** It holds the
gate, its legs and its audit, and nothing sample-level beyond `sample_map`. **So
E36 rebuilds the quadrant** from the frozen constructor, the same call in the
same order, **and re-asserts the two declared assertions.** That is duplication,
and it is the good kind: the assertions become a precondition of E36's own run
rather than something inherited from a previous script's say-so.
