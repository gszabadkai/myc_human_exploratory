# E44 - the respiration-proliferation contrast, tested

**Option A: Claude Code wrote the script, the author sourced it.** Nothing was
refitted, nothing was rescored, and nothing was written to
`myc_human_validation` (`d3ac60e`) or `myc_mouse`.

## 1. Verification

Six redirected dry runs; the final version ran twice and agreed on **9 of 9**
non-timestamp elements; the digests went into `6533a1f` **before** the author's
run; the author's run matched **9 of 9**.

**Every component coefficient and SE extracted from the fitted objects equalled
E43's stored value with a discrepancy of exactly 0, in all eight fits.** That
was a hard stop, so the contrast is built on the coefficients E43 reported
rather than on a re-derivation of them.

**This was computable only because declaration 14.7 required E43 to save the
fitted objects.** The contrast needs `Cov(bP, bO)`, and only the fitted object
carries the variance matrix - a coefficient table cannot supply it at any
precision. Under the extraction E40 and E41 used, E44 would have required a
third refit of the same eight models.

## 2. The contrast, both endpoints

`delta = beta(PROLIF) - beta(OX)`, from the same m1 fit, with
`Var(delta) = Var(bP) + Var(bO) - 2*Cov(bP, bO)`.

### 2.1 pCR - log odds ratio

| cohort | n | events | OX | PROLIF | delta | 95% CI | p |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| GSE25066 | 465 | 93 | -0.0507 | +0.8246 | **+0.8753** | [0.3636, 1.3870] | 8.0e-04 |
| GSE194040 | 988 | 319 | -0.1731 | +0.3675 | **+0.5407** | [0.2434, 0.8379] | 3.7e-04 |
| GSE164458 | 482 | 236 | -0.2053 | +0.5242 | **+0.7296** | [0.3482, 1.1109] | 1.8e-04 |
| **pooled** | | | | | **+0.6577** | **[0.4446, 0.8709]** | **1.5e-09** |

Fixed effect is the summary: **I2 = 0.0, Q p 0.49, signs + + +**. Exponentiated,
the pooled delta is a **ratio of odds ratios** of **1.930 [1.560, 2.389]**.

**The pooled delta is the meta-analysis OF THE PER-COHORT DELTAS**, each already
covariance-corrected (15.4). It is not the difference of two pooled estimates -
see section 6.

### 2.2 METABRIC breast-cancer-specific survival - log hazard ratio

| set | n | events | OX | PROLIF | delta | 95% CI | excl 0 | p |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **ER-pos (IHC)**, the primary | 1,505 | 431 | -0.1096 | +0.2064 | **+0.3160** | **[0.1180, 0.5140]** | **yes** | 0.0018 |
| pooled ladder | 1,981 | 623 | -0.0486 | +0.1254 | **+0.1740** | **[0.0051, 0.3429]** | **yes** | 0.043 |
| ER-pos (`ER.Expr`) | 1,512 | 432 | -0.0734 | +0.1666 | **+0.2401** | **[0.0382, 0.4419]** | **yes** | 0.020 |
| ER-neg (IHC) | 435 | 183 | +0.0648 | -0.0376 | -0.1024 | [-0.4283, 0.2236] | no | 0.54 |
| ER-neg (`ER.Expr`) | 469 | 191 | +0.0095 | +0.0495 | +0.0401 | [-0.2636, 0.3438] | no | 0.80 |

The ER-negative strata are uninformative, which is what 15.4 said to expect of
them in both terms.

**The two endpoints are NOT combined, pooled or compared as values** (15.4,
4.2). A pCR delta exponentiates to a ratio of odds ratios and a METABRIC delta
to a ratio of hazard ratios; they are never set side by side. **The scale label
is asserted against each fitted object's class** - `glm` must carry log odds,
`coxph` log hazard - so a log-odds delta cannot be reported as a log-hazard one
(section 7).

## 3. The naive calculation would have been anti-conservative in every cell

`Cov(bP, bO)` is **negative in all eight fits**, with coefficient correlations
from **-0.512 to -0.312**. So `-2*Cov` is positive, the correct variance is
larger than the naive sum, and the correct SE runs **14.3% to 22.9% larger**
than `sqrt(seP^2 + seO^2)`.

**15.7 predicted this before the computation** - positively correlated
predictors usually give negatively correlated coefficient estimates - and it
held **8 of 8**.

**The size of the error is material, not cosmetic**, on exactly the cells the
Results depend on:

| set | correct CI | correct p | naive CI | naive p |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| ER-pos (IHC) | [0.1180, 0.5140] | 0.00176 | [0.1427, 0.4893] | **0.000352** |
| pooled ladder | [0.0051, 0.3429] | 0.0434 | [0.0278, 0.3202] | **0.0196** |
| ER-pos (`ER.Expr`) | [0.0382, 0.4419] | 0.0197 | [0.0647, 0.4155] | **0.00731** |

The quick calculation would have reported a p value **five times smaller** in
the primary stratum and **less than half** the correct one in the pooled ladder.
**The naive SE is reported beside the correct one for comparison only and is
never the test statistic** (15.5).

## 4. The two criteria, side by side - and why the test is not a demotion

15.7 recorded before the computation that the eyeball claim behind section 4 is
not universally true. Both criteria, computed:

| set | intervals disjoint | gap | delta excludes 0 | agree |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| GSE25066 | yes | 0.2695 | yes | yes |
| GSE194040 | yes | 0.1928 | yes | yes |
| GSE164458 | yes | 0.2909 | yes | yes |
| ER-pos (IHC) | yes | 0.0722 | yes | yes |
| **pooled ladder** | **NO** | **-0.0308** | **yes** | **no** |
| **ER-pos (`ER.Expr`)** | **NO** | **-0.0064** | **yes** | **no** |
| ER-neg (IHC) | no | -0.2875 | no | yes |
| ER-neg (`ER.Expr`) | no | -0.3275 | no | yes |

**Non-overlap is the stricter criterion**, so the two disagreements are in the
expected direction - overlapping intervals whose difference still excludes zero.
A disagreement the other way would have needed explaining; there is none.

**So E44 does not take anything away from section 4. It replaces the basis.**
The pooled ladder and the `ER.Expr` sensitivity are precisely the two
comparators 15.4 requires beside the primary, and **the eyeball claim fails in
both while the tested contrast holds in both.** The corrected statement is
therefore stronger as well as more accurate.

## 5. What this licenses - the replacement wording

### 5.1 Licensed

**15.6 row 1**: *the two coefficients differ, tested, on both endpoints.* The
two sentences in section 4's draft that rested on interval overlap are
superseded:

> **On pCR.** The draft's *"their intervals do not overlap"* **is true** - all
> three cohorts are disjoint - but it is superseded by the computed difference:
> **pooled +0.66 log odds [0.44, 0.87], a ratio of odds ratios of 1.93
> [1.56, 2.39], I2 = 0, signs agreeing in all three cohorts.**
>
> **On distant outcome.** The draft's *"the two intervals are again disjoint"*
> **must not be written**: it is true of the ER-positive IHC stratum and of
> nothing else on that endpoint, failing in both declared comparators. What may
> be written is the tested difference: **+0.32 log hazard [0.12, 0.51] in the
> ER-positive stratum, holding in the pooled ladder (+0.17 [0.01, 0.34]) and in
> the `ER.Expr` sensitivity (+0.24 [0.04, 0.44]).**

**The rest of section 4's draft needs no change.** Its closing move - *"we
therefore report no association between respiration and distant outcome, and
read the contrast with proliferation rather than the coefficient itself"* - is
exactly what 15.3 requires.

**Also licensed**: that the covariance-corrected standard error is the right one
and the naive version anti-conservative in all eight fits (section 3). Any
methods sentence describing this contrast must say the covariance was used.

### 5.2 NOT licensed - 15.3, which governs every row of 15.6

**A significant contrast does not make either coefficient a finding.** The
contrast excludes zero while **the OX coefficient alone remains exactly as weak
as E39/E40/E41 section 7.2 records it** - one boundary cell of thirty,
unreproduced by its own sensitivity, with the adjacent rungs including the null.
**E44 changes that by nothing.**

**Nothing in E44 may be cited in support of a distant-outcome claim for
respiration.** That prohibition is unchanged and is not relaxed by a contrast
against a different term. **What E44 licenses is that the two coefficients
DIFFER, not that either is established.**

Also not licensed:

- **No claim from the ER-negative strata**, in either direction.
- **No comparison of a pCR delta with a METABRIC delta as values** (15.4, 4.2).
- **Nothing about m0, m2 or m3.** Only m1 was contrasted (14.4, 15.4).
- **Nothing about GSE25066 DRFS**, excluded by 15.4 and declared rather than
  left silent.
- **No p value described as a near miss**, which forbids reading the pooled
  ladder's 0.043 as marginal as much as it forbids inflating it.

## 6. The calculation 15.4 forbids, computed once and shown as forbidden

The difference of the two separately pooled pCR estimates is **0.6566**, against
the correct pooled delta of **0.6577**. The two agree to three decimal places
here, **and that is luck rather than licence**: the forbidden version **has no
valid standard error at all**, because the two pooled estimates come from
overlapping patients and their covariance is not recoverable from pooled
summaries. It is carried in the saved object as
`$diff_of_pooled_forbidden` with that reason attached, and is **not an
estimate**. It appears so that nobody reaches for it on seeing how close it
lands.

## 7. One defect in the script, found by the author's run and fixed harmlessly

The run emitted one warning, `Unknown or uninitialised column: 'family'`.
`e43$model_info` carries `set`, `n`, `events`, `omitted`, `terms` and no
`family`, so the line reading it was a no-op whose only effect was the warning.
**The `scale` label was never derived from it** - it comes from the cohort name -
so **no reported number was affected**, and removing the dead lines left the
saved object **byte-identical** (9 of 9 unchanged), so `6533a1f`'s digests and
the verified run both still hold.

But deriving the scale from a hard-coded cohort list was the fragility the dead
line had been meant to remove. **It is now asserted against each fitted
object's class**, so `glm` must carry "log odds ratio" and `coxph` "log hazard
ratio" or the script stops. Mislabelling one would report a log-odds delta as a
log-hazard one, which is the confusion 15.4 exists to prevent. Saved as
`$scale_check`, appended as a tenth element. Commit `3c17de2`.

## 8. Limitations

1. **A contrast inherits every limitation of its components.** These are E43's
   coefficients from E40's and E41's models; their spines, coverage caveats,
   endpoint definitions and the OX boundary-cell caveat all apply unchanged.
2. **The reading is post-hoc** (15.2), replacing a descriptive statement rather
   than testing a declared hypothesis.
3. **Nothing here addresses whether either coefficient is correct** - only
   whether the two differ. A shared bias affecting both would cancel in the
   difference and be invisible to this test.
4. **The distant-outcome contrast rests on ER-positive disease.** Both
   ER-negative strata are uninformative, so there is no second stratum on that
   endpoint where the difference is demonstrable.
5. **GSE25066 DRFS is absent**, by declaration (15.4), so the distant-outcome
   contrast has one cohort and not two.
6. **The pCR heterogeneity in PROLIF does not propagate to the delta.** PROLIF
   alone pooled at I2 66.9; the delta pools at I2 0.0 with Q p 0.49. That is
   worth noting rather than assuming: the cohorts disagree about the size of
   the proliferation coefficient and agree about the size of the gap.
