# E36 - the question is not answered, and the reason is itself the finding

**EXPLORATORY.** Nothing pre-registered. **N3**: these are transcript scores;
nothing is "primed", in either tissue. **No outcome, survival or treatment
variable was read or joined. No product term. No per-gene FDR.** Nothing was
written to `myc_human_validation` or `myc_mouse`.

**This reads the JOINT snapshot built by E37 and nothing else.** No value here
is comparable with a value from `data/from_validation/`, so E34's and E35's
numbers are not a comparator for anything below.

---

## 1. The verdict

> **UNINTERPRETABLE.** The declared positive control - the Fe-S cluster
> assembly **cytosolic** half - changes between normal and tumour by at least
> half of the configuration's change, with a difference interval excluding
> zero, in **6 of 12** declared-adjustment cells. **The analysis is measuring
> composition and the apoptotic result is uninterpretable. No reading is
> taken.**

This is the stop condition the declaration set in section 2(b), fixed before
any fit: *"If it differs as much as apoptosis does, the analysis is measuring
composition and the apoptotic result is uninterpretable. This is a stop
condition, not a footnote."* **The mitigation worked. It is doing exactly the
job it was declared to do, and the right response is to report that, not to
work around it.**

**The verdict does not rest on the control alone.** Independently, **all six
cells are SPLIT BY ADJUSTMENT** - the unadjusted and the EPITHELIAL-adjusted
readings disagree in every single one - so the reading rule yields no reading by
a second route that never touches the control or its threshold.

## 2. What the control did, next to what the configuration did

Unadjusted, comparator `all`, both instruments:

| instrument | readout | normal (n = 113) | tumour (n = 1,095) | difference |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| GSVA | **configuration** | **-0.011** | **+0.548** | **+0.560** |
| GSVA | **Fe-S cytosolic (CONTROL)** | **-0.120** | **+0.323** | **+0.443** |
| mitoPPS | **configuration** | +0.107 | +0.441 | +0.333 |
| mitoPPS | **Fe-S cytosolic (CONTROL)** | -0.013 | +0.263 | +0.276 |

**A housekeeping cytosolic iron-sulfur assembly composite, with zero genes in
common with either the configuration or the OXPHOS arm, reproduces 69% to 96%
of the configuration's change.** Ratios across the six firing cells run 0.69,
0.72, 0.74, 0.79, 0.83 and 0.96. **Whatever changes between normal breast and
tumour here is not specific to the apoptotic configuration.**

## 3. Why - and this is the part worth keeping

**In normal breast, almost everything correlates with almost everything.**
Unadjusted, the five companion composites against OXPHOS:

| | normals | tumours |
|---|---|---|
| range over the five companions | **+0.729 to +0.902** | +0.445 to +0.735 |

Individually, in normals on GSVA: Fe-S mitochondrial **+0.902**, mitophagy
cytosolic **+0.877**, PINK1/PRKN cytosolic **+0.835**, mitophagy mitochondrial
**+0.819**, PINK1/PRKN mitochondrial **+0.815**.

**That is what a bulk tissue looks like when its variance is dominated by a
single composition axis.** Normal breast is mostly adipose and stroma with a
variable minority of epithelium; a sample with more epithelium has more of
everything, so every unsigned composite rides the same axis together.

**This reframes the configuration's own normal-side value.** `-0.011` is not a
low correlation in a quiet tissue. It is a **near-zero value in a tissue where
every unsigned composite sits far above it.** The configuration is the one
composite that does *not* ride the composition axis - and it is also the only
**signed** one, carrying `-MCL1`.

**Whether the signed construction is what cancels the common component is NOT
established here.** It is a hypothesis this analysis generated and cannot test,
and it is written down so the next analysis can.

## 4. The declared adjustment failed its own validity check

The amendment required `EPITHELIAL` to be *"strongly higher in tumours, and
strongly anti-correlated with the stromal score"*. **It is neither.**

| score | median normal | median tumour | AUC (tumour higher) | rho with EPITHELIAL |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **EPITHELIAL (declared)** | 0.213 | 0.081 | **0.445** | 1.000 |
| ADIPOSE (companion) | 1.721 | -0.164 | **0.054** | +0.077 |
| FIBROBLAST (companion) | -0.569 | 0.179 | **0.757** | +0.031 |

0.5 is no separation. **The declared composition estimate barely separates the
two tissues, and is not anti-correlated with either stromal score.**

**The diagnostic says exactly why**, and it was not foreseeable from the gene
list alone:

| half | AUC (tumour higher) |
|---|---|
| luminal (`EPCAM`, `CDH1`, `KRT8`, `KRT18`, `KRT19`) | **0.875** |
| basal / myoepithelial (`KRT5`, `KRT14`, `KRT17`, `TP63`) | **0.134** |
| **both (9), as declared** | **0.445** |

Per gene: luminal 0.612 to 0.879, basal 0.123 to 0.200. **Normal breast has a
myoepithelial layer that tumours lack, so the two halves separate the tissues in
opposite directions and very nearly cancel.**

**The amendment's reasoning for including both compartments was sound and the
outcome is still a failure.** It argued that a luminal-only score *"would be
part epithelial-content estimate and part subtype score, and would then adjust
away some of the thing being measured"*. That remains true. What it could not
know is that the basal half would cancel the luminal half almost exactly.

**The gene list was NOT changed and no substitute was introduced.** Swapping in
a different covariate after seeing this is precisely what fixing the list in
advance exists to prevent. The consequence is recorded instead: **the
EPITHELIAL-adjusted columns may not be read as "composition-adjusted"** - they
are adjusted for a score that does not track composition. Note also that the
adjustment **moves the normal correlation from -0.011 to +0.326**, which is a
further reason not to read it as a composition control.

**And the companions settle that composition itself is not the problem.**
Adipose AUC 0.054 and fibroblast 0.757 - **composition differs enormously
between the two tissues.** It is the declared estimate of it that fails to
capture the difference.

## 6. Q2 STANDS, and it is the substantive result

**The stop condition binds Q1 only.** Q2 is a within-patient difference in MYC
activity: it uses no configuration, no control and no adjustment, and it is
immune to the collection-site skew by construction, each pair being one patient
at one site.

### 6.1 Overall, over the 113 pairs

| estimator | median difference | IQR | % of pairs where tumour > its own normal |
|---|---|---|---|
| `M_a` (GSVA) | +0.237 | [-0.03, 0.68] | **69.9% [60.9, 77.6]** |
| `FELSHER__PROLIFSTRIP` (GSVA) | +0.244 | [-0.09, 0.64] | **69.0% [60.0, 76.8]** |
| `M_b__FULL` (ULM) | +0.474 | [-0.28, 1.38] | 66.4% [57.3, 74.4] |
| `M_b__MITOSTRIP` (ULM) | +0.373 | [-0.38, 1.46] | 64.6% [55.4, 72.8] |
| `M_b__PROLIFSTRIP` (ULM) | **-0.306** | [-0.87, 0.35] | **39.8% [31.3, 49.0]** |
| `M_b__BOTHSTRIP` (ULM) | **-0.393** | [-1.05, 0.39] | **39.8% [31.3, 49.0]** |

**The two proliferation-stripped CollecTRI regulons reverse the sign.** On
those, the tumour has *lower* MYC regulon activity than its own matched normal
in 60% of pairs. **This is `CLAUDE.md`'s trap 3 appearing in a new place**:
the tumour-versus-own-normal MYC difference is substantially proliferation, and
how much survives stripping depends on which estimator does the stripping - the
GSVA proliferation-stripped Felsher signature still gives 69.0%, the ULM one
gives 39.8%. **Both are reported; neither is promoted; the disagreement is the
finding.**

### 6.2 By quadrant - and this is what bears on the premise

Within-patient difference, GSVA quadrant, **106 of the 113 pairs** carry one:

| quadrant | n pairs | `M_a` median diff | % tumour higher | `M_b__MITOSTRIP` % higher |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Q1 MYC-low OXPHOS-low** | **39** | **-0.003** | **48.7%** | 51.3% |
| **Q2 MYC-low OXPHOS-high** | **17** | **-0.075** | **47.1%** | 35.3% |
| Q3 MYC-high OXPHOS-low | 18 | +0.721 | 100.0% | 94.4% |
| Q4 MYC-high OXPHOS-high | 32 | +0.708 | 90.6% | 81.2% |

**MYC-low tumours have no more MYC activity than their own matched normal
tissue.** Q1 and Q2 sit at a coin flip. MYC-high tumours clearly exceed theirs.

**The declaration said this must be written if it occurred**, in section 4: *"If
a substantial fraction of Q1 and Q2 tumours sit at or below the normal median,
the threshold reconciliation fails and that must be written."* On the amended
primary - the within-patient form - **about half of them do, and the threshold
reconciliation fails for MYC-low tumours.**

**The unpaired form disagrees, and both are reported.** Against the normal
*median* rather than each tumour's own normal, Q1 is 67.1% above and Q2 88.2%
above (comparator `all`, GSVA; 61.2% and 85.5% site-matched). **The paired form
is the better-controlled of the two** - it holds patient, site, age and genotype
constant - and it is the one the amendment made primary, before any value
existed. The divergence between the two forms is itself worth recording.

**n = 17 to 39 per quadrant. This is DESCRIPTIVE**, as data note 2 fixed in
advance, and the counts are in the table and on the figure rather than implied.

## 7. What this licenses, and what it does not

**Q1 licenses NOTHING.** Not ABSENT, not WEAKER, not PRESENT. The manuscript may
not say the coupling is absent in normal breast, nor that it is present, nor
that human normal tissue does or does not corroborate the mouse wild-type gland
result. **The question remains open and this analysis did not close it.**

**It does licence one methodological sentence**, which is worth more than it
looks: *a normal-versus-tumour comparison of any mitochondrial-to-cytosolic
relationship in bulk TCGA breast is dominated by tissue composition, to the
extent that a housekeeping control reproduces most of the apparent effect.*

**Q2 licenses two descriptive sentences**, both qualified:
1. Over 113 matched pairs, tumour MYC activity exceeds its own normal in about
   two thirds of patients on the mito-stripped estimators - **but the
   proliferation-stripped CollecTRI regulons reverse this**, so the statement is
   estimator-dependent and must be made with the panel, never from one.
2. **The excess is confined to MYC-high tumours.** Q1 and Q2 tumours do not
   exceed their own normals. **This qualifies the standing reconciliation**
   that the human comparison lies entirely above the condition the mouse
   manipulates: for MYC-low tumours, on this evidence, it does not.

**Nothing here concerns killing, treatment selection or outcome**, and no causal
language is licensed by any of it.

## 8. What would make Q1 answerable

Written now, before the next analysis, as `CLAUDE.md` requires:

1. **A composition estimate that actually works in both tissues.** Not a
   substitution into this analysis - a separately declared one. The diagnostic
   in section 4 says what it has to do: separate the tissues, and not be built
   from markers that cancel. The adipose and fibroblast scores already separate
   them; whether either is usable as an *adjustment* rather than a description
   is a question that needs declaring, not assuming.
2. **A signed control.** Every control here is unsigned and the readout is
   signed, and section 3 raises the possibility that the sign is what decides
   whether a composite rides the composition axis. **A signed control -
   matched arbitrary genes with matched signs - would separate "signed" from
   "apoptotic" and no analysis in this repo has one.**
3. **Purified or single-cell normal breast**, where composition is not the
   dominant axis. Bulk field-adjacent tissue may simply not be able to answer
   this question.
4. **A second cohort with normals.** SCAN-B has none, so nothing here is
   replicated.

## 9. The limits, restated

- **Field-adjacent tissue, not normal breast** - all 113 from breasts that grew
  a tumour, and every one of those tumours is in the same analysis.
- **n = 113, unreplicated**, from **6 of 40 collection sites**, 87.6% from three.
- **Cross-sectional, bulk, transcript-level.** The configuration is not
  lethality and nothing is "primed".
- **The control is pre-specified but NOT blind** - E14's numbers for it were on
  record and had been read. What advance naming bought is that one of the three
  could not be promoted after the fact; it did not buy ignorance.
- **INDETERMINATE is a fifth reading not in the declaration**, added in the
  script before any value existed because the declaration's own mitigation (c)
  says a wide interval around zero is not evidence of absence. It never fired.
