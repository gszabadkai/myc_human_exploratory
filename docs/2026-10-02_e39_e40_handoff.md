### 4.9 What the declared reading rules say, stated as fact

Section 9: *"A coefficient is read as a signal only where its CI excludes zero
AND, for the three-cohort pCR analysis, the sign agrees across all three
cohorts."*

- **m0 fails both conditions.** Signs `+ - +`; pooled CI includes zero. The only
  cohort-level m0 coefficient whose CI excludes zero is **GSE25066 at +0.270**,
  the smallest pCR cohort, disagreeing in sign with the largest. **That is the
  shape of the Block F1 precedent section 9 cites.**
- **m1, m2 and m3 meet both.** Signs all negative, I2 = 0, pooled CI excludes
  zero at each.
- **m0's I2 is 48.8, just under the declared 50% threshold**, so the fixed
  effect was labelled the summary. Correct per the rule, and close enough that
  the note should give the number and not only the label.

**Every VIF for OX is between 1.00 and 1.47.** None approaches the caveat
threshold of 5 that 5.3 set. **The MYC-proliferation collinearity did not reach
the exposure**, which is what 5.3's "declared IN" question asked.
