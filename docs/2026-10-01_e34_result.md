## 1. Two things that bound every number below

Both were written down before any readout existed, and both survive whatever the
verdict is.

### 1.1 The primary readout is not "as E11 defines it", because E11 defines nothing of the kind

The declaration names the primary as "the configuration composite, as E11
defines it". **`E11` builds no per-sample composite** - it works gene by gene
over 44 machinery genes, and the figures the declaration quotes are summaries of
per-gene correlations, not scores. The author was asked before any data was read
and chose **E33's signed composite, verbatim**: mean z of `BBC3`, `BID`, `BIK`,
`BAD`, `BCL2L1` and **minus** `MCL1`, on log2(linear + 1), z across the whole
cohort. Data note 1.1. **The divergence is stated here, first, as that note
required.**

### 1.2 THE CIRCULARITY - the OXPHOS contrasts confirm almost nothing

**The configuration's genes and their signs were read off OXPHOS correlations in
these same two cohorts** (reconciliation doc 3.1, "ordered by the cross-cohort
mean on OXPHOS"). A composite built from the genes most positively and most
negatively associated with OXPHOS separates OXPHOS-high from OXPHOS-low tumours
**largely by construction**.

**So the two OXPHOS contrasts being large and positive is close to guaranteed
and is not evidence of anything.** They are reported because the rule needs
them, and the figure shows them because they set the scale against which the
MYC contrasts are read. **The informative contrasts are the two MYC ones**,
`Q4 - Q2` and `Q3 - Q1`, whose size nothing forced.

**Both fail in the same place, and it is not a sign reversal.** In each, the
OXPHOS contrast at MYC-low (`Q2 - Q1`) has an interval that **includes zero** -
+0.340 [-0.067, +0.747] and +0.109 [-0.326, +0.544]. That breaks
OXPHOS-ORDERED's requirement that both OXPHOS contrasts exclude zero.

- **TCGA LumB** then just misses MYC-DEPENDENT too: it needs `Q4 - Q2` >= the
  smaller OXPHOS contrast, and +0.327 is a whisker under +0.340. Hence
  **neither**.
- **SCAN-B HER2** clears it, because the smaller OXPHOS contrast is +0.109 -
  a number whose interval spans [-0.326, +0.544]. **MYC-DEPENDENT fires there
  against a comparator that is indistinguishable from zero.**

**These are precision failures in small within-subtype cells, not disagreements
about direction.** That is a description of where the rule broke, **not a reason
to set it aside**. The rule was fixed before any number existed and it returns
MIXED.

**Three readings, and the first is the one that matters.**

   cohorts, two instruments, all within 0.016 of each other. The mandatory
   companion was included because proliferation usually *inflates* a MYC
   association; here removing it *uncovers* a slightly larger one. **Reported as
   observed; no mechanism is claimed.**

## 9. What this licenses, and what it does not

**Nothing here licenses a treatment-selection claim.** No cohort available
carries an OXPHOS-directed or BH3-directed intervention. The declaration says
this of both verdicts in advance, and MIXED does not change it.

### 9.1 NOT licensed

- **The declaration's OXPHOS-ORDERED reading.** It was conditional on that
  verdict and the rule did not return it. **The sentence "the human
  transcriptome gives no support to the claim that the standing pro-apoptotic
  state is MYC-restricted" is not licensed by this analysis as written.**
- **MYC-DEPENDENT, emphatically.** One cell of 18, against a comparator whose
  interval spans zero.
- **Any claim that the MYC contrast is zero.** It is small, mostly positive, and
  several intervals exclude zero.
- **Any interaction claim.** No product term was fitted, deliberately. That the
  two MYC contrasts are similar is a statement about four group means.
- **Any causal reading**, and anything prognostic. No outcome variable entered.

### 9.2 Licensed - description, and the figure

The four-group description does not depend on the reading rule and is what the
declaration called new:

> In both cohorts the apoptotic configuration separates by OXPHOS status and
> barely by MYC status: MYC-low, OXPHOS-high tumours (n = 167 TCGA, 546 SCAN-B
> after adjustment) carry it at a level close to MYC-high, OXPHOS-high tumours
> (adjusted means +0.41 against +0.40 in TCGA, +0.24 against +0.37 in SCAN-B),
> while both OXPHOS-low groups sit well below. The difference attributable to
> MYC status at fixed OXPHOS status is between 0.00 and 0.23 SD, against 0.40 to
> 0.98 SD for OXPHOS status at fixed MYC status (PAM50-, purity- and
> leukocyte-adjusted; adding proliferation raises the MYC range to 0.35 SD).

**Three qualifications that must travel with it:**

1. **The OXPHOS contrasts are near-guaranteed by construction** (section 1.2).
   The claim rests on the smallness of the MYC contrasts, not the largeness of
   the OXPHOS ones.
2. **The verdict was MIXED.** The description above is licensed as description;
   the pre-written interpretation of OXPHOS-ORDERED is not.
3. **Transcript abundance is not a functional readout.** The sibling study's
   null on functional BH3 assays already established that, and it bounds
   anything said here.

### 9.3 The Discussion, stated plainly

The therapeutic argument's premise - that the standing pro-apoptotic state is a
property of MYC-driven tumours - **is not supported by these data, and is not
refuted by them either.** What the data show is that the configuration tracks
OXPHOS status, with a small additional MYC contribution that is present in most
cells and larger after proliferation adjustment. **If the manuscript keeps the
MYC-restriction claim, it rests on the mouse, where MYC-dependence is
demonstrated by intervention, and the Discussion should say so explicitly.**

## 10. What would change this answer

1. **Precision in the within-subtype cells.** Both dissents are cells where an
   OXPHOS contrast failed to exclude zero (TCGA LumB n = 195; SCAN-B HER2
   n = 278). A larger cohort, or METABRIC, would resolve them one way or the
   other without touching the rule.
2. **A different composite.** The primary is E33's signed composite, chosen
   because E11 defines none. A composite built on genes **not** selected on
   OXPHOS in these cohorts would remove the circularity and is the single most
   valuable follow-up.
3. **The instrument split.** Every dissent is on mitoPPS, and the magnitude
   ratio orders GSVA below mitoPPS in 16 of 18 cells. CLAUDE.md trap 5 says
   instrument choice is not cosmetic; here it decides the verdict.
4. **Basal.** The Q2 cell is nearly empty in Basal tumours in both cohorts
   (min cell 3 and 5), so the claim is effectively about luminal and HER2
   disease. A Basal-enriched cohort would test it where it is currently untested.

**A note for the design of the next rule, and not a reason to revisit this
one.** Requiring unanimity across 18 cells, each read through a 95% interval,
makes MIXED the likely outcome even when the pattern is consistent - as here,
where 16 of 18 agree and the two that do not are the least precise. **The rule
was fixed before any data and it is applied as written.** Any future rule of
this shape should state in advance how many cells may dissent.
