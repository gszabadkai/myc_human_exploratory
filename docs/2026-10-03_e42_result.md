# E42 - does the pCR finding hold across subtypes?

**Option A: Claude Code wrote the script, the author sourced it.** Nothing was
written to `myc_human_validation`, frozen at `d3ac60e`, or to `myc_mouse`;
`h4_outcome_models.rds` was read read-only.

---

## 1. What this bounds, and what it could not do

E40's three-cohort `m1` is the arm's primary finding: pooled log-odds
**-0.162 [-0.281, -0.042]**, I2 = 0.0%, signs agreeing in all three cohorts.
That estimate is **pooled within each cohort across subtypes**, and the model's
assumption of one common OX effect inside every subtype **had never been
tested**. E42 tests it.

**The four prohibitions held and are restated because the result makes them
load-bearing** (13.2):

1. **It cannot promote or demote the primary finding.** E40's `m1` stands
   whatever the strata show.
2. **No subtype result is a finding in its own right.** The strongest stratum
   below is a stratum of a pooled estimate, not a discovery.
3. **No interaction term and no effect-modification test.** None was fitted.
4. **METABRIC was not touched.** The PAM50 split there is `X01`.

## 5. THE READING RULE, APPLIED MECHANICALLY - and where it ran out

### 5.1 Row 3 is not reached, so the claim is unchanged

13.6's third row is **the only one that changes the claim**, and it requires
**opposite signs AND non-overlapping CIs**. Every pair was tested:

| pair | opposite signs | CIs overlap | shared region |
|---|---|---|---|
| HRpos_HER2neg vs HRpos_HER2pos | no | **yes** | [-0.5299, -0.0219] |
| HRpos_HER2neg vs TNBC | no | **yes** | [-0.3680, -0.0219] |
| HRpos_HER2pos vs TNBC | no | **yes** | [-0.3680, 0.1467] |

**All three summaries are negative and all three intervals overlap.** No pair
has either condition, let alone both. **So the draft sentence stands
unqualified**, which is the outcome 13.2 fixed in advance that E42 could not
change and did not.

### 5.2 But 13.6 HAS NO ROW FOR WHAT WAS OBSERVED, and the rule is NOT amended

13.6 was written to compare subtypes against each other and **assumed each
subtype would produce a coherent estimate.** Two things it did not anticipate:

- **The subtype carrying the pooled estimate is HR-positive/HER2-negative, not
  TNBC.** Its pooled estimate is -0.2759 [-0.5299, -0.0219] at I2 3.9% with both
  cohorts negative. **TNBC's pooled interval includes zero.** This is the
  reverse of the expectation recorded alongside the prompt, and the reverse of
  where GSE164458 - a TNBC-only cohort - sits.
- **TNBC is heterogeneous rather than null-and-underpowered.** Row 2 of 13.6
  reads "others null but underpowered". TNBC is null and **the best powered
  stratum in the study** (441 events; detectable 0.176 against
  HRpos_HER2neg's 0.312). Its I2 is 62.1% and its signs disagree. **Row 2 does
  not describe it and row 1's "consistent across subtypes" does not either**,
  since the inconsistency is inside a subtype rather than between them.

> **13.6 IS NOT AMENDED, and that is deliberate.** Adding a row for
> within-subtype heterogeneity **after seeing this result** would be writing a
> reading rule to fit an outcome, which is the thing the declare-then-run
> sequence exists to prevent. **The gap is recorded as a limitation of the rule,
> not repaired.** If a future analysis wants a rule for this, it declares one
> first. Section 13.6 in the declaration carries a dated pointer to this
> subsection and its content is unchanged.

exactly the "one common OX effect inside every subtype" assumption 13.1 said had
never been tested.**

**Even here 13.6's row 3 is not met**: the two intervals overlap in

**And by the identity re-asserted before any fit**, `subtype2` and
`er_primary` partition GSE25066's 465 identically, 278 / 187 on both. **So this
cohort's TNBC stratum IS its ER-negative stratum** - the half the declaration's
section 10 assigned the sign-pair argument to.

## 6. Two post-hoc items, licensed as NOTHING

### 6.1 The TNBC heterogeneity is GSE25066's stratum alone

**POST-HOC. Not declared. Arithmetic on the saved estimates, not a new fit.**
Recorded because any reader will ask, and better recorded than rediscovered:

| TNBC meta | estimate | 95% CI | Q | I2 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| all three cohorts | -0.1480 | [-0.3002, 0.0041] | 5.273 | **62.1** |
| leave out GSE164458 | -0.0934 | [-0.3060, 0.1193] | 4.753 | 79.0 |
| leave out GSE194040 | -0.0896 | [-0.2769, 0.0977] | 4.176 | 76.1 |
| **leave out GSE25066** | **-0.2283** | **[-0.3954, -0.0612]** | **0.104** | **0.0** |

Dropping GSE25066 collapses the heterogeneity completely; dropping either other
cohort leaves I2 at 76 to 79%. **13.2 forbids this becoming a finding and it
does not become one.** A leave-one-out chosen after seeing which cohort
dissented is not evidence about that cohort; it is a description of an already
observed dissent.

### 6.2 What would falsify the HRpos_HER2neg stratum, written now

It is **not** a finding (13.2). This list exists so that if anyone later wants
to make it one, the conditions were fixed before the wanting:

1. **A third cohort carrying an HR-positive/HER2-negative pCR stratum** gives a
   positive estimate, or moves the pooled CI across zero.
2. **It rests on 94 events in two cohorts**, 30 of them in GSE25066 where
   `treatment` is constant so no treatment adjustment was possible at all. A
   cohort where that stratum is treatment-adjusted and the estimate attenuates
   falsifies it.
3. **Swapping `PROLIF_DISJOINT` for another of the arm's proliferation scores**
   moves the pooled CI across zero - the same falsifier the primary clause
   carries.

## 7. The s508 explanation is REFUTED

**`rho(OX, subtype2) = -0.0538`, n = 465** (Spearman; TNBC = 1,
HRpos_HER2neg = 0). Mean OX is **+0.0757** in HRpos_HER2neg against **-0.0315**
in TNBC - a gap of 0.11 SD.

13.7 fixed the asymmetry before the number arrived: **a substantial correlation
would be consistent with the proposed explanation without demonstrating it,
while a near-zero correlation refutes it.** This is near zero, which is the one
direction in which a single number here is decisive.

**So luminal tumours are NOT appreciably more oxidative than TNBC on this
exposure in this cohort**, and GSE25066's unadjusted DRFS coefficient cannot be
absorbing a between-subtype difference. **The s508 divergence now has no
explanation.**

**What the number could not have settled either way**, recorded in 13.7 before
it ran: `s508` differs from the primary set in **two** ways - it omits the
subtype term **and** adds 18 patients with no callable subtype, carrying 2 DRFS
events. `s490` keeps subtype on 490 and gives 0.8557 [0.6893, 1.0622], all but
identical to the primary's 0.8563, so the live comparison is `s490` against
`s508` and it is confounded. **The 18 have no `subtype2` value and cannot be in
this correlation.** The fit that would settle it - `m1` on the 490 **without**
the subtype term - **is not authorised and was not run.** No sentence may claim
the divergence is explained.

## 8. What this licenses, and what it does not

### 8.1 Licensed - two statements

1. **That the draft sentence needs no subtype qualification.** By 13.6's test as
   declared, no pair of subtype summaries has opposite signs or non-overlapping
   CIs, so the claim stands unqualified. **The stratum estimates in 4.1 and 4.2
   are reported beside it** and the power limitation in section 3 is stated with
   them.
2. **That 13.6 was incomplete** - it has no row for a well-powered subtype that
   is internally heterogeneous, and the rule was not amended to acquire one.

### 8.2 NOT licensed

- **No subtype estimate is a finding**, including the HRpos_HER2neg pooled
  estimate whose CI excludes zero (13.2).
- **Nothing promotes, demotes, confirms or weakens E40's `m1`.** It stands as
  the primary result (13.2).
- **Not that the effect is "carried by luminal disease"**, nor that it is
  "absent in TNBC". TNBC's pooled interval includes zero at I2 62.1%; that is
  heterogeneity, not absence, and section 8's words apply.
- **Not that GSE25066's TNBC stratum has a positive effect.** +0.2400
  [-0.1275, 0.6074] includes zero and is one stratum of one cohort.
- **Nothing from the leave-one-out in 6.1.**
- **Not that the s508 divergence is explained.** It is now explicitly
  *unexplained*.
- **No p value described as a near miss**, which forbids reading 0.0645 and
  0.0716 as nearly-signals as much as it forbids inflating 0.0467 and 0.0496.
- No interaction, no effect-modification claim, no per-gene FDR, no causal claim.
- **SINGLE-INSTRUMENT, as the whole line is.** No coefficient compared across
  cohorts as a raw value (4.2).

## 9. Limitations

1. **One of four subtypes was never fitted.** `HRneg_HER2pos`, minority class 33
   against 7 parameters. **A scope check that cannot reach one subtype has not
   checked the whole scope**, and the draft sentence's generality over
   HER2-positive disease rests on nothing here.
2. **Two of four subtypes exist in one cohort only**, so for `HRpos_HER2pos`
   there is no replication of any kind - a single estimate in a pooled table.
3. **Two fitted strata are under 10 events per parameter** and flagged:
   GSE194040's HRpos_HER2neg (7.1) and HRpos_HER2pos (8.1).
4. **GSE25066's two strata carry no treatment adjustment**, because treatment is
   constant across that whole cohort. Its -0.4889 is the largest single-stratum
   estimate in the table and the least adjusted.
5. **The detectable-effect figures are approximations** that ignore covariate
   adjustment, so the true detectable effects are larger than section 3 shows.
6. **`m1` only.** m0, m2 and m3 were not fitted within strata, so nothing here
   says how much of a stratum's coefficient is proliferation.
7. **Exposure coverage differs by cohort** - GSE25066 57 of 89 on GPL96 - and
   within-cohort SD standardisation makes the coefficients comparable in units,
   **not in instrument**.
