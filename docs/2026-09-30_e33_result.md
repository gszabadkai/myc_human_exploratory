## 1. What NEITHER means here - the declared reading, and where it undersells

**The declared reading, verbatim:** "the configuration is not
programme-specific. Reportable, and consistent with E11's finding that it is an
OXPHOS correlate." And: "knowing it would stop the manuscript implying a
programme-specificity it does not have."

**As to MB2, that is exactly right.** Neither arm tracks MB2 at fixed MB1.

**"Not programme-specific" undersells what the numbers show, and the note says
so rather than let the gloss stand.** Descriptively, the configuration leans
**MB1**:

- **Both arms meet the mirror image of the separation condition.** `MB1 | MB2`
  is positive, its interval excludes zero, and it exceeds `MB2 | MB1`. The paired
  difference excludes zero in both.
- **It is not an artefact of partialling on the +0.712 diagonal.** The
  **marginals** say the same thing. The trigger arm's marginal on MB2 is
  **+0.058 [-0.001, +0.117]**, which is null, against **+0.217 [+0.159, +0.272]**
  on MB1. The guardian arm's is +0.068 against **+0.411**. **The configuration
  does not track MB2 even before anything is held fixed.**
- **It holds in all three analysis sets** (tables 2.2).

**This is not a verdict.** The declaration fixed three outcomes, all about MB2,
and an MB1 lean is none of them. **No MB1-ALIGNED category is invented after the
fact.** The lean is recorded as a post-hoc observation and gets a falsifier list,
written now, in section 8.

**What it does to the manuscript.** E32 found the MYC-high, OXPHOS-high *state*
aligned with MB2, and that sentence stands (section 5). **E33 shows the
BCL2-family configuration does not travel with it.** A reader must not chain
the two - "the state is MB2, so the death configuration is MB2" - because the
configuration, measured directly, sits the other way. This is CLAUDE.md trap 1
in a new place: a property of the state is not a property of everything the
state correlates with.

### 2.3 THE PROLIFERATION COMPANION - post-hoc, and the cell to be careful with

Declared at `9b3ae75`, **before the script was written**, as post-hoc and

**Adding proliferation to the conditioning set turns the trigger arm's MB2
partial from -0.140 to +0.223**, and it now exceeds its MB1 mirror. The guardian
stays on MB1. **Read literally against the form of the declared rule, that is
the TRIGGER MB2-SPECIFIC pattern.** It is stated in full here precisely so that
nobody finds it later and promotes it.

**It is not the verdict and it cannot become one, for four reasons:**

1. **It was declared unable to change the verdict**, before any number existed.
2. **Its two intervals overlap** ([+0.162, +0.282] against [+0.076, +0.198]).
   No paired difference was specified for the companion, and none is computed
   after the fact to rescue it.
3. **MB2 and proliferation run at +0.876.** At fixed MB1 and fixed
   proliferation, what is left of MB2 is the small part of it that is not
   proliferation. The declaration already ruled that which of MB2 and
   proliferation is doing the work is not interpretable here, and that binds
   in this cell exactly as it does in limb 1.
4. **It is one cell of a grid chosen after the design was fixed.** CLAUDE.md:
   never a p-value plucked from the grid, and never a cell either.

**What it is: a hypothesis.** "At fixed MB1 and fixed proliferation, the trigger
arm rises with MB2 and the guardian arm does not." If anyone wants it, it needs
its own declaration and an independent cohort. Section 8 says what would
falsify it.

## 7. How this sits with E11

E11 found the configuration arranged along OXPHOS and not along MYC, with the
OXPHOS arrangement being what mitochondrial localisation produces. **NEITHER is
what E11 predicts**, and the declaration said so in advance. What E11 did not
predict, and E33 cannot explain, is the **direction**: an OXPHOS correlate need
not lean MB1, since both biclusters carry biogenesis. It would if OXPHOS itself
leans MB1 at fixed MB2. On GSVA it does (+0.316 against +0.171); on mitoPPS it
does not. That is item 1 below. It is untested, because OXPHOS against forkscale
was declared uninformative and is kept that way here.

## 8. What would falsify the MB1 lean - written before any next analysis

**The observation.** *The BCL2-family configuration tracks MB1 forkscale at
fixed MB2, and not MB2 at fixed MB1.* It holds on both arms and the composite,
on 5 of 6 genes, in three analysis sets, on partials and on marginals. That is
enough to be worth a falsifier list, and not enough to be a finding.

It dies, or becomes something else, if:

1. **It vanishes when OXPHOS joins the conditioning set, on both GSVA and
   mitoPPS.** Then it is E11's OXPHOS correlate inheriting OXPHOS's own lean,
   and nothing about the configuration. If it vanishes on one instrument only,
   that is trap 5, and it is unresolved rather than explained.
2. **It vanishes inside Luminal, or inside ER+, alone.** MB1_UF is 85 of 86 ER+
   and mostly LumB. A lean that disappears within a subtype is subtype
   composition.
3. **It fails in METABRIC.** That needs "the METABRIC gap" in
   `data/menegollo_biclusters/README.md` closed first.
4. **It depends on the guardian arm alone.** The guardian lean (+0.517) is twice
   the trigger's (+0.250), and in Block C the trigger's `MB2 | MB1` interval
   reaches -0.046. If the trigger lean does not survive items 1 and 2, the
   observation is a statement about `BCL2L1` and `MCL1`.

**The companion pattern** (section 2.3) would need to be **declared as a
primary** in an independent cohort. It is falsified if the trigger arm's
`MB2 | MB1 + PROLIF` partial does not exceed its MB1 mirror **with a paired
difference excluding zero**.

**None of these was run.** Each needs its own declaration.
