# E47 - MYC and OXPHOS on the BCL-2-family signature, each held fixed: MIXED

## 0. The answer

**Q1. Does OXPHOS track the signature with MYC in the model?** Yes:
`beta_OX|MYC` is +0.30 to +0.61 in every primary cell. **This is expected by
construction and was not read** (declaration section 7). It is not a finding.

**Q2. Does MYC track it with OXPHOS in the model? The declared verdict is
MIXED.** On `M_a`, `beta_MYC|OX`'s CI is above zero in 4 of 8 primary cells.
It is negative in one, TCGA/GSVA at `m1`.

**Behind the label sits one structure, not noise.** Three gradients run in
the same direction (section 3):
1. **Proliferation adjustment raises `beta_MYC|OX` in every block.** With
   proliferation adjusted, MYC adds in 3 of 4 blocks.
2. **The less a MYC estimator is entangled with proliferation, the larger
   `beta_MYC|OX`.** On the cleanest estimator, MYC adds in all 8 cells,
   including `m1`.
3. **mitoPPS shows more MYC than GSVA** in all four comparisons.

RETRIEVED, not declared: **proliferation's own coefficient is negative** in
all eight retrieved fits.

**Section 9: OXPHOS tracks `BCL2L1` more steeply than `BBC3`**, at fixed MYC,
in all four cohort-by-instrument blocks. **LICENSED.**

## 3. What sits behind MIXED: three gradients, one direction

### 3.1 Proliferation

`beta_MYC|OX` on S6 and `M_a` rises at each rung, in every block (D2):

| SCAN-B, GSVA | -0.092 | +0.033 | +0.165 |
| SCAN-B, mitoPPS | 0.000 | +0.127 | +0.269 |

- **`m1` to `m3` adds +0.10 to +0.14.** That is the same uniform enlargement
  E34 saw in its quadrant contrasts (+0.12).
- **`m0` to `m1` adds +0.12 to +0.18.** Adding PAM50 (and purity and leukocyte

**RETRIEVED, NOT DECLARED: why.** In the `m3` fits for S6, `PROLIF_DISJOINT`'s
own coefficient is negative, with its CI excluding zero, in all eight
retrieved cells: `M_a` and the clean estimator, both cohorts, both
instruments, ranging **-0.22 to -0.38**. **At fixed OXPHOS and MYC, more
proliferation goes with a lower signature.** A MYC estimator that carries
proliferation is therefore pulled down until proliferation enters the model.

This is the mechanism behind N9's sign flips. **It is a description of
coefficients already fitted, not a declared quantity, and no manuscript
sentence may rest on it without its own declaration.**

## 10. Falsifiers, written BEFORE any next analysis (CLAUDE.md)

What now looks real, as a hypothesis for a confirmatory study: **MYC activity,
separated from proliferation, is associated with a higher BH3 trigger arm
(`BBC3`, `BID`) at fixed OXPHOS, and not with higher `BCL2L1`.** It would be
falsified, or seriously weakened, if any of these held:

- **F1, estimator.** On any further MYC activity estimator no more entangled
  with proliferation than `M_a`, and clean of the twelve, the trigger arm's
  `beta_MYC|OX` at `m1` is at or below zero in either cohort.
- **F2, proliferation measure.** At `m3`, replacing `PROLIF_DISJOINT` with
  another proliferation score (e.g. `PROLIF_STD`) leaves `M_a`'s trigger-arm
  coefficient non-positive in two or more of four blocks.
- **F3, subtype.** Within PAM50 subtypes, without PAM50 in the model, the
  clean estimator's trigger-arm coefficient reverses sign in two or more
  subtypes in both cohorts.
- **F4, an independent RNA-seq cohort that carries `BBC3`** (not METABRIC,
  which lacks it). The trigger arm's clean-estimator `beta_MYC|OX` there is
  at or below zero.
- **F5, composition.** In TCGA, a composition adjustment that passes its own
  validity check (none yet exists in this repo) removes the association.

**None of these is run here. Each needs its own declaration.**
