**Section 2 is the author's message, recorded unchanged.** It came after the author had run E57's first version and seen its two panels.

**What it changes in E57's second version.** This is Claude Code's reading, set out here so that it can be corrected.
- **Every string on both panels becomes black.** In the first version, three kinds of text were grey, as E45 drew them:
  - each row's second line (n, and pCR or deaths), in grey35;
  - I-squared, in grey35;
  - the direction cues' words, in grey30.
- **The cues' arrows stay E45's grey30.** They are lines, not text. If the author wants them black too, that is a one-line change.
- **On the pCR panel, the line joining each row's two OXPHOS circles becomes thinner and dashed.** These are the circles without and with the proliferation term.

  - **Its pattern** is R's "dashed", whose dashes and gaps scale with the line's width: here 1.02 pt each.

**The message says "on the figures".** Claude Code applies it here to E57's two panels. Two earlier panels print white values on their darkest cells, for legibility:
- Fig. 4g, with Extended Data e and f (E50), on 9 cells;

Whether the rule reaches them is the author's to say. Neither is changed.
