## 3. What was corrected along the way

Three things were found wrong and fixed, and they are the reason to trust the
rest:

1. **MitoCarta's `Synonyms` column is not injective.** It lists `QARS` under
   both `QARS1` and `EPRS1`. The old candidate filter also punished a gene for
   its own old name appearing elsewhere in the universe, which had silently cost
   `H2AZ1`, `POLR1G` and `VARS1` their resolutions. Fixed;
   `docs/2026-08-31_symbol_map_note.md`.
2. **`MT-ND6` is a technical artefact and was dropped from F3.** In SCAN-B it
   correlates 0.12-0.36 with every other mtDNA gene where every other pair sits
   at 0.6-0.94. It is the only light-strand, non-polyadenylated protein gene.
3. **The `_MITO` death strata are near-tautological** against a mitochondrial
   arm - both sit at +0.80 regardless of direction of effect. Found while
   verifying E05 and written into the script before it was run.

## 4. What can be answered WITHOUT re-running anything

**Read this before writing any new script.** Most "more detail" questions are
already in the saved grids and need a query, not compute.

```r
source(here::here("scripts", "E00_setup_packages.R"))
a <- readRDS(file.path(DIR_RESULTS, "correlation_atlas.rds"))   # the plane
d <- readRDS(file.path(DIR_RESULTS, "celldeath_axis.rds"))      # the death axis
f <- readRDS(file.path(DIR_RESULTS, "mtdna_falsifiers.rds"))    # the F3 verdict
```

**Free, instantly, from `a$atlas` (68,255 rows):** any combination of
2 cohorts x 5 instruments x 21 MYC estimators x (18 arms + 13 mtDNA genes) x
8 strata x 3 adjustments. So: any arm in any subtype under any estimator; the
entanglement slope for any arm (`a$entanglement_slope`); cross-cohort
reproducibility for any cell (`a$reproducibility`); instrument agreement per
estimator (`a$instrument_agreement`).

**Free from `d$death_grid` (73,422 rows):** any of 18 death sets against any of
105 partners (21 MYC estimators, 72 arm x instrument, 12 product terms) in any
stratum under any adjustment; plus `d$overlay` for ~60 individual death genes
against 7 partners, `d$null_test`, and `d$cdc_contrast`.

**Free from `f`:** the three mitoPPS denominators, the five instrument-free
ratios, and the per-gene mtDNA table raw and content-adjusted, all x 20
estimators x 2 cohorts.

**Cheap - a script edit and a few minutes' re-run:**
- expression-matching the pro-death and pro-survival strata against each other
  (**D1's remaining falsifier, and the single most valuable thing to run**)
- per-gene rho for any new gene list, either cohort (`.per_gene_rho`, 2.3 s)
- new mtDNA ratio denominators (`E03b` section 3 takes one more line each)
- more genes in the E05 overlay, or more estimators in it

**Expensive - a new GSVA call, ~15-25 min, and it must go in ONE call with the
pins:**
- scoring any gene set that is not already among E02's 36
- size-matched *random* GSVA sets as a comparator

**Out of scope until explicitly reopened:** MCbiclust / forkscale, survival,
treatment, METABRIC, DepMap, causal or mediation modelling, and anything that
revisits the validation study's hypotheses.

### Candidates already visible, offered not chosen

Listed so they are not lost, and so they can be rejected deliberately.

1. **`MT-CO1` vs `MT-CO2`.** Adjacent on the same transcript, opposite signs
   after conditioning on mtDNA content (+0.440 vs -0.295 in TCGA). The most
   striking and least explained thing in the study. Their mutual correlation is
   unremarkable (0.77/0.78) so it is not obviously mismapping - but that is an
   absence of evidence, not a test.
2. **D1's expression-matching falsifier.** If pro-survival genes are on average
   more highly expressed than pro-death genes, the contrast follows from that
   and not from biology. Cheap, and it decides whether D1 is real.
3. **Why does `M_b` behave differently?** The CollecTRI regulon estimator is the
   weakest on the OXPHOS plane (+0.240 / +0.117) yet gives the sharpest
   BCL2-family signal (`BCL2A1` +0.658, `BID` +0.556, `BCL2` -0.550). Something
   about it is measuring a different thing and it is not understood.
4. **`MENSSEN_MYC_TARGETS` is the top signature** on OXPHOS (0.670 / 0.616) at
   only 13.2% entanglement and 53 genes, above every Hallmark set. Worth knowing
   what is in it.
5. **The `ELLWOOD` / `ALFANO` anomaly.** These two low-entanglement signatures
   are what make the entanglement slope positive. If they are simply weak
   signatures rather than informative ones, F1's complication softens.
6. **`TANG_ENTOTIC_CELL_DEATH`** is -0.392 against OXPHOS in TCGA, the most
   negative death cell in the grid, on 17 genes and 0% mitochondrial. Real or
   small-set noise? Its null z is only -1.15 / -1.30.
7. **The ER-negative gap.** ERneg is the weakest stratum for MYC-OXPHOS in both
   cohorts (median 0.315 / 0.416 vs 0.479 / 0.512 for ERpos). Nothing has been
   done with that.

## 6. Rules that still bind

- **Option A.** Claude Code writes and edits the numbered scripts. It does not
  run them. Infrastructure - git, snapshots, provenance READMEs, `CLAUDE.md`,
  planning and result notes - it may execute directly.
- **Exploratory posture.** Nothing is pre-registered. Report structure,
  gradients and cross-cohort reproducibility; never a p-value plucked from a
  grid of 68,255 cells. When something looks real, write down what would
  falsify it *before* the next analysis - that is what E03b was.
- **Never write to `myc_human_validation` or `myc_mouse`.** Read-only via
  `git -C ... show <ref>:<path>`.
- **Human only.** The ortholog tripwire is now narrowed to calls:
  `grep -rnE "(mouse_to_human|human_to_mouse|ortholog[s]?)[[:space:]]*\(" scripts/`
  must return nothing.
- **Scale discipline.** GSVA wants log VST; mitoPPS wants linear DESeq2. They
  never share an input object. The correlation engine rank-transforms
  everything, so correlations are immune - the discipline binds where scores are
  *built*.
- **Never pool GSVA or mitoPPS values across cohorts.** Compare correlations and
  patterns.
- R: no `print(n = X)` after `head()`; always `dplyr::count()`; ASCII-only
  strings; every numbered script ends with an `if (FALSE) { ... }` sandbox.
