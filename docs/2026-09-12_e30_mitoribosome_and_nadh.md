# E30 - the mitoribosome gap fails the ladder exactly as central dogma did, and that is the finding

**Two parts with different standing, and they do not merge.** PART A is a
**declared check** of an existing finding on a second ruler, with the comparator
named in advance. PART B is a **descriptive panel** on the author's provisional
annotation, which is ambiguous and contested, is not a classification, and
**nothing in it may be promoted to a readout.**

**2026-09-12. EXPLORATORY, POST-HOC, DESCRIPTIVE**, as all of Phase 2 is. R1 to
R5 were fixed before any number and none was a prediction.

Script: `scripts/E30_mitoribosome_and_nadh.R`. **Sourced by the author
2026-09-12**; the run reproduces **all 22 saved elements identically**. `E25`,
`E26`, `E27`, `E28` and `E29` are not modified and nothing is re-scored.

## 0. The answer in six lines

| | |
|---|---|
| **Do the controls pass?** | **Yes.** 1,136 positions reproduce `E25` and 88 reproduce `E27` at max delta 0, and all six supplied point estimates reproduce within 0.03. |
| **Does PART A pass its ladder?** | **No, and it fails identically to the comparator it was meant to improve on.** With the same numerator, **2 of 4** non-exempt estimators agree for the mitoribosome and **2 of 4** for central dogma. |

**The one-sentence reading: two different comparators with the same numerator
fail the estimator ladder in exactly the same way, which turns `E28`'s
pre-declared inference into a demonstration - the failure is a property of the
rank-gap statistic, not of the comparator.**

## 1. What PART A actually is, measured before it was run

**It is a refinement of the statistic `E28` failed with, not an independent
one.** The mitoribosome is **100% contained** in Mitochondrial central dogma,
all 83 of its genes, and across the eight groups the two gaps correlate at
**rho +0.905**. The *prior* is independent, since `E20` and `E21` named the
mitoribosome in advance on a level ruler, but the *statistic* is nested. So the
informative case was always passing where the parent failed.

**The numerator is better here, and that is not a variation.** `E28` used the
OXPHOS umbrella; this uses OXPHOS subunits. `E27` showed the umbrella is exactly
subunits plus assembly factors with assembly factors barely moving, and `E28`
measured it diluting by 8.5 percentile points. Both terms changed and one change
is an improvement, **so the ladder is run for central dogma with the same
numerator, like for like.**

**`E20`'s finding was already estimator-dependent, and R1 was built knowing it.**
On the level ruler, `rho(ox_lvl) − rho(ox_nuc_mtrib)` was positive in 8 of 8
adjudicating cells **including the regulon**; separately `mtrib_lvl − ox_lvl` was
positive 6 of 6 for the three signature estimators and **not** for the regulon,
at +0.004 and +0.003 with intervals covering zero, which `E21` explained as a
size artefact. **R1 therefore exempts `myc_regulon`, with the reason stated
rather than the exemption hidden.** Its values here are +12.7 and −16.9.

## 2. R1 - the ladder fails, and the like-for-like comparison is the point

| instrument | mitoribosome, TCGA | SCAN-B | central dogma, TCGA | SCAN-B |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `MYC_UP.V1_UP` | +2.8 | −12.0 | +22.5 | +11.3 |
| **`FELSHER`, primary** | **−17.6** | **−21.1** | −6.3 | −2.8 |
| `HALLMARK` | +10.6 | +1.4 | +35.2 | +31.7 |
| `myc_regulon`, **exempt** | +12.7 | −16.9 | +26.1 | +4.2 |
| MYC mRNA | −29.6 | −41.5 | −52.8 | −55.6 |
| **TCGA copy number** | **+47.2** | — | +38.0 | — |

**Both comparators give exactly 2 of 4.** Only the primary and MYC mRNA hold the
sign in both cohorts, `HALLMARK` is positive in both, and the copy-number split
- the one instrument not defined on the expression matrix - disagrees for both.

**That equality is the result.** `E28` inferred that its failure was a property
of the rank-gap statistic rather than of its comparator. Running a second,
independently motivated comparator with the same numerator and getting the
identical failure pattern **turns that inference into a demonstration.**

**One thing the comparator does change: the magnitude.** With the same numerator
the primary effect is −17.6 and −21.1 for the mitoribosome against −6.3 and −2.8
for central dogma. **The mitoribosome is the better comparator on effect size
and it makes no difference to the outcome**, which is itself informative about
how little the choice of denominator matters here.

## 3. R2 and R3 - the mouse side, where this does improve on `E28`

**R3: the two mouse windows now clear zero, where `E28`'s did not.**

| contrast | mitoribosome gap | central dogma gap (`E28` as run) |
|---|---|---|
| WT window | **−38.0 [−55.6, −1.4]** | −42.3 [−80.3, +7.0] |
| Myc+ window | **−38.0 [−57.7, −4.2]** | −34.5 [−73.2, +11.3] |
| MYC at 6W | −16.9 [−45.1, +16.9] | −12.7 [−57.0, +36.6] |
| MYC at 12W | −16.9 [−40.1, +7.0] | −4.9 [−43.7, +35.2] |

The intervals are roughly half as wide, ±27 against ±44 on the wild-type window.
**So on the mouse developmental contrast the mitoribosome comparator is
materially more precise and the fall is resolvable at n = 6.** Two of four mouse
contrasts clear zero, and they are the two that matter for the developmental
claim.

**R2: the tiers do not separate.** Within the mouse, on one paired resample of
all four groups, the developmental tier minus the MYC tier is **21.1 with an
interval of −7.7 to 33.1**. The point estimates are reported as two tiers only
as point estimates.

**And the exactness is a coincidence, named so it is not read as structure.**
Both mouse windows land on **−54 rank steps** from different components, −76 and
−22 against −80 and −26. Both within-timepoint MYC contrasts land on **−24**,
from +40 and +64 against +36 and +60. **Different components, identical
difference.** Four contrasts hitting two integers makes the tier story look
structural when the components say it is not.

## 4. R4 and A5 - composition and proliferation

**R4: differencing does not protect, for the second time.** The gap moves **3.5**
percentile points under composition adjustment against **2.8** and **0.7** for
its two components, so differencing adds rather than cancels. `E28` measured 2.1
against 1.4 and 0.7 for central dogma. **Every gap number in this script
inherits that.** TCGA only, because SCAN-B has no purity estimate and nothing is
imputed.

**A5: the proliferation stratification repeats `E28`'s shape.**

| cohort | low proliferation | mid | high |
|---|---|---|---|
| TCGA | +5.6 | +1.4 | **−23.2** |
| SCAN-B | −21.1 | −34.5 | **−56.3** |

Four of six strata keep the primary's sign, but **in TCGA the effect is positive
at low and mid proliferation and appears only at high**, while in SCAN-B it is
negative throughout and grows with proliferation. The adjustment was
**deliberately not run**: `E28` showed it uninformative here because its positive
control grew 2.2 times under it.

## 5. PART B - the NADH candidate panel, description only

**The annotation is the author's, provisional, ambiguous and contested.** It is
not a classification, nothing in it is defended as set membership, and **no
set-level statistic is computed, no members are counted by direction as though
the count were a result, and no programme is named.** All eighteen members are
present in the shared panel.

**Three entries have gaps that are partly or wholly arithmetic, and they are
marked.**

| pathway | genes shared with the numerator | consequence |
|---|---|---|
| `CI subunits` | **37 of 37, 100%** | **the gap is ARITHMETIC** |
| `Complex I` | 37 of 59, 63% | partly arithmetic |
| `TCA cycle` | 4 of 20, 20% | partly arithmetic |
| `Electron carriers` | 2 of 14, 14% | partly arithmetic |
| everything else, including `CI assembly factors` | 0 | clean |

That the consumer-side entries sit inside or beside OXPHOS in the hierarchy was
anticipated; **the sharper fact is gene containment, and `CI subunits` is wholly
contained.** Its panel is drawn and labelled rather than hidden.

`E29`'s aligned-change means are carried forward for every member, so this panel
sits beside the map that produced the candidates rather than replacing it.
`E29` already found the off-diagonal structure diffuse and the sign counts
incoherent; **this is finer-grained description of the same object, not a second
attempt at the same claim.**

## 6. What PART A could carry, and what it could not

**The sentence it can carry, and it is about the mouse only:**

> Across the mouse developmental window the respiratory chain loses position
> relative to the mitoribosome by 38 percentile points in both genotypes, with
> intervals clear of zero at n = 6.

**What it cannot carry.**

1. **Nothing on the human side.** The sign of the human gap difference depends
   on which MYC estimator defines the groups, and the copy-number instrument
   disagrees with the primary. **No human gap number from this script belongs in
   a manuscript**, exactly as for `E28`.
2. **It does not corroborate `E20`/`E21` on the rank ruler.** That was R1's
   agreement branch and it did not fire. The level-ruler evidence stands on its
   own and is not strengthened here.
3. **It does not establish two tiers.** The separation is not established at
   this n and the exactness is a coincidence.
4. **It is not protected against composition.** Its own check failed.
5. **`E28`'s gap readout is not rehabilitated.** PART A joins it as a negative,
   and the two together are stronger evidence about the statistic than either
   alone.
6. **Nothing from PART B may be used as a comparator** without a separately
   declared validation.

**What `E30` adds to the record is a negative with a mechanism.** Two
independently motivated comparators, same numerator, identical failure. The
rank-gap statistic does not survive the estimator ladder, and the comparator is
not what decides that.
