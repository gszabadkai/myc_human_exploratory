**One qualification, found after the run (Part 2, H2; AD HOC arithmetic, not declared).** Part of the signature's proliferation coefficient can be inherited from the way the signature was built.
- **The mechanism.**
  - The rule chose genes by their correlation with OXPHOS at fixed proliferation (E47 4a.7).
  - A component chosen that way raises OXPHOS's coefficient. It lowers proliferation's by the slope of OXPHOS on proliferation times as much: 0.26 in TCGA and 0.39 in SCAN-B.
  - **It leaves MYC activity's coefficient unchanged.**
- **The extreme case: all of the signature's OXPHOS coefficient is such a component.** Then the inherited part would be:
  - on 4g's model, -0.13 of -0.28 in TCGA and -0.12 of -0.35 in SCAN-B;
  - on the reference model in TCGA, -0.15 of -0.22.
- **What remains is negative in every cell.** No interval has been computed for it.
- **So brief section 2's "not circular" holds for MYC activity's coefficient, and only in part for proliferation's.** Whether to declare the test is the author's call (section 5).

### H2. Part of the signature's proliferation coefficient may be inherited from its construction

**Observed (AD HOC, after the run, from E49's saved frame and coefficients).**

**1. The raw-to-adjusted change is the omitted-variable arithmetic** that the 2026-11-02 brief named in its 1.3: "not biology".
- The signature correlates positively with OXPHOS and MYC activity, and both correlate with proliferation.
- Holding them fixed removes the positive path through them, which leaves a negative coefficient.
- **The raw correlations alone reproduce the fitted coefficient**, by standardised regression on the three exposures with no spine:

  | | from the raw correlations | fitted, with the spine |
  |---|---|---|
  | TCGA | -0.33 | -0.28 |
  | SCAN-B | -0.34 | -0.35 |

**2. How the construction enters.**
- **The rule (E47 4a.7).** It kept genes by their Spearman correlation with OXPHOS level, partial on proliferation, with no spine and no MYC term.
- **A component chosen that way is OXPHOS minus c times proliferation.** c is OXPHOS's raw slope on proliferation: 0.26 in TCGA and 0.39 in SCAN-B.
- **That component is an exact combination of the model's terms.** Per unit, it moves the `m3` coefficients by:
  - +1 on OXPHOS;
  - 0 on MYC activity;
  - -c on proliferation.
- **The extreme case:** all of the signature's OXPHOS coefficient is such a component. The inherited part of proliferation's coefficient, and the remainder, on OXPHOS level:

  | model | signature | cohort | proliferation's coefficient | inherited, at most | remainder |
  |---|---|---|---|---|---|
  | 4g's | S6 | TCGA | -0.279 | -0.134 | -0.145 |
  | 4g's | S6 | SCAN-B | -0.347 | -0.122 | -0.225 |
  | 4g's | S7 | TCGA | -0.285 | -0.142 | -0.143 |
  | 4g's | S7 | SCAN-B | -0.308 | -0.151 | -0.158 |
  | reference | S6 | TCGA | -0.221 | -0.154 | -0.067 |
  | reference | S6 | SCAN-B | -0.347 | -0.154 | -0.193 |
  | reference | S7 | TCGA | -0.257 | -0.160 | -0.097 |
  | reference | S7 | SCAN-B | -0.320 | -0.180 | -0.140 |

- **What the table does not cover:**
  - S5's rule also uses MYC, so this arithmetic does not describe S5;
  - on OXPHOS priority, the selection's exposure is not in the model.
- **A different geometry, for comparison.** Suppose the component were OXPHOS at fixed MYC activity *and* proliferation.
  - On 4g's model, the inherited part would then be near zero in TCGA and about -0.08 in SCAN-B.
  - **MYC activity's coefficient would be pushed down, not up.**
  - **This is not the rule's geometry.** A first pass of this arithmetic held the signature's other correlations fixed, which amounts to this geometry, and concluded that TCGA inherits nothing. That conclusion is withdrawn.

**3. The spine's share, for the genes.**
- **The same arithmetic without the spine** reproduces the adjusted proliferation coefficient within 0.15 for most of the fifteen genes.
- **Where it does not, the spine moves the coefficient by 0.15 or more:**
  - in TCGA: `PMAIP1` and `BCL2` (where the spine includes purity and leukocyte fraction);
  - in SCAN-B: `BAX`, `BAK1`, `BID`, `BMF`, `BCL2A1` and `BCL2`.
- **So the raw-to-adjusted sign changes of 1.8 have two sources:**
  - the exposures' arithmetic, as for the signature and for `BAX` in TCGA;
  - the spine: for `BAX`, `BAK1` and `BID` in SCAN-B, for `BCL2` in both cohorts, and, by a smaller move (+0.12), for `BCL2L11` in SCAN-B.

**Reading.**
- **"At fixed OXPHOS and MYC activity, higher proliferation went with lower signature expression" correctly describes a partial association.** It does not say that proliferation lowers these transcripts.
- **How much of its size could be inherited:** up to about half of the coefficient on 4g's model in TCGA, and a third in SCAN-B. On the reference model in TCGA, it is over two-thirds.
  - **This is the OXPHOS coefficient's circularity, carried into proliferation's through the correlation of OXPHOS with proliferation.**
- **The remainder is negative in every cell**, but it has no interval.
- **Brief section 2 calls the proliferation coefficient not circular.** The rule never read it, but it partialled on proliferation, so the coefficient is not independent of the rule.
  - The draft's sentence S5 says the associations with proliferation and MYC activity "played no part in its definition". That is literally true, but for proliferation it implies more independence than the arithmetic allows.
  - **The author's call** (section 5).
- **The MYC sentence is unaffected:**
  - a component of the rule's kind leaves MYC activity's coefficient unchanged;
  - the alternative geometry would lower it.

**What would falsify the claim that the signature's proliferation coefficient is not wholly inherited from its construction:**
1. **The remainder's 95% interval including zero.** The remainder is the linear contrast `b_P + c b_OX`, computed from E47's saved coefficients and `vcov`.
2. **A control signature, built by the same rule from non-apoptotic mitochondrial genes.** Its proliferation coefficient at fixed OXPHOS and MYC activity would have to be as negative as S6's.

**Both need their own declaration**, as E50.
