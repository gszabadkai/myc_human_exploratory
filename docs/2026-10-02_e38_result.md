# E38 - the signed near-zero is not cancellation, and MYC mRNA is down

**EXPLORATORY AND POST-HOC.** Nothing pre-registered. **N3**: transcript
scores; nothing is "primed". **No outcome variable, no product term, no causal
or killing language.** Nothing was written to `myc_human_validation` or
`myc_mouse`.

**THIS CANNOT OVERTURN E36.** E36 returned UNINTERPRETABLE on a declared
control and a declared reading rule, and that verdict stands exactly as
recorded. **E38 is a diagnostic underneath it, not an appeal against it.**

Everything below is on the **paired comparator** (n = 113 in both tissues),
**unadjusted**, with 10,000 patient-level resamples, unless stated.

---

## 1. The gate: NOT CANCELLATION ALONE, and both legs fail

The question was whether the configuration's near-zero correlation with OXPHOS
in normals (**-0.011**) is biology or merely the arithmetic of a signed
composite cancelling a common component.

| leg | threshold, fixed at `94d5fd7` | observed | passes? |
|---|---|---|---|
| **A** - unsigned configuration joins the unsigned composites | **>= +0.729** | **-0.037** | **NO** |
| **B** - arbitrarily signed comparators collapse | both **< 0.30** | **+0.457**, **+0.834** | **NO** |

**Leg A fails as completely as it could.** Taking the configuration's own six
genes and making every one of them positive moves the normal-side correlation
from **-0.011 [-0.22, 0.19]** to **-0.037 [-0.24, 0.17]**. **A difference of
0.026.** The sign is not doing the work.

**Leg B fails too, in the other direction.** Signing a comparator does not
collapse it: mitophagy at the configuration's own 5:1 balance gives **+0.834
[0.76, 0.89]**, Fe-S **+0.457 [0.29, 0.60]**. Even the *balanced* splits, which
cancel far more than the configuration's construction ever could, stay up at
**+0.533** and **+0.299**.

**The brief's leading hypothesis is refuted.** It was the right thing to test
first and the answer is no.

### 1.1 The sign-permutation, which needed no threshold, agrees

Making exactly one of the six genes negative, in turn:

| variant | normal | 95% | tumour |
|---|---|---|---|
| `config, -BIK` | **+0.529** | [0.38, 0.65] | +0.305 |
| `config, -BBC3` | +0.141 | [-0.06, 0.33] | +0.483 |

| `config, -BCL2L1` | -0.189 | [-0.38, 0.01] | +0.222 |
| `config, -BID` | -0.194 | [-0.38, 0.00] | +0.234 |
| `config, -BAD` | -0.276 | [-0.46, -0.07] | +0.180 |

**Neither pre-written pattern occurred.** Not all six collapse (`-BIK` is at
+0.529), and it is not only the `MCL1` variant that does. **The spread is 0.8
wide**, so *which* gene carries the negative sign matters enormously - but that
is a statement about those six genes, **not** about signing as an operation.

## 3. THIS CORRECTS A SENTENCE IN E36'S COMMITTED RESULT NOTE

**E36's qualification 4 contains a claim that E38 falsifies, and it is recorded
here rather than quietly left.** It read:

> *"Unadjusted, the five COMPANION composites correlate with OXPHOS at +0.729 to
> +0.902 in normals ... In normal breast almost everything correlates with
> almost everything ... it is a near-zero value in a tissue where every unsigned
> composite sits far above it."*

- **The first clause is a fact and stands.** Those five did run +0.729 to +0.902.
- **The generalisation does not.** E36's five companions were **all Fe-S or
  mitophagy halves** - one kind of set. Widening to 24 composites, **the
  normal-side range is -0.483 to +0.902**, not +0.729 to +0.902.
- **"every unsigned composite sits far above it" is false.** The unsigned
  configuration is **-0.037**, E10's twelve unsigned **-0.122**, the apoptotic 44
  cytosolic half **-0.483**.

**E36's VERDICT IS UNAFFECTED.** It rested on the declared Fe-S control firing
in 6 of 12 cells and on all six cells splitting by adjustment - neither of which
involves qualification 4. **What changes is an interpretation offered alongside
the verdict, not the verdict or any number behind it.** A pointer to this
section is added to E36's note; **its recorded numbers are not edited.**

is not a half either.

**The brief's informative case did not occur.** It was *"both halves running
near +0.8 in normals and separating only in tumours"*. Instead the gap is
**present in normals and LARGER there**, on both instruments, for both sets that

**And it is not specific to the apoptotic machinery.** Fe-S cluster assembly
shows a *larger* gap in normals (+1.022 against +0.754) and a comparable one in

**The limitation declared in advance is the likely explanation**: a
mitochondrial half correlates with OXPHOS partly through shared mitochondrial
content, which varies far more in normal breast - adipose against epithelium -

this cannot be separated, and no reading is taken.

**Two further reasons this is not evidence about the configuration**: 5 of its
6 genes sit *inside* the apoptotic 44's mitochondrial half, so that half is not
independent of E36's readout; and the whole leg is post-hoc.

## 6. Diagnostic 4 - MYC mRNA is DOWN in tumours against their own normal

**This is the clearest result in E38 and it depends on no regulon, no gene set
and no scoring choice.** Within-patient, 113 pairs, log2 expression:

| gene | median difference | 95% | % of pairs with tumour higher | 95% |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **`MYC`** | **-1.394** | [-1.78, -1.07] | **15.0%** | [9.6, 22.8] |
| `MYCN` | +0.666 | [0.22, 1.07] | 63.7% | [54.5, 72.0] |
| `MYCL` | +0.561 | [0.16, 0.84] | 61.9% | [52.7, 70.4] |

**In 85% of patients the tumour has LESS MYC mRNA than that patient's own
adjacent normal tissue** - a median 2.6-fold decrease. `MYCN` and `MYCL` go the
other way.

**The brief said this would be the more interesting answer. It is the one that
occurred.**

**This is `CLAUDE.md`'s trap 4, in its sharpest form yet.** *"MYC mRNA is not
MYC activity, and the difference is total."* E36 found MYC *activity* higher in
the tumour in 70% of these same pairs on `M_a`. **The transcript goes the
opposite way in the same patients.**

**The caveat is large and it is composition.** Normal breast is mostly adipose
and stroma, and `MYC` is expressed across cell types; a bulk comparison between
two tissues of such different composition is not a statement about MYC in any
cell. **It is reported as a fact about bulk transcript levels and nothing more.**

### 6.1 The 82 stripped targets are MYC's own canonical targets

**So "proliferation-stripped MYC regulon" is a misleading name.** Stripping

`M_b__BOTHSTRIP` should not be read as "MYC activity with growth removed"
without saying so. **This qualifies E36's section 6.1**, where those two
estimators reversed the sign of the paired MYC result.

## 7. What E38 licenses, and what it does not

**It licenses nothing about normal breast, the coupling, or MYC biology.** Every
quantity is post-hoc; two legs are second attempts.

**It licenses three methodological statements**, all of which are about
instruments rather than tumours:

1. **The configuration's near-zero correlation with OXPHOS in normal breast is
   not an artefact of its signed construction.** Unsigning it moves it by 0.026,
   and arbitrarily signed comparators do not collapse.
2. **E36's "almost everything correlates with almost everything in normal
   breast" was an over-generalisation from five sets of one kind.** The true
   range across 24 composites is -0.483 to +0.902, and the apoptotic sets are at
   the bottom of it.
3. **`M_b__PROLIFSTRIP` strips out MYC's canonical targets** - 4.9-fold enriched
   in `HALLMARK_MYC_TARGETS_V1` against what it keeps - so it is not "MYC
   activity minus growth" and should not be described as such.

**And it records one observation for a future analysis to test properly**: bulk
`MYC` transcript is lower in tumour than in matched adjacent normal in 85% of
pairs while MYC *activity* scores are higher in 70%. **In bulk tissue of this
composition difference, that is as likely to be composition as biology, and
E38 cannot separate them.**

**It withdraws nothing from E36 except the generalisation in section 3.**

## 8. What would be needed to go further

1. **A composition-matched null for the compartment gap**, which is E14's and is
   not rebuilt here. Without it, section 4 cannot be read.
2. **Purified or single-cell normal breast.** Every limitation above reduces to
   bulk composition, and no adjustment built from bulk markers has yet survived
   its own validity check - the declared nine-gene score failed, and the
   luminal-only score failed by +0.048.
3. **A second cohort with normals.** SCAN-B has none.
4. **For the `MYC` transcript observation**: an epithelium-restricted
   measurement. Nothing in bulk TCGA can separate a real decrease from a
   compositional one.
