**Nothing was downloaded. Nothing was written to `myc_human_validation` or
`myc_mouse`.** The counts below are input facts - joins, memberships, file
contents. No arm, gene or MYC quantity was correlated with a forkscale before
this note was written.

## 2. The UF / LF / none assignments - located, and what they cover

**They are not in the per-bicluster files.** Those carry `X`, `pc1`, `index`,
`PAM50`, `LumA`, `LumB` and nothing else. **They are only in the assembled
frame**:

```
data/menegollo_biclusters/TCGA.all.biclusters.RNAseq.Rdata
  -> TCGA.all.biclusters.RNAseq.df2$MB1.fork, $MB2.fork
     factor, levels None / Lower / Upper, 849 rows
```

| | None | Lower (LF) | Upper (UF) | total |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `MB1.fork` | 605 | 151 | 93 | 849 |
| `MB2.fork` | 604 | 159 | 86 | 849 |

`Upper` is read as UF and `Lower` as LF, by name. The script reports ER and PAM50
by fork so a reader can check the labelling against Menegollo's descriptions
(MB1_UF almost all ER+, MB2_UF ER-negative). That check is context and not a
test.

The same frame also carries `MB{k}.forkscale.fork_0.4` and `_0.8`. **Those are
cuts on forkscale at two thresholds, not the MCbiclust fork calls, and are not
used.** The declaration keeps forkscale splits and fork assignments apart; so
does this.

### 2.1 What the 849 reveal about how the calls were made

Checked on the 849, where `pc1` and `index` agree exactly with the
per-bicluster files:

- **Membership is an index cut.** Every assigned sample has `index <= 299` and
  every `None` has `index >= 301`, in both biclusters. **`index == 300` is absent
  from the 849 in both**, so whether the cut is 299 or 300 cannot be told.
- **UF against LF is a pc1 threshold.** There is a clean gap: MB1 LF
  `pc1 <= -2.226`, UF `>= -0.982`; MB2 LF `<= -3.799`, UF `>= +2.611`.
- **The upstream rule itself is not identified.** A threshold of zero
  reproduces MB2 exactly and misses 2 MB1 samples; the mean of the top 300
  misses 2 and 1; the median misses 23 and 35; the largest gap in pc1 fails
  outright on MB1. The rule is not needed (next section), and it is not guessed.

### 2.2 The other 188 - a bounded extension, not a reconstruction

For a sample outside the 849, the call is **determined by the stored calls
themselves** wherever it lies outside both boundaries: `index >= 301` is `none`;
`index <= 299` with `pc1` at or below the LF maximum is LF, at or above the UF
minimum is UF. **Any rule consistent with the 849 gives the same answer there.**
What is left undetermined is set to `NA` and counted:

| | from the 188: none | LF | UF | undetermined | extended total |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| MB1 | 132 | 28 | 27 | **1** (index 300, pc1 -7.71) | 1,036 |
| MB2 | 133 | 33 | 21 | **1** (index 300, pc1 +0.057, also inside the pc1 gap) | 1,036 |

**DECISION.** The **stored calls are primary**, because they are the snapshot's
own assignments. The 188 appear as an explicit fourth level, `not in 849`, so
that nothing is dropped silently and every cell size still sums to the analysis
set. **The bounded extension is reported alongside as a sensitivity.**

## 6. DECISION - how the arms are scored. Taken by the author, 2026-09-30.

**Not in the declaration**, which names the genes of each arm but not how an arm
becomes one number, or how the guardian arm is read "together" when its two
genes run in opposite directions: on OXPHOS `BCL2L1` is +0.388 and `MCL1` is
-0.266 (reconciliation doc, section 3.1). **No per-sample arm score or
configuration composite existed anywhere in this repo.** The question was put to
the author before the script was written and before any arm was correlated with
anything. The answer:

**Each gene is signed by its direction in the configuration** - section 3.1's
"sensitisers and `BCL2L1` up, `MCL1` down" - so `BBC3`, `BID`, `BIK`, `BAD` and
`BCL2L1` carry +1 and `MCL1` carries -1. **Each arm is E16's `.comp` recipe**:
per-gene z of log2(linear + 1) across the full TCGA cohort (1,095), multiplied by
the sign, averaged.

| readout | built as | role |
|---|---|---|
| **trigger arm** | mean(z `BBC3`, z `BID`, z `BIK`, z `BAD`) | **the verdict turns on it** |
| **guardian arm** | mean(z `BCL2L1`, **-z `MCL1`**) | **the verdict turns on it** |
| configuration composite | signed mean of all six | reported third, for continuity; not the verdict |
| each of the six genes | the gene itself, **unsigned** | reported always; never the verdict |

**Why signed.** The declaration calls these "the two arms of the configuration",
and reads BOTH ARMS SEPARATE as "the whole configuration is MB2-associated". An
unsigned guardian mean was the alternative. It was rejected because its two
genes partly cancel, which **biases it towards "does not separate", and so
towards the licensing verdict**.

**Consequence for reading the tables.** `MCL1` is reported unsigned on its own
row and enters the guardian arm negated. **Its own row and its contribution to
the arm therefore have opposite signs by construction.**

The z-scores are computed once, on the full cohort. Inside each bootstrap
resample the scores are re-ranked and never re-standardised, which is how E32
treated its GSVA scores.

## 7. How the verdict rule is operationalised - fixed here, before the script

The rule is applied **as written**. These are the choices it leaves open:

- **"Exceeds MB1|MB2" is the point estimate.** E32's declaration had a
  non-overlap clause; this one does not. **The paired difference
  `MB2|MB1 - MB1|MB2`, bootstrapped on the same resamples, is reported beside
  every readout as the stricter reading.** The note says whether the stricter
  reading would have given a different verdict. **It is not the verdict.**
- **An arm "separates"** iff its `MB2|MB1` partial is > 0, its 95% interval
  excludes zero, and its point estimate exceeds its own `MB1|MB2`.
- **The verdict turns on the two arm composites only.** Genes and the
  configuration composite are reported, and they neither rescue nor overturn it.
- **A fourth outcome the declaration does not name**: the guardian arm
  separates and the trigger arm does not. If it arises it is reported under that
  literal description, `OUTSIDE THE DECLARED OUTCOMES`, and **given no licensing
  reading**. It is not folded into any of the three.

Each is named here before the script is written, and **none can change the
verdict**.
