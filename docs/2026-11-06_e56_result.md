## 4. The declaration's stated expectation: it held, and it licenses nothing

**The expectation (the declaration, section 6):** BAX's and BAK1's OXPHOS-high quadrants should sit above their OXPHOS-low ones, in both cohorts and on both instruments.

**It held for both genes in all four blocks, on either reading:**
- **at matched MYC activity**, Q2 above Q1 and Q4 above Q3;
- **and in full**, both OXPHOS-high means above both OXPHOS-low means.

**What it is worth:**
- **It is a consistency check, not a replication.** The expectation came from E49, which fitted the same tumours with a different model. Two summaries of the same data agree.
- **It compares point estimates only,** with no contrast or test, as the declaration requires.
- **It was written down so that a different pattern would be seen as one.** None was.

**BOK, about which the expectation said only "less so"**, is recorded and not read:
- at matched MYC activity, its OXPHOS-high means sit above its OXPHOS-low ones in 3 of 4 blocks, and in full in 2 of 4;
- the exception at matched MYC activity is SCAN-B on mitoPPS, where its four means lie between −0.05 and +0.11.
