## 3. The one pattern worth stating

**The movement is not random, and it is not symmetric.**

| kind | n | mean delta | max abs | all same sign |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| OXPHOS | 8 | **+0.0008** | 0.011 | no |
| **MYC** | 8 | **-0.0384** | 0.047 | **yes, all negative** |

**The OXPHOS contrasts barely move** — mean delta eight ten-thousandths, signs
mixed, which is what noise looks like. **All eight MYC contrasts shrink**, by a
consistent ~0.04.

**This does not change any conclusion**, and it is reported because a consistent
eight-of-eight direction should not be averaged away as noise:

- E34's reading was that the MYC contrasts are several times smaller than the
  OXPHOS ones. On the rebuilt snapshot they are **slightly smaller still**, so
  the direction of the shift **strengthens** E34's reading rather than
  threatening it.
- It is **not** evidence that E34 over-stated MYC. 0.04 on contrasts whose
  intervals span roughly 0.33 is well inside the noise of the estimates
  themselves; what is notable is only that the sign is consistent.
- **No mechanism is claimed.** Including 113 normals changes the size factors
  and the gene filter, and the MYC quantity is a GSVA score, which is
  cohort-relative by construction and therefore the quantity most exposed to a
  change in what the cohort is. That is a plausible reading and it is not
  tested here.

## 4. The checks that could have caught a wrong answer

1. **The transcription cross-check.** The gate compares against E34's values as
   transcribed from its committed note. A mistyped comparator would produce a
   spurious PASS or FAIL, so the script also compares that transcription with
   E34's own saved object: **16 of 16 match exactly.** The comparator is right.
2. **The declared assertions**, which are a stop regardless of any threshold:
   **Q3 == `STATE` level 2 and Q4 == levels 3 + 4, TRUE on both instruments** on
   the rebuilt scale. The quadrant is still the constructor's own call.
3. **The sample-count assertion.** The script refuses to continue unless the
   filter lands on exactly 1,095 and 113.
4. **The mitoPPS identity.** The universe's global mean is **1.0000**, as the
   construction requires.
5. **Determinism.** Two dry runs after the fix are identical in all 15
   non-timestamp elements. Every step is deterministic — DESeq2, VST, GSVA and
   `lm` carry no RNG — so this is by construction rather than by seed.

## 5. One dry-run defect, and what it says

The first dry run **halted** in section 8. E34 names the column `subtype`; the
cross-check asked for `subset`, which is also a base R function, so the
comparison hit a closure and **errored rather than silently returning nothing**.
Fixed at `2e098d9`; no gate logic changed and no threshold was touched.

**It is worth recording which way that failed.** A wrong column name that
returns an empty frame would have produced a cross-check that silently verified
nothing. Because `subset` happens to be a function, it stopped the script
instead. The dry run existed to catch exactly this class of thing, and the
defect was in the *verification* code rather than in the gate.

  from the other.** The gate in section 2 is the sole exception and is labelled
