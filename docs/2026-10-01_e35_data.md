# E35 - inputs, two feasibility constraints, and the decisions taken before the script

**Nothing was downloaded. Nothing was written to `myc_human_validation` or
`myc_mouse`.** Nothing below is a coupling estimate. The only data touched were
distributions, missingness, correlations and cell counts.

**TCGA only.** SCAN-B carries no CNV, so **no replication cohort exists for this
question** and the result note must say so.

---

## 1. Two constraints the declaration did not anticipate

Both were found by counting, before any contrast existed, and both are recorded
here so neither can later look like an excuse.

### 1.1 `genome_doublings` cannot be split into tertiles

It takes **three values**. Over the whole covariate table: 0 (576), 1 (394),
2 (50), NA (75). Within the analysis set (section 3) it splits **523 / 367 / 48**.
A tertile cut of a three-level variable is not a tertile cut.

**DECISION: `genome_doublings` is stratified on its own three native levels**,
which are already an ordered burden measure, and the result note calls them
levels rather than tertiles. **Level 2 will not be readable**: its minimum
quadrant cell is **4**, against the declared floor of 20. It is reported and
marked *not readable*, never estimated.

### 1.2 The declared `m2` layer is almost entirely unreadable

The declaration makes within-subtype estimates "the result, not a robustness
check", with a floor of n >= 20 per quadrant cell. At subtype x stratum
resolution that floor is met in **1 of 15 cells, for every one of the three
proxies**.

The cause is the circularity the declaration anticipated, and it is severe
(section 2.2): burden tracks subtype so hard that the lowest tertile is three
quarters LumA and **Basal has a minimum quadrant cell of 0** in both continuous
proxies.

**DECISION, taken by the author on 2026-10-01 when put to them before the script
was written:** `m2` is run **exactly as declared** and reported honestly as 1 of
15 readable, **and** a **combined-Luminal companion** is added alongside it.

| stratification | AS | FGA | GD |
|---|---|---|---|
| per PAM50 subtype x stratum (as declared) | 1 of 15 | 1 of 15 | 1 of 15 |
| **LumA + LumB combined x stratum** | **3 of 3** | **3 of 3** | **2 of 3** |

Minimum quadrant cells for the combined-Luminal companion are **32 / 24 / 32**
(AS), **31 / 28 / 29** (FGA) and **46 / 36 / 3** (GD, level 2 failing).

**Why combined Luminal and not something else.** `functions/strata.R` already
defines `Luminal = LumA + LumB`, added 2026-09-01 for precisely this reason:
*"each alone is underpowered for a gene-level breakdown where the combined
696/2436 is not."* This uses an existing stratum definition rather than
inventing one to fit, and it is declared **before** any estimate exists.

**The companion does not replace the declared `m2` and cannot.** Both are
reported. If they disagree, that is reported too.

## 2. The burden proxies

### 2.1 Distributions and missingness, whole covariate table (n = 1,095)

| proxy | class | n | NA | range | quartiles |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **`Aneuploidy.Score`** (primary) | integer, 36 levels | 1,038 | 57 | 0-35 | 0 / 5 / 11 / 19 / 35 |
| `FGA` | continuous | 1,065 | 30 | 0-0.997 | 0 / 0.125 / 0.251 / 0.436 / 0.997 |
| `genome_doublings` | **3 levels** | 1,020 | 75 | 0-2 | 576 / 394 / 50 |

They are **not independent measurements of different things**. Spearman between
them: `Aneuploidy.Score`-`genome_doublings` **0.800**,
`Aneuploidy.Score`-`FGA` **0.645**, `FGA`-`genome_doublings` **0.502**. The
declaration already says the two secondary proxies are "replications of the same
question on the same cohort, not independent evidence"; these numbers are the
measure of that.

### 2.2 The circularity, quantified

The declaration states that burden "correlates with proliferation and with basal
subtype, which is the circularity this analysis exists to escape". Measured:

| | rho with `PROLIF_DISJOINT` |
|---|---|
| `Aneuploidy.Score` | **+0.375** |
| `FGA` | **+0.568** |
| `genome_doublings` | **+0.343** |

Median `Aneuploidy.Score` by PAM50: **LumA 7**, Normal 4.5, **Basal 15**,
LumB 16, Her2 16.

**Subtype composition of the tertiles** is the sharpest statement of the
problem:

| proxy | T1 | T2 | T3 |
|---|---|---|---|
| `Aneuploidy.Score` | **74.7% LumA**, 6.4% Basal | 38.8% LumA, 23.4% Basal | 35.7% LumA, 22.7% Basal |
| `FGA` | **76.0% LumA**, 5.1% Basal | 55.4% LumA, 9.6% Basal | 21.2% LumA, **36.2% Basal** |

**A burden tertile is close to a subtype stratum.** This is why the declaration
says stratify rather than adjust, and it is why the within-subtype layer matters
and why it is also the layer the data can barely support.

## 3. The analysis set, and the cells

Everything is rebuilt exactly as `scripts/E34_quadrant_configuration.R` does
(section 4). The m1 covariate set — quadrant + PAM50 + purity + leukocyte
fraction — gives the same **938** patients E34 used, which is also exactly the
Block C 938 set, patient for patient (E34 result note 8.3). Block C is not used
here and is named only so the coincidence is not mistaken for a restriction.

Complete cases after adding each proxy, and the tertile cuts computed **within
that set**:

| proxy | n | strata | sizes | cuts |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `Aneuploidy.Score` | 938 | T1/T2/T3 | 344 / 286 / 308 | > 7, > 16 |
| `FGA` | 936 | T1/T2/T3 | 312 / 312 / 312 | > 0.176, > 0.380 |
| `genome_doublings` | 938 | 0/1/2 | 523 / 367 / 48 | native levels |

`Aneuploidy.Score` tertiles are **unequal (344/286/308) because it is an integer
with 36 levels** and the cuts fall on ties. That is reported, not corrected.

**Quadrant cell sizes per stratum** — all pooled strata clear the floor except
`genome_doublings` level 2:

| proxy | stratum | Q1 | Q2 | Q3 | Q4 | min | readable |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| AS | T1 | 148 | 74 | 47 | 75 | 47 | yes |
| AS | T2 | 70 | 43 | 58 | 115 | 43 | yes |
| AS | T3 | 75 | 50 | 65 | 118 | 50 | yes |
| FGA | T1 | 143 | 62 | 51 | 56 | 51 | yes |
| FGA | T2 | 101 | 61 | 43 | 107 | 43 | yes |
| FGA | T3 | 47 | 44 | 76 | 145 | 44 | yes |
| GD | 0 | 205 | 111 | 76 | 131 | 76 | yes |
| GD | 1 | 84 | 46 | 80 | 157 | 46 | yes |
| **GD** | **2** | **4** | **10** | **14** | **20** | **4** | **NO** |

**Note what the quadrant distribution itself does across burden.** On
`Aneuploidy.Score`, T1 is Q1-heavy (148 against 75 in Q4) and T3 is Q4-heavy
(118 against 75 in Q1). Burden tracks the MYC x OXPHOS quadrant, which follows
from burden tracking proliferation and MYC. **The tertiles are not exchangeable
groups**, and the contrasts within them are not estimated on comparable
populations. This bounds what any reading can mean and belongs in the result
note.

## 4. What is reused from E34, verbatim

E34 is on `main`, so it is **copied, not sourced** — this branch was cut from
`main` at `3ea6c76`, which already carries it, but copying keeps E35 readable on
its own.

| piece | taken from |
|---|---|
| the quadrant, both instruments | `E34` section 3 `.build_quadrant`, including the frozen constructor called twice and the `myc := oxphos` probe |
| the assertions | **Q3 == STATE level 2 and Q4 == levels 3 + 4, re-asserted on both instruments; the script stops on failure** |
| the configuration composite | signed mean z of `BBC3`, `BID`, `BIK`, `BAD`, `BCL2L1` and **-`MCL1`** on log2(linear + 1), z across the whole cohort |
| the arms | trigger = `BBC3`/`BID`/`BIK`/`BAD`; guardian = `BCL2L1` minus `MCL1` |
| the twelve transcripts | E10's `PRIMING_PRO` + `PRIMING_ANTI`, unsigned, no per-gene FDR |
| Blom normal scores | E22's `.blom`, pooled **once over the whole cohort**; every stratum subsets and never re-scores |
| m1 covariates | quadrant + PAM50 + purity + leukocyte fraction |
| standardised means and contrasts | `lm`, prediction averaged over the fit's own rows with quadrant forced to each level; classical intervals |

**The same divergence E34 recorded applies here**: the declaration says "the
configuration composite, as E11 and E34 define it", but **E11 defines no
composite**. The composite is E33's, which E34 adopted. Stated again so it is
not re-discovered.

**No product term anywhere.** The two OXPHOS contrasts are computed **within**
each stratum. There is no continuous interaction term and no burden x quadrant
product.

## 5. The two endpoints, and the models

The coupling is the two OXPHOS contrasts, exactly as E34 computes them:

| contrast | what it is |
|---|---|
| **Q2 - Q1** | the coupling where MYC is low |
| **Q4 - Q3** | the coupling where MYC is high |

| model | formula, within each stratum |
|---|---|
| **m1** | configuration ~ quadrant + PAM50 + purity + leukocyte fraction |
| **m3** | m1 + `PROLIF_DISJOINT` — mandatory companion, reported beside m1, never chosen between |
| **m2** | m1 without PAM50, within each PAM50 subtype — **as declared, 1 of 15 readable** |
| **m2L** | m1 without PAM50, within **combined Luminal** — the companion of section 1.2 |

Within a single subtype PAM50 is constant and is dropped; `m2` and `m2L` are
therefore quadrant + purity + leukocyte fraction.

**Readability floor: n >= 20 in all four quadrant cells.** Cells below it are
**reported as not readable, never estimated.**

## 6. The reading rule, as written

Applied to the **configuration composite** on the **primary proxy**
(`Aneuploidy.Score`), with the other two proxies reported as the same question
on the same cohort:

- **GRADED** — the OXPHOS contrasts increase monotonically across strata, with
  the low and high stratum CIs non-overlapping.
- **SATURATED** — present in all three strata and not differing.
- **ABSENT AT LOW BURDEN** — do not clear zero in the lowest stratum and do in
  the highest.

**SATURATED is the declared expected outcome** and the result note writes it as
the expected result, not as a disappointment or a failure.

Two things the declaration leaves open, fixed here:

1. **"Monotonically" is read on the point estimates**, across the three strata,
   for a given contrast. With two contrasts (`Q2 - Q1` and `Q4 - Q3`) the
   reading requires **both** to be monotone in the same direction, and the
   non-overlap requirement applies to **both**.
2. **"Present" in SATURATED means the CI excludes zero** in every stratum, and
   "not differing" means the low and high stratum CIs **overlap**. A pattern
   that is neither GRADED nor SATURATED nor ABSENT AT LOW BURDEN is reported as
   **NONE OF THE THREE**, literally, with no reading invented for it.
