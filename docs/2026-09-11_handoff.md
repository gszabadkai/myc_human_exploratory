## The result, in one place

**Split on MYC status, each human group maps onto one mouse group. Separated
from its runner-up in all four cases, and the two cohorts give the same answer.**

| human group | nearest mouse group | rho | 95% CI | runner-up rho |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| TCGA MYC-low | mouse 12W WT | 0.61 | 0.47 to 0.73 | 0.08 |
| SCAN-B MYC-low | mouse 12W WT | 0.64 | 0.52 to 0.75 | 0.16 |
| TCGA MYC-high | mouse 6W Myc+ | 0.46 | 0.30 to 0.60 | 0.00 |
| SCAN-B MYC-high | mouse 6W Myc+ | 0.51 | 0.36 to 0.64 | 0.09 |

**Two of the four mouse groups match nothing human.** Every cross-species pair
involving mouse 6W WT or mouse 12W Myc+ has an interval covering zero.

**The MYC axis and the timeline axis point opposite ways.** The human MYC
contrast agrees with the mouse Myc effect at both timepoints in both cohorts
(rho 0.39 to 0.54, every interval clear of zero) and disagrees with both
temporal contrasts (-0.38 to -0.52). The respiratory chain sits at the 76th and
79th percentile of the two mouse genotype contrasts and the 10th and 9th of the
two temporal ones.

**What human tumours share with the mouse is the MYC move, not the developmental
move.** That is the sentence to defend or destroy tomorrow.

## What changed yesterday and why, so it is not re-litigated

Four things the author raised, and what each turned into.

1. **mtDNA.** Confirmed and symmetric. Both species strip `mt-`/`MT-` from the
   canonical pathways into one synthetic pathway that stays in the scoring
   universe. Recorded, no action. It runs opposite to the nuclear subunits
   everywhere, which is trap 8 behaving as documented.
2. **Size normalisation.** The premise turned out not to hold and the real
   problem was elsewhere. **Both species take the mean per set** (mouse script 08
   line 386, this repo's `.path_scores`) and **mitoPPS is centred at exactly 1
   per pathway in both**, now asserted in code. The size bias was created by
   E24's own within-sample rank transform, which converts a variance difference
   into a mean-rank difference and scales with cohort heterogeneity. On E25's
   estimand nothing exceeds 0.21 against log set size, where E24's was 0.54 and
   0.57.
3. **Stratification.** The decisive one. Pooled over MYC status the cohorts
   averaged into the middle of the panel and read as nothing. See the table above.
4. **Purity.** Untouched by design. **This is tomorrow.**

**And one the author did not raise:** the mouse time axis is batch-confounded,
batch equals timepoint. **The author has ruled the timeline comparisons stay
in**, on the grounds that reprioritisation is a timeline effect measurable only
that way and DESeq2 normalisation is what handles the batch. That ruling is
implemented: every temporal quantity is computed and reported in full, the
confounder is stated in the object, the verdict table and both figure captions,
and **no number is withdrawn on its account. Do not reopen this.**

## Tomorrow: E26, purity and composition

The design was agreed before E25 was written and has not changed.

**Trap 15 governs the whole script and is the reason it exists.** The mouse
fat-pad result put `Mki67` at tau +0.313 before adjustment and -0.096 after,
while the adjusters explained only **15%** of its variance. So:

1. **A positive control is declared in the header and read FIRST**, before any
   adjusted OXPHOS number is looked at. If the control does not survive the
   adjustment, the adjustment is over-correcting and neither reading stands.
2. **TCGA only.** Purity, leukocyte fraction and ploidy exist for **1,020 of
   1,095** samples. **SCAN-B has none and nothing is imputed** (trap 2), so the
   purity arm structurally cannot replicate.
3. **Three reads:** the contrast adjusted for purity and leukocyte fraction; the
   contrast in a high-purity subset; an adipose-content estimate as a second,
   independent adjuster.
4. **The unreadability criterion is pre-declared**, before any number: if the
   MYC-high against MYC-low separation collapses under adjustment **while the
   positive control survives**, the mapping above is a composition artefact and
   the note says so.

**Why this is where the finding is most exposed.** MYC-high tumours are more
proliferative and plausibly more tumour-cell-pure, and adipose is OXPHOS-high.
Part of the separation could be composition rather than programme. Preliminary
and unadjusted, from E24's object: `rho(OXPHOS subunits percentile, purity)` is
+0.155 and `rho(., leukocyte fraction)` is -0.088 in TCGA.

**Workflow unchanged.** Option A: Claude Code writes `E26`, dry-runs it in the
