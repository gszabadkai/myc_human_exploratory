# B4 - the OXPHOS main effect on guardian dependency. Result.

**N3 throughout.** These are Chronos gene-effect scores and PRISM log fold
changes. Neither a tumour nor a cell line is described as "primed".

---

## 0. The answer in six lines

| | verdict | |
|---|---|---|
| **B4-a** | **FAIL** | and it is the **clean reverse**: **8 of 8** breast cells - 2 genes x 4 rulers - point the opposite way to the prediction |
| **B4-b** | **PASS** as declared | on breast, where the rule was set. But `POLR2B` is negative on all four rulers there and its pan-cancer CI excludes zero on two - see 4.1, this qualifies the reverse finding |
| **B4-c** | rank only | OXPHOS subunits ranks **11th-12th of 18** arms (17th once). The declared falsifier "as large on every other mitochondrial arm" is **met** |
| **B4-d** | **AGREES** | all 8 cells, and the reversal holds at **n = 1,127** where 3 of 4 `MCL1` CIs exclude zero |
| **B4-e** | mixed, and half of it is **absent** | the MCL1 drug leg is uninformative at n = 28; **the BCL-XL leg could not be fitted at all** |
| **the null** | **NOT required** | B4-a is FAIL, so there is no positive OXPHOS arm result to calibrate |

**What B4 establishes.** V1's dependency prediction does not transfer to DepMap.
The transcript configuration in OXPHOS-high tumours - `BCL2L1` up, `MCL1` down -
does **not** produce the dependency it was predicted to produce, and if anything
the cell lines run the other way: **OXPHOS-high lines are slightly MORE
MCL1-dependent and slightly LESS BCL-XL-dependent.**

That is the direction the declaration named in advance as live and as **not a
pass**: *a cell can need a protein more precisely because it has less of it*. It
is reported here as the reverse, not rescued as a finding.

`MCL1` **positive**. Breast, n = 51:

|---|---|---|---|---|
| `BCL2L1` | `ox_gsva` | **+0.196** | [-0.065, +0.458] | no |
| `BCL2L1` | `ox_rel` | **+0.173** | [-0.089, +0.436] | no |
| `BCL2L1` | `ox_ppd` | +0.074 | [-0.234, +0.383] | no |

breast null at n = 51 is uninformative, not evidence against*. **What makes it
more than that is section 3.**

| `MCL1` | `ox_lvl` | -0.091 | **-0.038** | [-0.073, -0.004] | **yes** |
| `MCL1` | `ox_gsva` | -0.048 | -0.029 | [-0.061, +0.003] | no |
| `BCL2L1` | `ox_lvl` | +0.066 | +0.041 | [-0.004, +0.086] | no |
| `BCL2L1` | `ox_rel` | +0.173 | +0.037 | [-0.003, +0.078] | no |

**All 8 agree in sign with breast.** The `MCL1` leg reaches CI-excludes-zero on

**This is what turns a reverse-signed null into a reverse-signed result.** The

but they are consistent across four rulers, two scopes and a 22-fold change in
n, which is the kind of consistency this repo counts as evidence and a single
cell of a grid is not.

**It is a negative for V1 either way.** V1 predicted `BCL2L1` negative and `MCL1`
positive. It got the opposite on both, on every ruler, in both scopes.

## 4. B4-b - the floor, and the caveat the rule does not capture

Declared rule: neither direction present on `RPL3` or `POLR2B`, breast, where
"present" is a CI excluding zero. **On that rule, B4-b PASSES** - no floor CI
excludes zero on breast:

| gene | mean estimate over 4 rulers | negative on |
|---|---|---|
| `RPL3` | **+0.019** | 1 of 4 rulers |
| `POLR2B` | **-0.081** | **4 of 4 rulers** |

### 4.1 But POLR2B is not silent, and that qualifies section 3

Beyond the declared criterion, and reported because it bears directly on how
section 3 should be read:

| ruler | `MCL1` pan | `POLR2B` pan | `RPL3` pan | `MCL1` / `POLR2B` |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `ox_rel` | -0.041 | **-0.026** (CI excludes 0) | +0.008 | 1.56 |
| `ox_ppd` | -0.040 | **-0.025** (CI excludes 0) | +0.011 | 1.64 |
| `ox_lvl` | -0.038 | -0.021 | +0.016 | 1.81 |
| `ox_gsva` | -0.029 | -0.020 | +0.005 | 1.46 |

**`POLR2B` carries a same-signed effect about 60% the size of `MCL1`'s, and on
two rulers its pan-cancer CI excludes zero.** `RPL3` is clean everywhere.

Read honestly, that means **part of the `MCL1` reversal is a general fitness
gradient**: OXPHOS-high lines appear marginally more sensitive to losing a
pan-essential, and `MCL1` sits about 1.5-1.8x above that floor rather than on
clean ground. It does not explain `BCL2L1`, which moves the **opposite** way to
the floor and is therefore not a fitness artefact.

**This is exactly the hazard the declaration named**: an interaction is robust to
anything that shifts all dependencies together and a main effect is not. B4-a
failed regardless, so B4-b's void clause never fires - but had B4-a passed, this
floor would have needed settling first, and that is worth recording.

**The rule is not retro-fitted.** B4-b was declared on breast and is scored on
breast: PASS. The pan-cancer floor is additional information, reported as a
caveat and not as a changed verdict.

## 5. B4-c - and the third declared falsifier is met

Where `OXPHOS subunits` ranks among the 18 mitochondrial arms, rank 1 being most
extreme in each gene's declared direction:

| gene | `ox_gsva` | `ox_ppd` | `ox_lvl` | `ox_rel` |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `MCL1` | 12 | **17** | 12 | 12 |
| `BCL2L1` | 11 | 11 | 12 | 12 |

**Mid-pack to poor on every ruler.** For `BCL2L1` on `ox_gsva` the arms above
OXPHOS subunits (+0.196) include `TCA cycle` (+0.330), `CII subunits` (+0.288),
`Fatty acid oxidation` (+0.278) and `Amino acid metabolism` (+0.251).

Declaration section 9 named three falsifiers. **The third is met:** *the effect
as large on every other mitochondrial arm as on OXPHOS - not respiratory, a
general mitochondrial-content effect.* Whatever small signal is here is not
specific to the respiratory chain.

**No p-value is attached to this panel and none should be.** There is no
expression-matched null in CCLE and the TCGA nulls are not transferable. It is a
rank ordering. Because B4-a is FAIL, **the CCLE matched null is not required by
this result** - there is no positive OXPHOS arm result to calibrate.

## 6. B4-e - half uninformative, half absent

**The MCL1 leg is uninformative**, n = 28, all three compounds, all four rulers:
estimates -0.090 to +0.499 with CIs a full unit wide or more. 4 of 12
coefficients agree in sign with the CRISPR `MCL1` result, which is what a coin
would give. A null at this n means nothing either way, as declared.

**The BCL-XL leg is ABSENT, not null, and this was discovered rather than
assumed.** Both selective BCL-XL inhibitors are missing from Repurposing 24Q2 -
known in advance. What was not known is that **navitoclax and venetoclax could
not be fitted either**:

| compound | PRISM screen | lines with data (of 28) | fittable at floor 25 |
|---|---|---|---|
| `S63845`, `AMG-176`, `AZD5991` | `REP.1M` | **28** | yes |
| `NAVITOCLAX` | `REP.PRIMARY` | **22** | **no** |
| `VENETOCLAX` | `REP.PRIMARY` | **21** | **no** |
| `navitoclax - venetoclax` | - | **21** | **no** |

The 24Q2 release is two screens and they do not cover the same lines. The three
MCL1 compounds are in `REP.1M` and cover all 28; the BCL2/BCL-XL pair is in
`REP.PRIMARY` and covers 21-22.

**So the one interpretable BCL-XL quantity - navitoclax minus venetoclax on the
same lines - has 21 lines and was not fitted.** All 12 skipped fits are recorded
in `$skipped` with their n and the reason. **A navitoclax result is never
reported as a BCL-XL result, and there is no navitoclax result here to misreport.**

This is why the script records skips rather than returning quietly: an endpoint
that fails an n floor otherwise disappears from the results table looking exactly
like an endpoint nobody asked for.

## 7. The diagnostic: is the additive `OX` readable as a main effect?

`OX` was extracted from both models. On the primary arm they agree closely:

| scope | median \|additive - at mean MYC\| | max |
|---|---|---|
| breast | 0.0044 | 0.158 |
| pan-cancer | **0.0006** | 0.012 |
| drug | 0.064 | 0.142 |

**So the additive `OX` is interpretable as a main effect**, which is what Block
G's null interaction implies and this confirms rather than assumes.

The four largest disagreements anywhere are all in the 18-arm panel on small
arms at n = 51 - `mtDNA-encoded OXPHOS` on `ox_ppd` (-0.594), then `CII
subunits` and `TCA cycle` on `ox_lvl`. A 13-gene arm on the composition
instrument at n = 51 is an unstable fit, and those cells should not be read.

**None of this is an interaction claim in either direction.** Block G settled
that and B4 does not revisit it.

## 8. What this does and does not mean

**Does:**

- **V1's dependency leg does not transfer to cell lines.** The prediction was
  specific, directional and pre-declared, and it is wrong on both genes, on
  every ruler, in both scopes.
- **The transcript-to-dependency step is the weak link, and now it is measured.**
  Synthesis 5.2 arrow (f) - "the configuration reaches protein and sets priming"
  - had **no** evidence attached to it. It now has evidence pointing the other
  way in one system.
- **The direction that did appear is the inverse-of-supply one.** OXPHOS-high
  tumours carry less `MCL1` transcript; OXPHOS-high cell lines are marginally
  more MCL1-dependent. Same for `BCL2L1` in mirror image. Both legs point the
  way the declaration's two-sided caution said they might.

**Does not:**

- **Not a null overturned.** N2 is pre-registered and stands untouched. B4 asked
  a different question of a different quantity in a different system.
- **Not an interaction claim**, in either direction.
- **Not a refutation of M2.** M2 is about transcript levels in tumours and is
  unchanged - the twelve-gene table is what it was. What falls is the inference
  from that configuration to a dependency, which is V1, not M2.
- **Not clean.** `POLR2B` carries 60% of the `MCL1` effect (4.1) and OXPHOS
  ranks 11th-12th of 18 arms (5). Even the reversal is neither specific nor
  respiratory.
- **Not a cross-cohort comparison.** CCLE scores are never pooled with TCGA or
  SCAN-B scores. Only the pattern is read.

## 9. What would change this answer

1. **A protein-level or functional readout of the twelve.** Every quantity here
   is a gene-effect score or a drug LFC in cell lines, and cell lines have no
   stroma, no infiltrate and no selection history. Synthesis 8.1 already lists
   this as something the human arm cannot decide.
2. **BH3 profiling stratified by OXPHOS**, which is V1 proper and which neither
   this nor Block G addresses. B4 tested a corollary of V1, not V1.
3. **A PRISM release with dose-response AND MCL1 coverage.** None exists: the
   dose-response screens are 19Q4/20Q2 and contain none of the three MCL1
   compounds, so the trade would cost the whole MCL1 arm. The BCL-XL leg needs a
   different resource entirely - both selective inhibitors are absent.
4. **The floor.** If a wider panel of pan-essentials shows the same small
   negative gradient with OXPHOS, then section 3's `MCL1` result is a fitness
   gradient and not a dependency at all. That is the cheapest next check and it
   is not a matched null - it is a handful of extra control genes.

## 10. Out of scope, and stayed out

No MYC stratification. No Johnson-Neyman. No interaction claim. No new endpoint
genes - `POLR2B` is a declared floor substitute. No CCLE matched null, and it is
not required by this result. **Nothing written to `myc_human_validation`**: its
`results/depmap_dependency.rds` was neither read nor rewritten, and its working
tree is untouched.
