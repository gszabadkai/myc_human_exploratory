# E38 - what will be run, and the splits that have to be named first

**Nothing was downloaded. Nothing was written to `myc_human_validation` or
`myc_mouse`.** No composite was built, no correlation computed, no model
fitted. Everything below is a gene list, a count or a cross-tabulation of
covariates that already existed.

**E38 IS A DIAGNOSTIC, NOT A HYPOTHESIS TEST.** It decomposes quantities E36
already computed. **E36's verdict of UNINTERPRETABLE stands as recorded and
nothing here can change it.**

---

## 0. Convention, and one thing that does not exist

`git branch -a` gives seven local branches: `main`, `bh3-mimetic-oxphos`,
`e33-programme-specificity`, `e34-quadrant-configuration`, `e35-burden-coupling`,
**`e36-normal-comparison` (current)** and `mb2-forkscale`, each with an `origin`
counterpart.

**There is no `branch_manifest.md` in this repo and there never has been** -
checked across every branch with `git log --all --diff-filter=A`. The
information it would hold lives in `CLAUDE.md` on `main` and in the dated notes.
**E38 does not create one**; inventing a convention mid-analysis is the wrong
moment for it, and it is flagged here rather than silently skipped.

**E38 runs on `e36-normal-comparison`, which is still live** - E36 and E37 are
both on it and neither is merged. **The next free script number is `E38`**, as
`CLAUDE.md` on `main` states.

## 1. What E38 reuses, and the one place it diverges

Read from `results/joint_tcga_*.rds`, **the joint 1,208-sample snapshot E37
built, and nothing else.** Reused verbatim from `scripts/E36_normal_comparison.R`:
the composite constructor, the partial Spearman, the patient-level resampling,
the three comparators (`all` 1,095, `site` 394, `paired` 113), the two
instruments, and the three adjustments.

**One divergence, stated because the brief asks for Blom scores.** E36's Q1 path
**does not Blom-transform anything** - the partial Spearman ranks its inputs
internally, so a monotone pre-transform would change nothing and E36 applies
none. E38 reproduces E36's Q1 path exactly, so **no Blom transform enters E38
either.** Blom appears in E37's *linear-model* frame, which E38 does not use.

**Bootstrap scope, declared:** 10,000 patient-level resamples **on the `paired`
comparator only**, which is the one the brief names for intervals and the one
where both tissues are n = 113. The `all` and `site` comparators are reported as
point estimates. This is a cost decision, declared rather than discovered.

## 2. Diagnostic 1(a) - what "the unsigned configuration" means

The brief says *"the same twelve genes, all entering positively"*. **The
configuration is six genes, not twelve**, and the twelve is a different object:
E10's priming panel, which `E34` reports unsigned. Both are built, because they
answer different questions, and **which one answers diagnostic 1 is fixed here:**

| composite | genes | signs | role |
|---|---|---|---|
| **configuration, signed** | 6 | `BBC3 BID BIK BAD BCL2L1` +1, `MCL1` -1 | E36's readout, the thing being diagnosed |
| **configuration, UNSIGNED** | the same 6 | all +1 | **PRIMARY for diagnostic 1(a)** |
| E10's twelve, unsigned | 12 | all +1 | what the brief asked for literally |
| E10's twelve, signed | 12 | 7 pro +1, 5 anti -1 | the full priming balance, 7:5 |

**The six-gene unsigned composite is primary because it isolates the one thing
in question.** It changes the sign and nothing else. The twelve-gene version
changes the gene set *and* the signing at once, so a difference could be either.
E10's twelve are `BCL2L11 BMF PMAIP1 BBC3 BID BAD BIK` (pro) and
`BCL2 BCL2L1 MCL1 BCL2L2 BCL2A1` (anti).

## 3. Diagnostic 1(b) - THE ARBITRARY SIGN SPLITS, NAMED NOW

**The brief is right that this check is worthless unless the splits are named
before the script runs.** They are named here, by gene, verbatim.

**The rule, so the lists are auditable rather than trusted:** sort the set's
genes alphabetically; the negative set is taken by position in that order. This
is arbitrary with respect to biology and it spreads the negative genes across
the set rather than taking a contiguous block, which an alphabetical *block*
would not do.

- **BALANCED**: every 2nd gene negative. Maximal cancellation.
- **RATIO-MATCHED (5:1)**: every 6th gene negative. **This one is PRIMARY**,
  because the configuration's own sign balance is 5 positive to 1 negative
  (mean weight **+0.667**), and a balanced split cancels far more than the
  configuration's construction ever could.

### Fe-S cluster assembly, n = 23

> **BALANCED, negative (11):** `BRIP1`, `CIAPIN1`, `FDX1`, `FXN`, `HSCB`,
> `ISCA2`, `LYRM4`, `NDOR1`, `NUBP1`, `POLD1`, `SLC25A28`
>
> **RATIO-MATCHED 5:1, negative (3):** `FDX1`, `ISCA2`, `NUBP1`
> - 20 positive : 3 negative, mean weight **+0.739**

### Mitophagy, n = 38

> **BALANCED, negative (19):** `ATG5`, `CSNK2A1`, `CSNK2B`, `MAP1LC3A`, `MFN1`,
> `MTERF3`, `PGAM5`, `PRKN`, `SQSTM1`, `TBK1`, `TOMM22`, `TOMM5`, `TOMM70`,
> `UBB`, `UBE2D2`, `UBE2L3`, `UBE2V1`, `VDAC1`, `VDAC3`
>
> **RATIO-MATCHED 5:1, negative (6):** `CSNK2B`, `MTERF3`, `SQSTM1`, `TOMM5`,
> `UBE2D2`, `VDAC1`
> - 32 positive : 6 negative, mean weight **+0.684**

**Both mean weights are close to the configuration's +0.667**, which is the
property that makes them a fair test of signing.

### 3.1 One addition beyond the brief, declared here

**The sign-permutation of the configuration's own six genes.** Six variants,
each making exactly one of `BBC3 BID BIK BAD BCL2L1 MCL1` negative and the other
five positive. This is **mine, not the brief's**, and it is added because it
answers the same question without any threshold at all:

- if **all six** variants collapse toward zero in normals, the collapse is a
  property of signing a six-gene composite 5:1, whatever the gene;
- if **only the MCL1 variant** collapses, something is specific to `MCL1`.

**It is a sharper instrument than 1(b) and it costs nothing**, since the genes
are already scored.

## 4. Diagnostic 2 - THE CONFIGURATION HAS NO CYTOSOLIC HALF

**The brief says "use E14's compartment assignment for the configuration genes".
That assignment does not split them.** Checked against the MitoCarta 3.0
snapshot, which is the rule E14 uses and nothing else:

| gene | in MitoCarta 3.0 |
|---|---|
| `BBC3`, `BID`, `BIK`, `BAD`, `BCL2L1`, `MCL1` | **all 6 TRUE** |

**All six configuration genes are mitochondrial. The cytosolic half is empty, so
the configuration cannot be decomposed by compartment and diagnostic 2 cannot be
run on it.** The same holds for E10's twelve: 12 of 12 are in MitoCarta.

**What is run instead, and it is closer to E14's actual claim.** E14's
compartment result is about its **`apoptotic machinery (44)`** set, which splits
**20 mitochondrial / 24 cytosolic** - reproduced exactly here from MitoCarta
membership. That is the object E14's claim is about, so that is the object
decomposed.

> **ONE GENE IS EXCLUDED, declared now: `CYCS`.** It is the single member of the
> 44 that is also in the `OXPHOS subunits` arm, so leaving it in would put a
> gene of the exposure inside the readout. The mitochondrial half is therefore
> **19 genes**, the cytosolic half 24. **E36's control sets needed no such
> exclusion** - both overlaps were zero - and this one is recorded because it is
> the only place in E36 or E38 where a gene is dropped.
>
> 5 of the 6 configuration genes sit inside the 44 (`BIK` does not), so the
> mitochondrial half is **not independent of E36's readout** and is never
> treated as a control.

**Fe-S assembly and mitophagy are decomposed the same way**, as the brief asks,
since E14's claim is a rank against them. Their halves are E36's, unchanged:
Fe-S 13/10, mitophagy 16/22, PINK1/PRKN 14/17.

**A limitation that cannot be fixed here.** The mitochondrial half is
mitochondrial, so part of any correlation with OXPHOS is shared mitochondrial
content. E14 built a compartment-matched null for exactly this; **E38 does not
rebuild it**, so the mitochondrial half is read only as a rank against the Fe-S
and mitophagy halves, never as an absolute.

## 6. Diagnostic 4 - the MYC transcript, and the 82

Four things, each cheap and each independent of everything above.

1. **`MYC`, `MYCN`, `MYCL` transcripts**, paired, over the 113 pairs: median
   within-patient difference, percentage of tumours higher, patient-level
   bootstrap interval. All three are present in the joint matrix. **No regulon,
   no gene set, no scoring choice.**
2. **The 82 stripped targets, printed in full.** `M_b__FULL` is 886 and
   `M_b__PROLIFSTRIP` is 804; the 82 removed are **all 82 in `PROLIF_REF`**
   (n = 327), which is the stripping reference recorded in
   `set_definitions.rds$strip_refs` - **not** `PROLIF_DISJOINT` (n = 318), and
   the difference is recorded so the number is traceable.
   **Their overlap with independent MYC target sets is reported**, against the
   `__FULL` variants only, because every `__PROLIFSTRIP` variant was stripped
   with the same reference and comparing to one would be circular.
3. **MYC amplification of the 113**, from `MYC_amp` in
   `data/from_validation/tcga_brca_covariates.rds`.
   **This is not snapshot-mixing.** `MYC_amp` is a per-patient GISTIC call. It
   is not derived from the expression normalisation, E37's declaration section
   4.2 kept the genomics untouched on purpose, and **E37 and E36 already carry
   `BUFFER_gistic` from the frozen object into the joint snapshot** by the same
   reasoning. No expression value crosses between snapshots.
4. **Subtype and site composition of the 113 against the 1,095**, recomputed in
   the script so the saved object is self-contained. It should reproduce data
   note 2 section 3 exactly (PAM50 Fisher p = 0.76; 6 of 40 sites, 87.6% from
   the top 3, Fisher p = 1e-05), and **a failure to reproduce would be a defect,
   not a finding.**

## 7. THE GATE - diagnostic 1 gates the reading of 2 and 3

Fixed here, before any value exists.

**Leg A** - does the unsigned configuration join the other unsigned composites?
> passes iff `rho(OXPHOS, unsigned configuration)` in normals is **>= 0.729**,
> the floor of the five unsigned companions E36 recorded (+0.729 to +0.902).
> **That floor is an already-committed E36 number**, not one derived here.

**Leg B** - do arbitrarily signed comparators collapse?
> passes iff **both** ratio-matched 5:1 signed comparators have
> `|rho|` in normals **< 0.30**, which is under half the unsigned floor, so a
> composite below it has lost most of the common component.

| | |
|---|---|
| **A and B both pass** | **CANCELLATION.** The signed near-zero is arithmetic. The normal comparison is closed, and **diagnostics 2 and 3 are reported as computed but NOT read as evidence.** |
| **either fails** | **NOT CANCELLATION ALONE.** Something is specific to the configuration, and diagnostics 2 and 3 are worth reading - still post-hoc, still unable to change E36's verdict. |

**The sign-permutation of section 3.1 is read alongside and needs no threshold.**

## 8. What E38 cannot do, written before it runs

1. **It cannot overturn E36's UNINTERPRETABLE verdict**, which was reached on a
   declared control and a declared reading rule and stands as recorded.
2. **It licenses no hypothesis test.** Every quantity is post-hoc and
   exploratory; two legs are second attempts after a first result.
3. **It does not make the tissues comparable.** The composition difference
   between normal breast and tumour is enormous whatever any adjustment does -
   adipose AUC 0.054, fibroblast 0.757 - and no diagnostic below changes that.
4. **It is one cohort, unreplicated.** SCAN-B has no normals.
5. **No outcome variable enters. No product term. No causal or killing
   language.**
